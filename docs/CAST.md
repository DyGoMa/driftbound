# Driftbound: Cast, Partners and Villains (v1, approved 2026-10-30)

Status: **approved by Dylan 2026-10-30** (cast locked; images not started). Every name, look and cost below is Claude's call (see the calls table in `DECISIONS.md`); change any of them.

Follows the World Bible hard rules: everyone is 21+ with adult faces and bodies; bold 2000s outfits, no nudity or sexualized posing; all designs original.

## Shared rules for the cast
- **Bond mark:** every bonded human has a glowing mark on the skin where the bond sits. Each mark is a unique simple shape in the partner's colour. It glows brighter when they borrow power. It is the one thing that must match on every image.
- **Surge:** borrowing your partner's power is a Surge. Small Surges are safe. Big or long ones cost the human something (one cost per lead, listed below). Each cost has a **base state** (how it looks on the reference sheet) and gets worse in the story.
- **The ship:** the crew sails a battered salvage ship, the **Gullwake**, towed by Sela's partner.
- **Elements:** Storm, Tide, Fire, Wind, Iron, Crystal. One per lead, so no two partners overlap.
- **Signature colours** (for UI, menus, VN name boxes): listed per character.

## The crew of the Gullwake (6 leads: 3 men, 3 women)

### 1. Kaito Brask — the hero
- **Age / build:** 21, man. 178 cm (5'10"), lean and wiry, runner's legs.
- **Face / hair:** sharp jaw, amber eyes, a scar cutting through his left eyebrow. Shaggy black hair with one streak bleached white (a childhood lightning strike).
- **Outfit:** black sleeveless zip top, half unzipped; short red flight jacket tied around his waist by the sleeves; grey cargo pants tucked into scuffed boots; brass flight goggles pushed up on his forehead; a fingerless metal gauntlet on his right forearm.
- **Bond mark:** a jagged three-point lightning fork on the back of his right hand.
- **Personality:** loud, reckless, starts fights he can't finish, never backs down. Wants to draw the first map of the Drift that stays right. Flustered and useless at flirting; the crew teases him for it.
- **Colour:** electric yellow + red.
- **Partner: Skarn**, a storm wolf the size of a horse. Slate-grey fur, a tall sail-like fin down its spine that crackles with lightning, glowing yellow eyes, and a forked tail. Glides on updrafts. Grumpy and loyal.
- **Borrowed power:** speed bursts and lightning arcing through his gauntlet.
- **Bond cost (scars):** every big Surge burns a branching lightning-shaped scar further up his right arm. **Base state:** scars from hand to elbow. Push too far and the arm goes numb for days.

### 2. Sela Vey — the captain
- **Age / build:** 28, woman. 175 cm (5'9"), tall, athletic, long legs.
- **Face / hair:** dark brown skin, sea-green eyes, gold hoop earrings. Long silver-blue hair in a high ponytail.
- **Outfit:** short cropped navigator's jacket (deep blue, brass buttons, high collar) over a black bandeau top, bare midriff; a wrap skirt with a high side slit; strapped sandal-boots; a brass spyglass on her hip.
- **Bond mark:** a whirlpool spiral (curls inward, no tail) just below her left collarbone. (Moved by Dylan, 2026-10-30, so the high collar doesn't hide it; shape changed 2026-10-31 because the spiral wave kept reading as the number 2; old lines in the archive at the end of this file.)
- **Personality:** calm, teasing, the crew's anchor. Big-sister flirt who calls Kaito "pup" and enjoys making him blush. Reads the Drift better than any chart.
- **Colour:** deep teal + gold.
- **Partner: Vesh**, a deep-sea serpent-dragon, long as the ship. Dark blue scales, a ridge of glowing lantern lures along its back, frilled jaw. Tows the Gullwake through tight gaps between islands.
- **Borrowed power:** water pressure blasts, and she can feel which way the islands are drifting.
- **Bond cost (memories):** every deep Surge drowns one of her memories. She keeps a worn logbook of her own life and rereads it. **Base state:** no body mark; the logbook is her signature prop.

### 3. Juno Calloway — the sharpshooter

- **Face / hair:** tanned skin with freckles, bright green eyes, a cocky grin. Copper-orange hair in one thick braid over her shoulder.

- **Personality:** loud gambler, bets on everything, constant innuendo, rival-banter with Kaito. Brave to the point of stupid.
- **Colour:** fire orange + white.
- **Partner: Ashka**, a fire raptor the size of a large eagle. Ember-red and black feathers that trail sparks, a hooked beak, glowing tail plumes. Rides on Juno's shoulder or flies spotter for her shots.
- **Borrowed power:** she throws Ashka's fire from her gloved hands. (Changed by Dylan, 2026-10-30, after her rifle was removed; old line in the archive at the end of this file.)
- **Bond cost (heat / pain):** each Surge raises her body heat; too much and she burns from the inside. **Base state:** scarred, blistered palms she hides under her gloves.

### 4. Silas Moreau — the duelist
- **Age / build:** 24, man. 183 cm (6'0"), slim, graceful.
- **Face / hair:** pale skin, violet eyes, a beauty mark under his left eye. Long dark-teal hair tied low at the neck.
- **Outfit:** white shirt unbuttoned to the stomach with a loose high collar, a wide red sash at the waist, loose black trousers, wrapped forearms and ankles, soft boots. Weapon: a chain-blade (a curved knife on a long chain).
- **Bond mark:** three short feather lines down the left side of his neck.
- **Personality:** smooth talker, the crew's charming flirt with everyone, ex-smuggler. Hides how much he cares behind jokes.
- **Colour:** violet + red.
- **Partner: Hush**, a silent wind owl-lynx: a big cat's body with owl wings, a flat owl face, and feathers sharp as knives. Moves without a sound.
- **Borrowed power:** wind cuts through his chain-blade and near-silent movement.
- **Bond cost (voice):** each Surge steals his breath. Too much and he can't speak for days, which is the worst thing that can happen to him. **Base state:** a faint rasp; no body mark.

### 5. Bram Torvald — the shipwright and cook
- **Age / build:** 31, man. 198 cm (6'6"), huge and broad.
- **Face / hair:** light-brown skin, kind brown eyes, a thick black beard, shaved head. Tribal-style tattoos (original shapes) across his shoulders.
- **Outfit:** shirtless under heavy leather work-dungarees with one strap down, a tool belt, thick boots, a cook's towel over his shoulder.
- **Bond mark:** a square anvil shape over his heart.
- **Personality:** gentle giant, crew dad, cooks huge meals, gets misty-eyed. Terrifying when the crew is threatened.
- **Colour:** iron grey + rust orange.
- **Partner: Grist**, an iron rhino-beetle beast the size of a truck. A shell of dark iron plates, one huge anvil-shaped horn, six thick legs. Slow, stubborn, sleeps on the deck.
- **Borrowed power:** his skin hardens to iron.
- **Bond cost (turning to stone):** the iron never fully goes away. Grey iron patches are spreading over his body and stiffening his joints. **Base state:** grey patches on his left shoulder and forearm.

### 6. Dr. Ione Raske — the bond scientist
- **Age / build:** 26, woman. 168 cm (5'6"), slim.
- **Face / hair:** pale skin, sharp grey eyes behind thin round glasses. Messy black bob with a single crystal-white lock.
- **Outfit:** a lab coat worn open and loose off her shoulders, a cropped black turtleneck, high-waisted shorts with a sample-vial belt, dark stockings and buckled boots.
- **Bond mark:** a cracked hexagon on her left wrist (broken, because her bond was half-stripped).
- **Personality:** deadpan, brilliant, deadpan innuendo delivered as science. Defected from the Empire's bond labs and feels guilty about what she helped build.
- **Colour:** ice blue + black.
- **Partner: Prism**, a glass mantis-moth the size of a large dog. Clear crystal wings that split light into rainbows, thin legs with blade-like forearms. Fragile-looking, deadly.
- **Borrowed power:** light beams and crystal shields, but only in short bursts.
- **Bond cost (crystal growths):** the half-stripped bond is unstable; every Surge grows painful crystal shards on her skin. **Base state:** small crystal growths on her left wrist and up the side of her neck.

## The villains: the Sever

The **Meridian Empire** wants to stop the Drift by pinning islands in place with giant anchor pylons. Its secret unit, **the Sever**, hunts bonded partners, cuts the bond, and rebuilds the monsters into weapons called **Hollows**.

- **Uniform:** long grey coats with a white collar and a black bar across the chest (an original symbol: a straight line cutting a circle). Troopers carry **bond-cutters**, hooked blades that glow white.
- **Hollows:** stripped monsters fitted with armour plates, cables and a glowing white core where the bond used to be. Blank eyes. They are the weapons the Sever sells to the Empire.

### Director Aurel Vasch — the leader
- **Age:** 46, man. Tall and thin, neat grey hair, a calm smile.
- **Look:** white version of the Sever coat, gloves, and a mechanical right arm with a glass chamber holding a stripped bond's glowing core.
- **Role:** the scientist who invented bond stripping. Ione's former mentor. Believes freeing humans from monsters is mercy.

### Captain Hekka Dorn — the enforcer
- **Age:** 33, woman. Very tall, muscular, buzzed white hair, a scar across her lips.
- **Look:** heavy grey armour with an open midriff, a bond-cutter lance taller than she is.
- **Partner:** a chained Hollow bear-beast that used to be her own partner. She stripped it herself to prove her loyalty.
- **Role:** the Sever's muscle; a rival for Sela.

### Silt — the hunter
- **Age:** 29, man. Wiry, always masked (a smooth white mask with one eye slit), long ragged black coat.
- **Look:** rides a Hollow hound-beast with cable "veins" and a white core in its chest.
- **Role:** tracks bonded humans across the Drift. Kaito's rival. Nobody knows his real name.

### Sever troopers
Faceless grunts in grey coats and visored helmets with bond-cutters. Used in games as the common enemy.
