## 1. Kaito Brask

### 1.1 Birthplace and family
Born on **Pinhold** [new], a cliff island on the Empire's trade edge. Pinhold has no beach: a tilted slab, high north cliffs, a harbour cut into the low south side, stone houses papered with charts that are always a little wrong [new].

- **Oren Brask** (54 now), father. Born in Meridian, trained in the Empire's survey office, posted to Pinhold at 31 to run the island's drift survey [new]. Kaito has his jaw, his temper (Vasch, Issue 37) and his dream. Kaito has not seen him since he was 6.
- **Maren Brask**, born **Maren Tull** [new], mother. Pinhold's well-keeper, as her family had been for six generations [new]: the Tulls measured and logged every well on the island, because on a drifting island the water table rises and falls and someone has to say which wells are safe. Died when Kaito was 17 (see 1.2). Would be 49.
- No siblings, no living grandparents. The only inheritance: the **Tull well-ledgers** [new], two hundred years of water levels on a shelf in the house on Tull Lane, which Kaito has never read because he hates columns of numbers. (Plant: Oren read them. See 1.8.)

### 1.2 The years before we meet him (ages at each event)
- **0.** Born on Pinhold. Oren is 33, Maren 28 [new].
- **6.** The cliff night (Issue 3, the only childhood flashback besides Issue 37). Oren takes the boy up the north point in the dark with a chart and a lantern, to "show him how the island breathes" [new]. The storm comes in faster than the chart says. Lightning strikes the point; it burns the lock of hair white and leaves the boy deaf for a day [new]. He wakes in his own bed. By morning Oren is gone. Maren tells him the survey boat went down in the Gap; a smashed survey skiff is found a week later with the Brask name on the transom, and the town calls it a drowning [new]. Kaito believes this until Issue 37.
- **7 to 13.** Raised by Maren alone. He runs: rooftops, cliff paths, the harbour wall, because a boy who runs is not at home being asked how he is. Courier for the **Pinhold Chart-house** from 12, carrying corrected charts door to door, seeing weekly how fast a map goes wrong [new].
- **14.** Draws his own chart of Pinhold's drift from a month of watching the current lines off the point. It stays right for nine days. Maren pins it to the kitchen wall and never takes it down [new]. It is still there in Issue 1, next to the "correct" Empire chart, and is the only chart in the house that is now wrong.
- **17.** Maren dies of lung fever the winter the new customs post holds every crate of medicine a month for "inspection" [new]. Kaito steals the crate off the customs pier; too late by one day. He keeps the broken customs seal in his jacket pocket (spoken of only, never drawn, as with Juno's story in Issue 10). This is why he runs medicine and will not take Empire coin for it: eggs, bread, favours [new].
- **17 to 21.** Alone on Tull Lane. "The Brask runner" to customs; the well-keeper's loud boy to the town. Eyebrow scar from a customs boathook on a rooftop at 18 [new].
- **19.** The bond with Skarn (1.3).
- **20.** First big Surge (1.3); scars reach the wrist. By 21 they reach the elbow. Customs posts a bounty on him the month the Great Pylon's footings arrive [new].
- **21.** Issue 1.

### 1.3 How the bond happened and what it first cost
Storm wolves come down on Pinhold's north point every autumn to ride the updrafts in the lightning season; the town calls the point **the Strikes**, and nobody goes up in weather except Kaito, who has gone up in every storm since he was 6, to the exact spot, for reasons he does not say [new].

At 19 he finds a half-grown storm wolf grounded there, a kite-wire torn through its fin, soaked and furious. Three fishers want it killed before it takes a goat. Kaito sits down ten feet from it in the rain and shouts about maps for two hours, because he has no idea what else to do [new]. When he puts his right hand in its ruff to cut the wire, the sky does to both what it did to him at 6: one bolt through the fin, the wolf and his hand. The fork burns onto the back of his hand. He names it Skarn, after the sound it made hitting him [new].

**What it first cost.** His hand is dead for a week; he cannot hold a pen or a cup. The town now has a storm wolf on its cliff and a Brask who chose it over the goats; two families stop speaking to him, and the door that shuts in Issue 5 has been closing since this night [new]. The first scar comes at 20, Surging to outrun a customs searchlight: the fork climbs knuckles to wrist; he laughs, then is sick behind the chart-house [new]. He has never admitted he knew the bond had a price before he took it. He knew. He takes what is his and pays later.

### 1.4 Three defining memories
1. **The lantern on the point (6, Issue 3).** His father's hand holding the chart flat against the wind, saying one day there would be a map of the Drift that stays right. Then white. Then his mother's face, and no father. The dream he shouts in Issue 1 is a sentence he was given, not one he made [new].
2. **Nine days right (14).** Maren with a nail in her teeth, pinning his chart to the wall. "Nine days. Your father never managed nine." The only time she spoke of Oren with pride [new]. Spoken of only, never drawn.
3. **Two hours of shouting (19).** Rain, the wolf's yellow eyes, his own voice going hoarse explaining current lines to an animal, and the moment it stopped snarling and tilted its head, as if it had been waiting for someone to say it properly [new].

### 1.5 What he wants and what he fears
**Wants:** the map that stays right, said out loud to anyone, because saying it is how he keeps his father alive without admitting that is what he is doing. Under that: to be let back in. To Pinhold, to a kitchen, to anyone who will shut the door *behind* him for once instead of in front.

**Fears:** that the dream is stupid and everyone laughing is right. That the arm dies before the chart is done. That the people he saves still bar the door (Issue 5; worse in Issue 43). After Issue 37: that the dream was never his and was always a weapon. He does not fear Silt. He should.

### 1.6 How he talks (ten lines in voice)
Short, loud, sweary, declarative, dumb jokes under pressure, ears red at any flirting. He names things wrong on purpose when he is scared.
1. "Screw the chart. The chart's wrong. I'm the chart."
2. "I'm not reckless, I'm *early*. Everyone else is just late to the fight."
3. "Eggs. I take eggs. The Empire can keep its money and choke on it."
4. "Stop calling me pup. ...No, don't stop. Wait, yes, stop. SELA."
5. "It's a scar, Ione. You don't have to say 'tissue necrosis' in that voice."
6. "Three hits. Jump, Surge, core. If I say it enough times it's a plan."
7. "I've been hit by lightning twice. Technically I'm the expert here."
8. "He's a dog on a dog. I'm not scared of a dog on a dog."
9. (Juno winks at him.) "What. WHAT. Why is my face hot. Is the galley on fire."
10. (Issue 48, to the chart.) "The islands move. Fine. Let's go learn the tune."

### 1.7 With the crew, one line each
- **Sela:** the first adult who ever gave him an order he actually followed; he would die before telling her that, and she already wrote it down.
- **Juno:** a rivalry that is a bet that is a slow burn; she takes his boots, his goggles and eventually the whole pool (Issue 40), and he never once sees it coming.
- **Silas:** hates him on sight (Issue 15), says "I KNEW it" (Issue 17), is wrong, hands him back the slate (Issue 18); they insult each other as a way of saying thank you ever after.
- **Bram:** the father-shaped person Kaito pretends not to need; the "vegetable" rule exists because of him.
- **Ione:** he is the loudest voice for shooting her in Issue 33 and the first to forgive her, because he knows what it is to take a thing knowing the price.
- **Otti:** the only teacher he has had since the cliff; "beautiful moron" is the kindest thing anyone calls him and it still makes him walk into a mast.
- **Tobias:** the rival he is allowed to enjoy.
- **Silt:** his dark mirror: a bonded man who paid the whole price on purpose and now asks Kaito, every time, why he runs.

### 1.8 Secrets the reader learns later (plant exactly; add nothing past these)
- **He cannot swim well.** Pinhold has no beach. Revealed when he falls overboard in Issue 11; Juno gives odds, Vesh fishes him out [new].
- **The seal in the pocket, and his mother.** Spoken once, flat, to Juno after her own father story, Issue 10 (if that writer has room; otherwise Issue 14, on the night everyone tells Sela stories she will write down) [new]. Never drawn.
- **His chart notation is his father's.** The small tick Kaito puts on a current line to mark its "beat" is the mark on the lantern chart. The reader sees Oren's hand make the same tick in the Issue 37 flashback [new]. Otti's Short Surge "on the beat" (Issue 22) is the first time anyone tells him the beat is real.
- **Oren found the pulse in the Tull well-ledgers** [new]: two hundred years of water levels on one island rising and falling to the Undersong. The survey the Empire built the pylons from began in Kaito's mother's kitchen. Revealed Issue 37 (Oren's signature and the flashback), made plain in Issue 47 when Oren, at the chart table, is using the same columns.
- **What Oren was running from (Issue 37; proposed for that writer, inside the outline):** the survey office summoned him with the sheets; he understood the Empire meant to stop the Drift and his survey told them where; he went up the point that night to bury the master sheets under the cairn; the storm, the strike, the boy. At dawn an Empire cutter was in the harbour. He went with it so it would leave without searching the house. Maren knew, said the sea took him, and died without saying otherwise [new]. The wrecked skiff was the cutter's doing.
- **His mark answers the Undersong.** When Mother Kell hums (Issue 34) Skarn lies down and the fork on Kaito's hand flickers once; nobody but Ione notices [new]. Pays off Issue 48, when the mark flickers at Skarn's bite and the hum comes up through the hull.

### 1.10 Fighting style
A runner's fight. He never stands still and never blocks: over, through or past, best hits off a wall, a mast or Skarn's back. Right-hand gauntlet punches with lightning down the forearm; left hand grabs and shoves. Speed bursts to close a gap or leave one. He starts fights he cannot finish and wins by getting up one more time than expected (Silt: "You're the only one who keeps getting up"). Weak against patience: Silt beats him twice by waiting. From Issue 22 Otti's **Short Surge**, small bursts on the beat, beats Tobias (Issue 24) and carries him through Part Two: left-handed, one beat at a time, the rig for leverage.

## 2. Skarn

**Species.** Storm wolf [new on all detail below]: a cliff-and-sky animal of the lightning islands, horse-sized grown, with a sail fin that stores charge and rides updrafts for hours. They live about forty years and roost alone; a bond is rare. Skarn is about five, full-grown since last winter; three and half-grown when found.

**Where found.** The Strikes, Pinhold's north point, the cliff where the boy was struck at 6; Issue 1's last splash is Silt looking down at that roost. The torn fin healed with a notch a hand wide near the tail; it sparks blue at the notch before weather [new].

**Temperament.** Grumpy, loyal, judges everyone. One face for Kaito's ideas, which the crew has named: the disgusted face (running gag). He is a he. He is not a dog.

**Three habits.**
1. **Weather glass.** He sleeps in the Gullwake's crosstrees, the highest point aboard, and his fin crackles about an hour before a squall; Sela writes "Skarn says rain" in the logbook and is right [new].
2. **The lick and the bite.** After any big Surge he licks the new scar once, up the arm, then bites Kaito's ear, hard enough to mean it [new].
3. **Warm iron.** When Grist sleeps on deck Skarn lies against his shell, because the iron holds the sun; Grist has never once acknowledged him [new].

**How they fight.** From above. Skarn glides over the fight, drops onto whoever is behind Kaito, and is airborne again before anyone turns. The fin discharges along its edge; the forked tail cracks like a whip. He grounds lightning *into* Kaito's gauntlet, which is why the hits land hot. He will not fight below decks and hates the Dive (Issue 13: he howls throughout). Against Silt's hound he goes for the neck cables. Fast, not clever; patient enemies bait him, the pair's shared weakness.

**How they show affection.** Skarn leans his full weight into Kaito's side until Kaito falls over, every morning, since the cliff [new]. Kaito scratches under the ruff where the kite-wire scar is and talks to him about maps in a normal voice, the only normal voice he has. Neither would call it affection. Issue 42: Skarn takes the bond-cutter and Kaito runs lightning through a dead arm to stop the count. Issue 48: half-cut, Skarn neither attacks nor stops, and Kaito walks up and takes the bite.

**Skarn with the others, one line each.** Vesh: will not sleep below her waterline, respects her, never says so. Ashka: roof arguments nightly; the pair of them are the Issue 40 cutaway. Hush: cannot hear her coming and cannot forgive it. Grist: warm iron (above). Prism: snaps at the rainbows, misses, is embarrassed. Silt's hound: the only thing he has ever hated.

**Skarn's never list.** Never in a flirting panel or a Pal panel in any way but as the disgusted cut-to. Never drawn cute or small; he is a predator the size of a horse. Never speaks, thinks in captions, or "understands" dialogue; he reads tone and acts. Never leaves the cliff side of Kaito in a fight.
