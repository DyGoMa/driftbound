# Driftbound backstory dossier: allies and rivals (bible section 4)

v1, 2026-11-06, for every medium. `CAST.md` and `SERIES_BIBLE.md` section 4 win on any clash; where silent, this file invents. Hard rules apply. "Reveal:" marks the issue a secret surfaces in. Anchors: Kaito 21; Regatta every seven years; first pinning nine years ago.

## Ottoline "Otti" Ferro (52)

**Birthplace, family.** Lowmark Shoals, a kelp-cutting island of long sea walls. Only child of two kelp-cutters, both dead of old age. Never married.

**Before.** Bonded at 19 on the sea wall to **Merrow**, a gale-skate (wide grey wing-finned glider, Wind). Raced at 24 (fourth), won at 31, 38 and 45. Her cost was cold: each Surge took body heat, slower back every year; the flask began as a cure. At 49 the Sever took Lowmark's partners. She stood between a cutter and Merrow with a boathook, lost, and counted the ten aloud so his last voice was hers. She kept the harness.

**Bond, cost.** Issue 21: she sees Kaito burn himself in one long Surge. Teaching him costs a sober week (Issue 22), then her life (Issue 41).

**Memories.** Merrow landing on the wall and refusing to leave. Carrying a drunk rival over the line at 45 so he wasn't last. Counting to ten.

**Wants, fears.** A student who outlives her. A warm death in bed; pawning the harness.

**Voice.** "You beautiful moron." "Short bursts, pup. Like kissing. Nobody enjoys the long ones." "I was flying before your mother picked a surname." "Sit down, giant, I only want to look." "Drink, swim, regret." "That boy's hurting himself on purpose." "A Surge is a drumbeat, not a scream." "I lost once. Didn't like it. Stopped." "You're out of booze. I'm fixing that." "Merrow liked idiots. He'd have liked you."

**Crew.** Kaito: last student, flirted into listening. Sela: equals who never explain. Juno: books Otti's bets; Otti rigs them. Silas: out-flirted, delighted. Bram: propositioned weekly, feeds her. Ione: asked about the wrist kindly.

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 40: the third win wasn't clean; Merrow was failing and she Surged him past it, and she believes that's why he couldn't fight the cutter. Reveal, Part Two (post-48): her will leaves flask and harness "to the moron."

**Wardrobe.** Sleeveless racing coat, flask on a strap, harness over one shoulder. Regatta: one-piece racing suit under the coat. Formal: a man's dinner jacket.

**Fighting.** No Surge. Boathook as spear, stolen flag as distraction, dirty footwork. Nothing of hers can be cut.

**Never.** Says "sorry." Flirts with anyone not laughing. Lets anyone carry the harness. Gives up on a student. Cries in view.

## Tamsin "Old Tam" Reed (67)

**Birthplace, family.** Born in Keelbreak's yard, a caulker's daughter. Wife **Hesper**, sailmaker, died of fever when Tam was 55. Never bonded: "a hammer never asks for anything back."

**Before.** First keel alone at 22; master of the dry dock at 30. At 58 a grown, broke, grieving giant of 22 was drinking in her yard; she handed him a hammer.

**Bond, cost.** Bram is the bond. It costs the yard's quiet and, Issue 32, her life, between Hekka's bear and him.

**Memories.** The first keel. Hesper sewing a sail by lamplight, swearing better than Tam. Bram taking the hammer, silent an hour, then fixing a plank.

**Wants, fears.** The Gullwake afloat another year out of spite. Bram going to iron where she can't reach.

**Voice.** "That's not a hull, that's a rumour." "Sit DOWN, you great tin kettle." "Who caulked this? A fish?" "You're limping. Don't lie to a shipwright." "Hesper liked idiots too." "Lighter work. No arguing." "I built better boats drunk." "Feed me before you feed the island." "Get off my dock, Empire." "Bollocks." (Issue 32, her last word.)

**Crew.** Kaito: "the loud one," holds planks. Sela: two captains, one ship. Juno: Tam bets against her, loses. Silas: "the shirt." Bram: her son in all but paper. Ione: never meet.

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 32, after her death: the yard's memorial post already carries Bram's name in her hand, her own carved above it years earlier.

**Wardrobe.** Leather apron, rolled sleeves, hammer loop. No fan service.

**Fighting.** A shipwright's maul, two hands, one swing. Stops fights rather than winning them.

**Never.** Praises Bram to his face. Cries. Calls the ship a kind word. Turns anyone from the yard. Ends a sentence politely.

## Tobias Quill (27) and Flint

**Birthplace, family.** Gullsand Bar, a sandbar of kite-makers. Parents alive, in debt to a Meridian chandler.

**Before.** Bonded Flint at 20 from a storm-nest egg; raced his first Regatta that year, came last, and Otti bought him a drink. His cost is teeth: every big Surge cracks one; the gold tooth replaced the first. Skiff: the **Brag**.

**Bond, cost.** Issue 24: Kaito wins by a hair; they punch each other and drink. Loyalty costs his purse (he'd bet against himself to clear his parents' debt) and, Issue 26, his friend.

**Memories.** Flint hatching in his hands. Last place at 20, the crowd laughing. Issue 26: bringing down a Hollow made from his friend **Dessa Marr**'s (31) partner while she watches.

**Wants, fears.** To be remembered as better than the man who beat him. Being forgettable.

**Voice.** "Second place is a long word for losing." "Flint likes nobody. It's why I love him." "Race me or marry me, Brask, I've a schedule." "Gold tooth? Earned it." "Your wolf's got a face like my tax bill." "I bet against myself. Don't look at me like that." "That was HERS." "The Brag's faster than your whole ship." "Fine. Loyal. Shut up about it." "Sand gets everywhere. Ask anyone."

**Crew.** Kaito: rival, then brother. Sela: "ma'am," terrified. Juno: endless bets, no winner. Silas: blushes, denies it. Bram: Issue 40 cookout, one dish learned. Ione: offers to see the teeth; refused.

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 24: the self-bet. Reveal, Issue 39: he has paid Dessa's passage since 26.

**Wardrobe.** Ribbon sash over a bare chest racing, open shirt in port. Formal: ribbons as a cravat.

**Fighting.** Flint's sand as wall, blind or blast; a kite-line whip. To win, not hurt, until Issue 26.

**Never.** Races clean if dirty is funnier. Mocks a loss that cost a partner. Admits fear. Lets a stranger touch Flint. Says Dessa's name lightly.

### Flint
Sand kite-ray, huge: flat diamond body, sawtooth tail, grit-coloured, flies on gusts and swims in sand. Found as an egg on Gullsand Bar. Haughty; sulks for days. Habits: buries itself to the eyes; rattles its tail when Tobias lies; steals ribbons. Fights with sandstorms, tail sweeps, dives. Affection: lands on all of Tobias and won't get off.

## Captain Marisol Thane (35) and Gnash

**Birthplace, family.** Corrow Dock, a ferry island; a ferryman's daughter. Brother **Oso Thane** (33), silent, enormous, her crew.

**Before.** Deckhand at 18, first mate of the **Red Tally** at 25, captain at 28. Red tally marks on the mast are debts owed the ship; her forearm tattoos are ships taken. Gnash came from a reef-net at 24. Her cost is salt-lung: a big Surge and she coughs seawater for a day. The gold patch covers an eye lost at 30 to a snapped harpoon line.

**Bond, cost.** Rivals since Sela was 22, racing for the same wrecks; flirting since the second. Allying (Issue 27) costs the Red Tally its neutrality and half its crew.

**Memories.** The first tally mark. Sela beating her to a wreck and leaving a bottle: "You're slow. Drink faster." The dark, Gnash's teeth in her coat.

**Wants, fears.** The last word with Sela. Sela forgetting her, which she might.

**Voice.** "VEY! You owe me a bottle and a decade." "That's a tally mark, sweetheart. Well. Both." "Gnash, don't eat the duelist. He'd enjoy it." "I lost an eye, not my aim." "Red Tally pays its debts. Loudly." "Your boy walked into my mast. I'm keeping the dent." "Two captains, one bottle, no witnesses." "We don't do mornings." "Oso, hit something." "Write this one down, Vey. I want in that book."

**Crew.** Kaito: "the dent." Sela: history, heat, a bottle each meeting. Juno: kindred gamblers. Silas: loses to her on purpose (Issue 23); she buys drinks. Bram: she tries to steal him. Ione: the first straight answer about the salt-lung.

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 39: she saw Sela at 19, before they met, with a first mate on deck, "pretty boy, sang under his breath, never got his name."

**Wardrobe.** Red coat over a corset-style vest, forearms bare. Regatta: bikini top and open shirt (Issue 20). Formal: the coat buttoned.

**Fighting.** Cutlass and shoulder-charge, water pressure through the blade, Gnash under the hull. Boards first, always.

**Never.** Flirts with anyone she wouldn't fight beside. Leaves a debt unmarked. Mentions the eye first. Lies to Sela, since Sela can't check. Goes below in a storm.

### Gnash
Coral hammerhead, boat-length, rose-and-bone coral plates along head and spine, teeth like broken reef. Found in a reef-net as a pup. Cheerful menace. Habits: bumps the hull hello; eats fenders; sleeps belly-up under the stern. Fights by ramming, dragging boarders under, shedding coral splinters. Affection: rolls over for a coral scratch, and grins.

## Wren (25) and Dove Aster (27), and Pell

**Birthplace, family.** Saltlight atoll; daughters of the Regatta's buoy-riggers, still rigging. Issue 26 burns their home.

**Before.** At Dove 23 and Wren 21 a two-headed heron hatched from one egg on the rig and bonded both, one head each. Their cost is sight: after a big Surge the Surger goes grey-blind for an hour and the other calls her moves. "Dove calls, Wren flies," and the reverse.

**Bond, cost.** Issues 21 to 23, losing to Juno with style. Issue 26 costs their parents' rig and Pell a burned crest.

**Memories.** The egg cracking twice. The first blind catch mid-air. The buoys burning.

**Wants, fears.** To be the act everyone remembers. One blind, nobody calling.

**Voice.** Wren: "We're not a double act." Dove: "Triple. Pell counts twice." Wren: "Left head likes you." Dove: "Right head bit a trooper." Wren: "Grey-blind. Call it." Dove: "Three steps, drop, roll, you look ridiculous." Wren: "She's afraid of heights." Dove: "I am NOT." Dove: "Our parents rigged that course." Wren: "We'll rig the next."

**Crew.** Kaito: taught to fall. Sela: adored, walk copied. Juno: cheated them, repaid double. Silas: shirt rated daily. Bram: makes them eat. Ione: wants to study Pell; "ask Pell."

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 23: Dove fears heights; Wren has covered for years. Reveal, Issue 39: they refused an Empire exhibition contract at 24 and 26 and never said the sum.

**Wardrobe.** Matching striped leotard-style race suits with short jackets; festival wraps (Issue 20). Formal: one white suit, one black, swapped at midnight.

**Fighting.** Acrobatics on and off Pell, rope and weighted line; Pell's wind-beat flattens people. Comic until it isn't.

**Never.** Flirt with each other, or get joked about that way, by anyone, ever. Perform apart. Let the heads be called "good" and "bad." Admit which head is whose.

### Pell
Two-headed heron, man-tall, grey-white, one neck, two heads that disagree. Hatched on the Saltlight rig. Left head curious, right head furious. Habits: the heads groom then fight; stands one-legged on the rail in storms; raids Grist's dish. Fights with wind-beats and two beaks, a sister each side. Affection: both heads on both sisters' shoulders at once.

## Mother Kell (71)

**Birthplace, family.** Cantor Isle, youngest of a singing family. Great-aunt of **Corin Vale**; she taught him the Undersong, and at 20 he left to crew for a silver-haired girl of 19 with a battered ship.

**Before.** Bonded at 19 to **Hollin**, a bell-whale that sang back. Cantors steered by the song and each paid differently; Kell paid in sight, blind by 44. Hollin died of age when she was 60. Six years ago the Sever took the isle's partners and the Cantors scattered; she stayed.

**Bond, cost.** Issue 34: she hums, Skarn lies down, Sela cries. Helping costs her solitude; she will not say Corin's name.

**Memories.** Hollin answering her first note. Her eyes greying mid-song, and not stopping. A letter from Corin she could not read and asked no one to.

**Wants, fears.** The song outliving her. Being the last who knows it.

**Voice.** "Sit. You smell of lightning." "The sea has a heartbeat. You've been drawing its skin." "You poor girl, you paid it all." "Hum it back. Wrong. Again." "I'm blind, not deaf, and that giant's shirtless." "A song is what the map is for." "Nobody's home but me, and I'm not sure of me." "Ask no names. Names are what we pay." "Your wolf lies down because he remembers. You don't." "Go. The Sleeper's turning in its bed."

**Crew.** Kaito: hears the dream in his footsteps. Sela: the girl she grieves. Juno: wins a bet by hearing dice. Silas: pats his throat, "paid like a singer." Bram: hears his joints, says nothing. Ione: "the one who stopped at five," unprompted.

**Secrets.** Reveal, Part Two (post-48): the unread letter.

**Wardrobe.** Layered sea-grey shawls. Issue 34 bath gag: she makes the remarks.

**Fighting.** None. She hums and wild monsters lie down.

**Never.** Says Corin's name to Sela. Explains the song plainly. Asks for help. Leaves the isle in Part One. Lies; so she stops talking.

## Nim Hadda (30)

**Birthplace, family.** Ashmarket, a dice-cutter's son; father alive, disappointed.

**Before.** Stoked furnaces from 18 to 24; a vent blowout took his left arm. Bookmaker since. Met Juno when she was 19 (he 26): two years of a rigged dice table to pay her marker faster. She got out; he stayed. Never bonded: "I've seen the odds."

**Bond, cost.** Issue 7: explains the rigged city. Issue 10: forgives the house's share of her debt, costing his cut and his standing at the Cinder Palace.

**Memories.** Counting fingers he no longer had. Juno dealing with blistered hands, smiling. Keeping her chair the night she left.

**Wants, fears.** To be the one honest book in a rigged town. Needing to be brave.

**Voice.** "Evens you cheat, sweetheart. I'd be hurt otherwise." "I'm not the house. I just stand near it." "I don't bet on love. Bad liquidity." "Krell buys markers. I don't sell mine." "One arm, two eyes, three to one." "Kaito and who? Book's open." "Odds on the world? Nobody could pay." "Debt's cleared. Don't thank me, it ruins my margins." "Cynics live longer. Sadder, but longer." "Go on. Stake it all. You always do."

**Crew.** Kaito: "long odds, worth it." Sela: reads his tells; he enjoys losing. Juno: the one he went broke for. Silas: "unquantifiable," unbooked. Bram: bets on the stew. Ione: asks about the arm; gets odds on prosthetics.

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 39: Krell offered three times for Juno's marker; the third refusal cost Nim his licence. He never told her.

**Wardrobe.** Waistcoat, pinned left sleeve, pencil behind the ear. Formal: the same, cleaner.

**Fighting.** None; he knows who owes whom.

**Never.** Sells a marker. Books a crewmate's death. Says Juno's father's name. Pities himself in her hearing. Rounds odds his way.

## Commander Yevka Sorn (38)

**Birthplace, family.** Meridian, the capital. Mother a harbourmaster, father a records clerk; both proud, neither asks.

**Before.** Navy at 21, lieutenant at 26, commander of the escort fleet at 35. Never bonded; Navy rule forbids it above lieutenant. Her fleet escorted the Pinhold pylon barge (Issue 1, offscreen).

**Bond, cost.** Issue 39: an enemy on the horizon. Issue 40: a Hollow made from a pylon worker, and the forbidden question. Defecting (by Issue 45) costs rank, name and her family's safety.

**Memories.** Her mother's harbour, every ship logged. Filing Standing Order 12 unread. The worker's face above a core.

**Wants, fears.** The Empire she was promised. That there never was one, and she knew.

**Voice.** "Commander Sorn. State your cargo." "I escort pylons. I do not ask what they pin." "That was a civilian." "I followed every order. That is the problem." "Stand down or be stood down." "Vey. Your reputation is accurate." "I don't defect. I correct course." "The Director doesn't lie. I checked. That's worse." "The braid stays. The uniform goes." "Hollow is the right word. For all of us."

**Crew.** Kaito: escorted the pylon that killed Pinhold; tells him, Issue 45. Sela: two commanders, grudging then real. Juno: bets she'll turn; Yevka hates being odds. Silas: once arrested him; he reminds her. Bram: soup set before her (Issue 45); she eats. Ione: "welcome to the guilt, it has footnotes."

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 45: the Pinhold escort.

**Wardrobe.** Stiff Navy uniform, neat braid. Part Two: plain civilian coat, braid kept, no insignia.

**Fighting.** Straight sabre, fleet gunnery, signals. By the book until Issue 40, then better.

**Never.** Swears. Cuts the braid. Fires on a civilian hull. Says "mercy." Asks forgiveness; she pays instead.

## Oren Brask (54)

**Birthplace, family.** Meridian; Imperial Survey Office. Posted to Pinhold at 31; married a Pinhold woman (her name and fate are the crew dossier's); Kaito born when Oren was 33. Never bonded, which is why Vasch trusted him.

**Before.** Surveyed the Drift from 31 to 39 and found the pulse. Took the boy (6) to the cliff in a storm with the chart; that night's lightning gave Kaito the streak. Gone by morning. What he ran from (Issue 37): Vasch, then 31, had sent men for the survey. Oren went with them so the family wouldn't be taken, told the boy would be left alone while he worked. Fifteen years under guard in Meridian, siting pylons on his own chart.

**Bond, cost.** Issue 37, his signature; Issue 47, two men through glass. It costs Kaito the dream's innocence and Oren the lie he lived on.

**Memories.** The first pulse on his instruments, a heartbeat under the sea. The boy asleep on the chart. The day his sites drew a line through a circle.

**Wants, fears.** One honest chart. That his son inherited the dream and its bill.

**Voice.** "Measure twice. The sea moves between." "It isn't geology. It never was." "I plotted the sites. I didn't choose them. That distinction kept me alive, nothing else." "He has my temper. Vasch tells me things to watch my face." "A map that stays right. I said that once. To a boy." "Doctor Raske. You footnote too much." "I'm not dead. I'm filed." "Don't shout. The glass carries." "He kept the streak." "Draw it as a song, son. I never could."

**Crew.** Kaito: a father who is a signature. Sela: he reads a navigator in her hand. Juno: wants to shoot the glass; "not yet." Silas: knows his secret routes. Bram: unmet. Ione: met in Meridian when she was Vasch's student at 23; she never knew whose father (reveal, Issue 47).

**Secrets.** Reveal, Issue 37: alive, the survey his. Reveal, Issue 47: his chart is already half a stave; he has tried for years to write the song and cannot hear it.

**Wardrobe.** Prisoner's grey coat, chart-weights for cufflinks, glasses on a cord. No fan service, ever.

**Fighting.** None. Throws a chart weight once, Part Two, and misses.

**Never.** Curses Vasch; he describes him. Asks about his wife. Signs a chart he hasn't measured. Calls Kaito by name in Issue 47; can't yet. Stops drawing.
