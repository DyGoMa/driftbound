# The Ten Count: backstory dossier (v1, 2026-11-06)

Companion to `docs/manga/SERIES_BIBLE.md` section 4. Looks and ages in `CAST.md` and `shared/kit/cast.js` are kept exactly; all else is Claude's invention (see newFacts). Hard rules: everyone 21+; childhood memories plain and sad, no jokes on the page; no fan service near Sorrel, Pike, anyone frightened, a Pal or a childhood memory. "Later batch": Part Two past Issue 48, a suggestion only. Invented ground: Vasch's unit has cut bonds for about ten years; count-names came with the Great Pylon plan (Issue 25).

---

## Two: Deacon Sorrel (44, woman)

**Birthplace, family.** Lowmass Skerry, a chapel island of the Stillwater creed (Meridian's faith: stillness is grace, drift is sin; its clergy are Deacons). Mother Agathe kept the chapel; father Tomas set ordinary anchor pylons.

**Before.** 19 seminary; 23 ordained. 24: a flood fills the cistern, a slate tortoise climbs out, she warms it all night; bonded by morning. Power: stillness (slows a panicked heart, flattens water). Cost: each Surge takes heat from her hands. 38: Vasch preaches at Lowmass; she agrees to the cut; her last Surge holds Even still for the blade, and her hands never warm again. 39 chaplain; 41 Two.

**Memories.** Nine, plain and sad: her father's pylon finishing, the well going grey. Twenty-four: the tortoise's head in her palm. Thirty-eight: "ten," nothing where Even had been; she called it peace.

**Wants / fears.** Nobody pays a bond's price again. Fears the stillness was emptiness. Fears fire.

**Voice.** Soft, scripture-plain, "child" for everyone, counts under her breath.
1. "Be still, child. It is only ten."
2. "I don't ask you to agree. I ask you to breathe."
3. "I have met cruel men. They enjoyed it less."
4. "Your hands are warm. Hold mine. I forget."
5. "One. Two. You may close your eyes. Three."
6. "Grief is love with nowhere to go. We give it somewhere."
7. "I do not say the animal's name. I know it."
8. "You are frightened. Good. You are here."
9. "Mercy is not gentle, child. It is thorough."
10. "I stay until it stops. That is all I am for."

**Crew.** Kaito: a boy screaming at the sea; she'd hold his hand. Sela: envies the forgetting. Juno: fears her fire, blesses her anyway. Silas: makes her laugh once (Issue 26); she hates it. Bram: the bowl (Issue 45). Ione: "Doctor."

**Secrets.** She asked that Even never become a Hollow; Pike did it anyway (Issue 35, his notes: "slate tortoise, chapel, ambulatory"; she learns later batch). Issue 45: she eats the soup.

**Wardrobe (look invented).** 170 cm, soft round face, brown eyes, cropped ash-brown hair under a grey veil-cap, hands faintly blue. Cassock-length Sever coat, white collar, bar, a cord of ten beads; rain cape; a grey wrap at the Regatta. No fan service, ever.

**Fighting style.** None. Walks into the line of fire talking; grabbed, goes limp and keeps talking.

**Never.** Raises her voice. Lies. Touches a cutter. Hurries a death. Says "Hollow" (says "the animal").

**Partner: Even (gone).** Cartwheel-sized slate tortoise from the cistern. Patient; hated being moved. Habits: head into her palm, climbed the font, clicked at the bell. Fought by bracing until the ground went still; affection was the same head in the palm. Now Pike's Hollow.

## Three: Warden Krell (41, man)

**Birthplace, family.** Wallmarch, garrison town on Meridian's home island. Father Aldous, wall sergeant; younger brother Piet.

**Before.** 18 enlists; 21 first shield wall at a grain riot. 29: Piet (24), bonded to a moor-bull, holds a flood gate too long; the bond's cost was his heart. 33: joins Vasch, builds the shield line (shields faced with unburnable pylon kiln-glaze). 36 Warden of Ashmarket. 41 Issue 6.

**Memories.** Twenty-one: twelve shields locking on his shout. Twenty-nine: Piet's hand going grey-hot on the gate. Thirty-six: Ashmarket from the crag, "a city betting its partners."

**Wants / fears.** Nobody under his shield dies. Fears fire, knows it. Dies in it, Issue 41.

**Voice.** Parade ground; counts his men; the enemy is "the problem."
1. "LINE! Daylight between you and I'll put my head through it!"
2. "You, the hot one. Stay hot. Easy to find."
3. "A marker's a marker, girl. I'm the house now."
4. "Nobody dies under my shield. That's the whole job."
5. "Bonds. Had a brother. Don't talk about it. LINE!"
6. "Pretty one with the chain: hit him first, he talks."
7. "I don't do mercy. The Director does mercy. I do standing in front."
8. "You cheated well. Now sit down. The stakes changed."
9. "Burn me, then. Burned before. Still counting."
10. "Piet."

**Crew.** Kaito: a brat he'd drill straight. Sela: salutes before fighting her. Juno: respect that looks like fear. Silas: "the pretty one." Bram: the man he'd want in his line. Ione: "the doctor who ran."

**Secrets.** His brother: half at the High Table (Issue 8), whole in one word as he burns (Issue 41). He never meant to take Ashka, only to recruit Juno (Issue 8).

**Wardrobe.** As `cast.js` (195 cm, open-faced helmet, dark grey coat, plated forearms and shins); invented: first name Dunstan, broken nose, a neck burn after Issue 9. Dress coat at the Cinder Palace (Issues 7, 8); Issue 9, shirt burned off, bandaged, grim, not fan service.

**Fighting style.** Twelve shields, one step per shout. Two shields himself, a short hooked cutter, grapples, headbutts. Fire cannot take the glaze; heat under it can.

**Never.** Retreats before his line. Hits anyone already down. Says "mercy." Bets. Takes the helmet off in front of troopers.

**Partner.** None. "The shield's my partner. Doesn't talk back."

## Four: Ledger Maro (52, man)

**Birthplace, family.** Counting houses of Meridian's capital; father a clerk. Wife Odile, in Meridian, believes he exports lamp oil. Daughters Perrin (27) and Lise (24).

**Before.** 21 to 30: Exchequer clerk tallying pylon costs per head. 30: customs factor at Lanternreach; its lender within five years. 45: Vasch's quartermaster wants monsters; Maro sees a partner is worth more stripped than alive, and buys. 48: takes on Silas (21). 51: the night of the eleven. 52: Issue 15.

**Memories.** Twenty-three: dead wells as a line item. Forty-eight: Silas talking his way out of a stabbing on his doorstep: "asset." Fifty-one: eleven empty cages, the pen stopping, then writing their names anyway.

**Wants / fears.** Books that balance; retiring to Odile. Fears being a number in someone else's book.

**Voice.** Polite; numbers as pronouns; never a threat, always a cost.
1. "You're a six, I'm afraid. Sixes don't get credit."
2. "Nobody uses names here. Names are a liability. I keep them."
3. "Who will you tell?"
4. "Sit. There's tea. The tea is on account."
5. "Silas, dear boy. My only entry in ink."
6. "A captain who can't remember what she owes. Imagine."
7. "I don't sell monsters. I sell the difference between two columns."
8. "Violence is a bad bargain. It never balances."
9. "The boy pays in eggs. I have that on file."
10. "Everyone owes me something. You owe me attention."

**Crew.** Kaito: a one-egg bad debt. Sela: a lender's dream. Juno: "I own the house, dear." Silas: the line in ink, crossed out. Bram: cheaper fed than fought. Ione: "the line item that wouldn't close."

**Secrets.** Against Order 12 he records every human and partner he sold, by name, in the Steam Ledger's back pages: Silas and the smugglers in Issue 16, the names in Issue 18 ("Who will you tell?"). A second ledger of what Vasch owes the Empire: insurance (later batch).

**Wardrobe.** As `cast.js` (172 cm, stout, balding, plum coat, white collar, gold belt, no black bar: bought, not sworn); invented: half-moon spectacles, inked finger, a hooded brass pocket lamp. Lantern Baths (Issue 16): towel wrap, shoulders-up, ledger in oilcloth; nightshirt and lamp in the warehouse (Issue 18).

**Fighting style.** None. The pocket lamp opens into a blinding beam he sweeps across Silas's dark; guards do the rest.

**Never.** Raises his voice. Carries a blade. Forgets a debt. Lies about a sum. Calls Silas "darling" back.

**Partner.** None.

## Five: Doctor Pike (39, man)

**Birthplace, family.** Knackers' Row, Meridian. Father a horse-doctor who put beasts down kindly; mother a midwife. First name Ambrose.

**Before.** 18 to 24 medical college; 25 surgeon. 27: hears Vasch (34) lecture "The Bond as Wound." 28: first Hollow surgery, a stripped mule kept walking. 30: runs the Glass Ward's theatre. 36: holds the clock for Ione (23); Vasch stops at five; Pike is reaching for the cutter when Vasch says "no." 37: the Ward closes; his notes stay (Issue 35). 39: Five.

**Memories.** Eight, plain and sad: his father's hand over a horse's eye, "the kindest thing is quick." Twenty-eight: the mule standing, Pike laughing. Thirty-six: "five," the hexagon cracking, "no."

**Wants / fears.** A Hollow that walks like it's alive; to finish Ione (he asks yearly). Fears boredom.

**Voice.** Bedside cheer; explains the procedure during it.
1. "Lovely. That's the worst of it. I'm lying."
2. "This will hurt a great deal. I don't believe in surprises."
3. "Doctor Half! Up the neck, I see. Let me look."
4. "Anyone can make a Hollow stand. I make it stroll."
5. "Hold still. The Orderly gets confused."
6. "A thermal case! Could I keep you?"
7. "Nobody dies in my theatre. They stop being the problem."
8. "Don't shout at the Director. He has a headache. I put it in a jar."
9. "Five is not a number. Five is an interruption."
10. "I don't name them, Deacon. I number them."

**Crew.** Kaito: "beautiful nerve damage." Sela: a memory he'd like to open. Juno: his thermal case (Issue 26). Silas: "larynx." Bram: "halfway to a specimen." Ione: unfinished work.

**Secrets.** Made Even a Hollow against Sorrel's request; keeps a plaster cast of Ione's cracked hexagon (both Issue 35). The Issue 45 mercy clinics are his design; his notes say "outpatient."

**Wardrobe (look invented).** 180 cm, soft, pink-cheeked, round spectacles, thinning fair hair, big clean hands, a whole-face smile. White apron over the grey coat, bar on the apron, sleeves pinned; theatre whites; a straw hat at the Regatta. No fan service; he is the horror.

**Fighting style.** He brings a patient. The Orderly pins; Pike follows with a cutter-scalpel and anaesthetic mist. He talks, you get sleepy. Juno's heat ignites the mist (Issue 26).

**Never.** Swears. Hurries. Kills what he could keep walking. Lies about what will hurt. Touches a Pal ("uninteresting").

**Partner: none. Patient: the Orderly.** A Hollow from a stripped ox-sized river-hog with two fitted surgical arms, a Glass Ward original. No temperament. Habits: tilts its head when Pike hums, follows his lamp, stands in doorways. Fights by charging and pinning. No affection; Pike pretends otherwise.

## Six: Lucan Ash (35, man)

**Birthplace, family.** Meridian capital, House Ash, sword-masters to the Regent's guard for four generations. Father Sir Corwin ran the salle; mother Lady Ilse; sister Maud (38) inherited it.

**Before.** 21 to 26: Regent's guard, undefeated. 27: escorting Vasch's unit, sent to a Cantor dock for Corin Vale (21), who sits with a hound and does not draw; Lucan calls it his only lost duel: the man was beaten before he arrived. 29: Six. 33: beats a bonded duelist without a bond, his proof. 35: Issue 25.

**Memories.** Twelve, plain: told to stand still an hour, standing two. Twenty-seven: the dock, Corin not drawing. Thirty-three: the salle silent as the bonded man fell.

**Wants / fears.** Proof that a man needs no bond. Fears wanting one.

**Voice.** Formal, complete sentences, courtesy as a blade, never innuendo.
1. "Sir. I am still here when you have finished spending."
2. "Captain Vey. An honour. I regret only the circumstances."
3. "Madam, a woman who shouts her odds has already lost them."
4. "Mister Moreau. Do up a button. You are bleeding on it."
5. "I do not Surge. I have nothing to borrow."
6. "I have lost one duel. He did not draw."
7. "Kindly stop talking. You cannot afford the breath."
8. "The Director's animals are not my concern. The Director is."
9. "You fight beautifully. A shame about everything else."
10. "Stand up, sir. I will wait. I am good at it."

**Crew.** Kaito: undisciplined; he'd take him as a student. Sela: bows. Juno: the shouted odds. Silas: his vulgar mirror; he can't stop watching. Bram: "the only honest weight in the room." Ione: blamed for the Ward.

**Secrets.** He brought Corin Vale to Vasch, said mid-fight to Silas (Issue 41). He let Silas live in Issue 26 on Vasch's order: the crew whole for the offer (Issue 36).

**Wardrobe (look invented).** 185 cm, slim, brown skin, close-cut black hair greying at the temples, thin moustache. Grey coat buttoned to the throat, cut like a fencing jacket, high white collar, bar, white gloves, straight cutter-sabre: Silas undone, Lucan done up. Full coat and parasol on the Regatta beach; sleeves rolled once, no further; Otti bets on one button and loses.

**Fighting style.** Salle fencing. Stands still and lets you spend, catches Silas's chain on the guard, wins by patience because he never pays. Issue 26: Silas outclassed. Issue 41: rematch left open, as in the outline.

**Never.** Surges. Flirts. Swears. Strikes first. Undoes the top button. Says "Hollow."

**Partner.** None; never bonded.

## Eight: The Ostlers, Bren and Hale (31 and 31, men)

**Birthplace, family.** Harrowmere, an Empire-side moor island where drovers ride red moor-stags. Parents Jory and Wyn (Ostler is the trade) died of moor fever when the twins were 20; sister Tamar (40) was the family's bonded rider.

**Before.** 21: they take the herd. 26: Harrowmere is pinned; the Sever strips every bonded drover; Tamar's stags Stile and Furrow become Hollows, and she never looks at them again. The unbonded twins sign on to keep the stags. 27: a lone Hollow stag keeps walking off cliffs, so they stitch the pair flank to flank. 31: Issue 25.

**Memories.** Ten, plain and warm: Tamar lifting them onto Stile. Twenty-six: the pound, Hale counting along to keep from screaming. Twenty-seven: the stitched pair's first still night.

**Wants / fears.** To keep the lads. Fear separation.

**Voice.** Moor-plain; they finish each other's sentences wrongly.
1. BREN: "The lads don't like fire." HALE: "They don't like anything."
2. HALE: "Hale, aye. No relation. Poor side."
3. BREN: "Don't call them that. They've got names."
4. HALE: "Wolf's a fine animal. Bit showy." BREN: "Bit small."
5. BREN: "Doctor, near the lads and I'll stitch you."
6. HALE: "Big man, what's in the stew?" BREN: "We're fighting."
7. BREN: "Sister never looked at them again." HALE: "We look for her."
8. HALE: "They still stop at cliffs. All they kept."
9. BREN: "We never paid a thing." HALE: "Not in coin."
10. Both, to Silas: "No." HALE: "He means maybe."

**Crew.** Kaito: jealous of the wolf. Sela: fear Vesh for the stags. Juno: Bren fancies her; she bets on which twin, wrongly (Issue 26). Silas: line 10. Bram: the stew recipe. Ione: would a half-cut stag know them?

**Secrets.** They never bonded; the other officers assume they were stripped; Pike says it aloud (Issue 26: "never paid a thing, lucky boys"). Likeliest defectors (later batch).

**Wardrobe (look invented).** Twins, 183 cm, rangy, wind-burned, grey-green eyes, sandy hair (Bren's long, Hale's cropped); Bren lacks a left ear-tip, Hale has a chipped tooth. Short grey riding coats, white collar, bar, chaps, stitching cable; oilskins; shirts off for a stag-washing, Otti scoring (filler-arc option).

**Fighting style.** Mounted only. The pair charges as one; the twins rope riders off and drag them. The cores show only when the pair is split.

**Never.** Spur. Leave a downed stag. Say "Hollow" about the lads. Separate. Let Pike near them.

**Partners (Hollows): Stile and Furrow.** Red moor-stags, 1.9 m at the shoulder, antlers iron-capped, ash-grey with red at the hocks, plates on brow and spine, white chest cores, stitched flank to flank. Before: Stile skittish, Furrow a bully; now nothing, though Bren swears Stile leans. Habits: step in unison, stop at cliffs, turn to Hale's whistle (he thinks). Fight: paired charge, ram, drag. Affection: none left; the twins brush them nightly.

## Ten: Sable Oriel (28, woman)

**Birthplace, family.** Meridian. A foundling left at the Regent's chapel, raised in the palace as a ward-servant, named by the steward for the coat she came in and the window above the steps. No family; Regent Emeric Hale (63) is the nearest thing, and even he has never seen her face.

**Before.** 18: palace cipher clerk. 22: Fetch, a mimic-salamander from the cisterns, takes her face while she sleeps; she wakes to herself looking back. Power: Fetch lends her the face of whoever you trust most. First cost: her own face leaves her memory; mirrors show strangers. 24: the Regent's envoy to Vasch, "Ten." 28: Issue 46, wearing Sela.

**Memories.** Seven, plain and sad: the steward counting the wards and skipping her. Twenty-two: the mirror. Twenty-four: Vasch, who trusts no one, looking straight at her: "I know you're there, child."

**Wants / fears.** To be seen once. Fears she's forgotten what that looks like.

**Voice.** Borrowed, in the voice of the face she wears; the tell is a pause before names. Her own voice: rare, quiet, questions.
1. (as anyone) "...Pup. There you are."
2. "Do you know what you look like? I'd like to."
3. "I've read every page. I know her better than she does."
4. (as Sela) "I wrote it down. I always do."
5. "The Director sees me. It's the worst thing about him."
6. "The Regent sends his regards. He never sends anything else."
7. "You caught me because she forgot. I'll remember that."
8. "Nobody trusts the gambler enough to stand still."
9. "Fetch isn't a pet. Don't make the joke."
10. "Ten is the one you don't see coming."

**Crew.** Kaito: caught her. Sela: knows her better than Sela does. Juno: can't be held. Silas: impossible; he trusts no one. Bram: he'd feed her and she'd eat; it frightens her. Ione: her only hope.

**Secrets.** She is Ten (Issue 46). The Sever keeps one live bond while cutting everyone else's; Kaito says so (Issue 46). She reports to the Regent, not Vasch (later batch).

**Wardrobe.** Whatever the trusted person wears, from that sheet; never their fan service (Issue 46 tell: "Sela" buttons the navigator's jacket). True look, two panels only (Issue 46): 165 cm, slight, mid-brown skin, cropped dark hair, flat grey eyes, unmarked grey coat, no bar or collar, black gloves. Tired and ordinary.

**Fighting style.** None. She vanishes into a crowd as each person's friend; Fetch bites, numbing; she runs.

**Never.** Shows her own face by choice. Lies in her own voice. Lets anyone joke that Fetch is a Pal.

**Partner: Fetch.** Pale, eyeless, forearm-long, skin like wet paper that takes the colour and warmth of what it touches. From the palace cisterns. Clingy, cold, curious. Habits: sleeps under her collar, licks mirrors, goes the colour of whoever is speaking. Fights with one numbing bite, then plays dead as a glove. Affection: it takes her face, the only creature that remembers it.
