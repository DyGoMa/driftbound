## Candidate 7. Petra Marlowe — the ship's surgeon ("Doc")

- **Age / build:** 34, woman. 170 cm (5'7"), stocky and strong: broad shoulders, thick forearms from hauling bodies, wide hips, stands with her feet planted.
- **Face / hair:** olive skin with a permanent sunburn across the nose, dark brown eyes under heavy straight brows, a chipped front tooth she shows when she grins. Black hair cut short and messy, shaved above the left ear, tied under a knotted red kerchief when she works. Her lips are always chapped white at the corners (see cost).
- **Outfit:** a sleeveless surgeon's apron, once white, now salt-stained, worn over a short white halter top that leaves her shoulders and midriff bare; both forearms wrapped in linen to the elbow; cut-off canvas trousers to mid-thigh with a belt of corked bottles, needles and a tin cup; tall rubber waders rolled down to the knee; a waterskin on a cross-strap, always. Her partner rides across her shoulders like a living stole (below).
- **Bond mark:** a single hollow diamond (a salt crystal, four straight sides, nothing inside) on the inside of her right forearm, glowing pale coral. Distinct from Ione's cracked hexagon by shape (four sides, whole) and colour.
- **Personality:** brisk, bossy, loud and cheerful, swears like a deckhand and means it kindly. Treats every body as a job, so fan service bounces off her: she has seen six of those today and yours is fine. The only person on the Gullwake who tells Bram to sit down, and he sits. Never uses anyone's name; she calls people by whatever is wrong with them (see running gag). Under all of it, a woman who left the Empire's navy rather than sign one piece of paper, and has never told anyone which paper.
- **Colour:** salt white + coral pink (`#f1ede4`, `#e8738f`). Nobody else on the cast uses pink.
- **Partner: Alum**, a salt moray the size of a large dog: a thick, air-breathing eel, bone-white, crusted along the back in flat plates of salt that flake and regrow, pink gill frills, pale pink-rimmed eyes that look blind (it tastes the air instead). Coils across Petra's shoulders and hangs down her back; strikes like a snapped rope. Hates fresh water, loves brine, licks wounds clean.
- **Borrowed power:** **salt-crusting.** She can pull the water out of something and leave salt behind: seal a bleeding wound shut in seconds with a crust that falls off a week later, lock a Hollow's cables or a trooper's grip in a crust that cramps and cracks, drop a fog into brine rain, dull a fire by a notch. A thrown crust shatters into stinging grit.
- **Bond cost (drying out):** every big Surge pulls the water out of Petra as well. **Base state:** lips chapped white at the corners, white salt crust on the knuckles of both hands, a thirst she never catches up with (the waterskin is her signature prop, the way the logbook is Sela's). Push too far and her skin cracks in a flat tile pattern like a dry salt pan, from the knuckles up, which stings in seawater and heals slow. Gets worse in the story: the cracks climb to the elbow and stop fading, she stops being able to sweat (dangerous on a hot island), and by Part Two she cannot cry at all, which is the one that gets her. Costs stay paid.

### Backstory (adult only; told in talk, no flashback pages)
At 24 Petra was a surgeon in the Meridian navy on a pinning fleet. At 25 a captain put a form in front of her certifying a stripped partner "sound for refit" and she walked off the ship at Saltlight, the neutral atoll, where nobody asks. She bonded with Alum at 26 by accident: it was a pest living in the fish market's brine vats, it bit through her hand and would not let go, and she stitched her own hand around its teeth while it watched her. ("It let go when I was done. Professional courtesy.") For eight years she has been the Regatta's hired field surgeon between Regattas and the atoll's only doctor the rest of the time. She knows the Sever's work from the other side of the table: she has sewn up what their cutters leave behind.

### How she joins (gap between Issue 27 and Issue 28; changes no beat)
Issue 27 ends with survivors at sea at dawn after Saltlight burns. Issue 28 opens with the Gullwake limping into Keelbreak. In between: the Gullwake is carrying wounded from three crews and nobody aboard can set a bone. Petra comes over the rail from a Red Tally longboat with Alum around her neck, a stretcher in each hand and no invitation, exactly as Otti did a page earlier, because her clinic was the atoll and the atoll is gone. She works through the whole crossing. She is not one of the Regatta's truce officials (who die in Issue 25; she is hired staff, elbow-deep in a racer's leg that night and never near them), so nothing in Arc 5 moves. By Keelbreak the wounded are off and she is still aboard, because Sela has quietly written her into the roster under "surgeon" and Petra finds out when Bram hands her a bowl. Her first panel of real feeling: Issue 32, the only one she cannot save is Old Tam, and the bible's beat (plain, ugly, one rude word, no last speech) stays exactly as written; Petra is simply the hands that fail, and she salts the deck where Tam lay so it never rots.

Mandatory beats she would stand inside without changing them: Issue 28 (dock work clothes; she scores Kaito lower than Juno and Otti did), Issue 36 (Vasch's free strip: for her, warm hands and a glass of water she could taste; her answer is one word and a drink from her own skin), Issue 40 (a one-on-one: she and Otti share the flask, two women who left uniforms), Issue 42 (flung with the rest). An alternative gap with a smaller footprint is 38 to 39 (the nine weeks before Anvil Rock), listed under Decisions.

### Part Two look (Issues 43 on; she is 36)
Kerchief gone; hair grown to the shoulders and gone salt-white at the ends; the tile cracks reach both elbows and are drawn every time; two waterskins now; the apron replaced by a long sleeveless oilskin coat worn open over the same halter. Alum's salt plates have gone thick and yellowed like old ivory. She runs a clinic on Juno's hulk, the Second Chance, which the outline already makes a refugee ship (Issue 43), so she is there to be found with Juno in Issue 44 and no beat moves.

### Three lines in her voice
1. "Arm, sit. Knee, get him a stool. Lungs, breathe on my needle one more time and I'll stitch your collar shut."
2. "Shirt off. No, that's not a flirt. Yes it is. Both things are true and I've still got a needle, so hold still."
3. (Silas: "Salt in the wound, darling? Cruel.") "Not darling. Doc. And it's not cruel, it's what salt's *for*. Cruel is me telling you how long you'd have lasted without it. Eleven minutes. Hold still."

### Running gag
She never uses names, only injuries, and the crew's names drift with their wounds: Kaito is "Arm" (permanently) and "Ears" whenever he blushes; Bram is "Knee"; Silas is "Lungs"; Juno is "Hands"; Ione is "Wrist" and bristles at it ("I am the doctor." "You're *a* doctor. I'm the doc."; Ione's footnote caption: *Imprecise.*); Sela, who is never hurt, is "Captain" through gritted teeth. Readers keep score of whose name has changed. The payoff is saved for a later outline batch: the one time she uses a real name, the panel goes silent, and everyone aboard knows what it means.

## Candidate 8. Reuben Fell — the grower

- **Age / build:** 34, man (he joins in Part Two, so this is his age on arrival; born the same year as Petra). 185 cm (6'1"), long-limbed and rangy, a farmer's forearms and hands, a slight stoop from bending to beds, moves slowly and never hurries.
- **Face / hair:** deep brown skin, hazel eyes with heavy lids, laugh lines already deep, a short beard flecked grey. Hair close-cropped and greying at both temples; the grey is his cost, and it spreads (below). Looks ten years older than he is, and gets asked about it.
- **Outfit:** no shirt under a long patched field coat with the sleeves torn off, worn open; a cross-strap of leather seed pouches over the bare chest; loose linen trousers rolled to the knee; barefoot on deck and on soil (he says he needs to feel it), soft boots tied to his belt for towns; a trowel where other men carry a knife. Dirt under every nail.
- **Bond mark:** a seed with one curled shoot (a dot and a single hook of a line) high on his right cheekbone, glowing moss green. The first mark on anyone's face in the cast, so it reads at once in a crowd.
- **Personality:** patient, wry, unhurried, an optimist who has earned it the hard way. Asks questions where other people make statements. Thinks in seasons and says "Give it a week." Takes Silas's flirting entirely at face value and agrees with all of it, which is the only defence that has ever worked. Plants things in other people's belongings. Under it: a man spending the years of his own life, on purpose, to feed strangers, who has decided that is a fair price and does not want to talk about it.
- **Colour:** moss green + bark brown (`#6a9b3c`, `#5a3a22`). Nobody else on the cast uses green.
- **Partner: Fallow**, a bark-scaled pangolin the size of a cart: overlapping plates of dark bark with moss in the seams, a long heavy tail that roots into soil (or deck planking, to Bram's fury) to drink, small black eyes, a sticky tongue. Eats rot and spoiled stores, so the galley hates it and Grist adores it. Rolls into a ball like a seed when it sleeps and unrolls leaving green shoots where it lay. Restless where Grist is slow: climbs rigging, digs, chews rope.
- **Borrowed power:** **growing.** Roots burst from any wood or soil to grab, trip and bind; bark armours a forearm for one blow; vines haul and lash. His real gift is slower and bigger: he can make dead soil live again for one season, which pinned islands (grey soil, dead wells, Issue 1) cannot do on their own.
- **Bond cost (years):** Fallow grows by taking time, and every Surge takes it from Reuben: his hair greys, his joints age, his hands spot. **Base state:** 34 with grey temples, a grey-flecked beard, early lines and a back that aches before rain. Gets worse in the story: the beard goes white, the hands go old, and he looks fifty before he is thirty-six. Nothing comes back. Costs stay paid. The one rule he keeps: he picks what he spends them on.

### Backstory (adult only; told in talk, no flashback pages)
Son of a seed merchant on an island that was pinned early. Drank the inheritance in his twenties. At 27 he found Fallow half dead in the island's grey soil, a beast that lives only on living ground, and carried it off the island on his back to the next one. He has been carrying it from soil to soil for seven years, buying one living season after another with his own, and feeding whoever was standing nearby while he did it. He came to Dunmarrow in the Problem years because it was the hungriest place he had heard of. He has never married; "nobody's waited for me since I started greying, which is their loss and a long list."

### How he joins (gap between Issue 45 and Issue 46; changes no beat)
Issue 45 is Bram's: half iron, feeding a collapsed island on nothing, Deacon Sorrel at the table, and Bram finally standing up. Issue 46 is the rescue of Silas from flooded Lanternreach. In between, the crew sails from Dunmarrow, and the question the outline never has to answer is who feeds Dunmarrow when Bram leaves it. The answer is the man who has been making its soil live from a greenhouse built inside a wrecked hull behind Bram's kitchen, with Fallow rooted under the floor: the only green on the island, which is why "on nothing" was ever possible. Bram will not leave until the island can eat; Reuben tells him it can ("Give it a week. Then shout at it, that's how you do everything else.") and that the Sever's mercy clinics are two streets from his beds and he will not spend another year of himself on soil they mean to pin. He comes aboard the stolen hull with his seed pouches and his trowel, Fallow swimming behind with its tail wrapped on the rudder, and plants cress in Kaito's spare boot before Lanternreach. He is there, silent, on the hull in Issue 48 when Kaito opens the chart, and the final-panel beat is untouched.

Because he joins in Part Two, he has no Part One beats, no Part Two "new look" and no place in the Issue 42 wreck; the outline past Issue 48 is open for him by the bible's own last line.

### Three lines in his voice
1. (Kaito: "We don't have a week.") "Then give it six days and shout at it. That's how you do everything else."
2. (Juno: "Odds you're forty, old man?") "Thirty-four. The rest I spent. Ask me what on." (Juno, after a beat: "No bet.")
3. (Silas: "You know, darling, I could make you very happy.") "Yes." "...Yes?" "Yes. Sit down, you look pale, I'll make tea." (Silas, to no one: "That's never happened to me before.")

### Running gag
Wherever Reuben has sat, something sprouts a few panels later: a tomato vine in the bilge, cress in a boot, moss on the mast under the chalk betting pool (which Juno treats as tampering), a flower in Fallow's seams, and a whole green crown on Grist's shell that Grist refuses to let anyone pull off. Readers learn to check the background of any scene he was in. Bram's Galley Rules gain a new one on his first week: "Nobody's partner drinks the deck."

## Three more filler-arc premises (beyond Issues 49 Laundry Day, 50 Galley Rules Volume Two, 51 The Hot Spring Island)

Each is one issue, comedy first, moves one relationship or plants one small thing, inside the fan service line.

1. **"Portrait Day."** A travelling painter is hired to put the crew on a Regatta programme; a full issue of outfit fights, pose fights and Silas directing everyone's "good side"; the finished painting is of Grist, asleep, and it is the best thing anyone has ever painted.
2. **"Where's Grist."** A truck-sized iron beetle goes missing in a market town and the crew splits into pairs to find it, each pair's search drawn as a different kind of story (Juno and Kaito a heist, Silas and Ione a romance, Sela and Bram a quiet walk); Grist is asleep on the one pier they all passed four times.
3. **"Three Birthdays."** Sela's logbooks give three different dates for her birthday and she cannot remember which is right, so the crew holds all three in one week, each party worse than the last; the punchline is her logbook entry, and the heart beat is that she writes down which one she liked best, so it can never go missing again.
