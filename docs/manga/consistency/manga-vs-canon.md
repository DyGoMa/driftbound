# Manga vs canon: consistency report (2026-11-07)

Read-only audit of `manga/issues/issue-01.js` to `issue-51.js` against `docs/CAST.md` (looks and ages), the locked sheets in `art/refs/STYLE.md`, `docs/WORLD_BIBLE.md` (hard rules), `docs/manga/SERIES_BIBLE.md` (sections 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9) and `docs/manga/WRITER_HANDOFFS.md` (known conflicts). Issues 1 to 24 were read closely (never audited before); 25 to 51 were skimmed for hard-rule breaks and cross-issue contradictions. Standing Order 12 was checked word for word against `shared/stories.js`; Pal names against `shared/pals.js`; three facts against `docs/manga/backstory/kaito.md`.

Page refs are `pN.M` = page N, panel M (a spread counts as two pages). Severity: **HARD RULE** (breaks a World Bible / Series Bible section 1 rule) · **CANON** (contradicts CAST.md, a locked sheet, a bible beat/plant/secret, or a fact another issue states) · **DRIFT** (wording or small-fact drift; a register call) · **STYLE** (voice, pronoun, framing, format).

Counting rule: each distinct item is counted once. A cross-issue contradiction is counted on the later issue (the one that diverges from what was already on the page), unless the earlier issue is the one that contradicts CAST.md or the bible. Items already in WRITER_HANDOFFS.md are marked **(handoffs)** and counted only where this pass extends them.
