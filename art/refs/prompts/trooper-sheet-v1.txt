Character reference turnaround sheet for building a 3D model. Image 1 is a reference for ART STYLE ONLY (line weight, cel shading, colour treatment, layout, background): do NOT copy that character's face, hair, outfit or marks. Image 2 is a guide ONLY for how hands look from behind in the BACK view: one smooth back of the hand, fingers together, a short thumb behind the hand on the inner side; do NOT copy anything else from it. Draw a new character, described below.

2000s TV-anime cel-shaded style: clean black ink lines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, slightly muted early-2000s colours.

Four full-body views of the same person in one row, same scale, evenly spaced, not overlapping, feet on the same baseline, head tops level:
(1) FRONT view, facing the viewer.
(2) LEFT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the LEFT edge of the image, so their LEFT arm and LEFT side are nearest us.
(3) RIGHT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the RIGHT edge of the image, so their RIGHT arm and RIGHT side are nearest us.
(4) BACK view, seen from directly behind.
In both side views the legs are in line: the far leg sits directly behind the near leg, so the far boot is mostly hidden. No three-quarter angles anywhere.
Pose: A-POSE in every view: standing straight, feet about shoulder-width apart, arms straight and raised away from the body so each arm makes a 45-degree angle with the torso, hands out at hip height, a clear gap of grey background between each arm and the body. Neutral face, mouth closed.

Character: a Sever trooper, an anonymous enemy soldier. An ADULT man in his thirties with an adult jaw and adult proportions, about 180 cm, fit but not bulky. Medium skin, clean-shaven.
Head: a plain grey helmet covering the top, back and sides of the head, with a solid black visor across the eyes. The eyes are completely hidden. The jaw, closed mouth and nose tip show below the visor. No hair shows.
Outfit: a long plain grey coat, closed all the way down the front and belted at the waist with a black belt. The coat ends at the knee, so the lower legs show clearly in every view. Full-length sleeves to the wrists. A white stand-up collar. Black gloves on both hands. Dark grey trousers. Plain black boots.

Chest bar, front of the coat only: one flat black bar runs straight across the chest from armpit to armpit. At the centre of the chest, one plain white ring sits on the bar, so the bar cuts straight through the middle of the ring. It is simple geometry and must not look like a letter or any written character.
- FRONT view: the whole bar and ring, centred on the chest.
- LEFT SIDE and RIGHT SIDE views: only the end of the bar shows, on the front of the chest; no ring.
- BACK view: hidden. The back of the coat is plain grey with no marks.

Weapon: a bond-cutter, one short hooked blade about as long as a forearm, its steel glowing faintly white, hanging point-down from the belt at his RIGHT hip.
- FRONT view: on the LEFT side of the image.
- RIGHT SIDE view: clearly visible at the hip nearest us.
- BACK view: on the RIGHT side of the image, partly hidden by the coat.
- LEFT SIDE view: hidden (far side).
His LEFT hip has nothing on it. Both hands are empty.

Plain flat mid-grey background. No floor, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no symbols, no emblems. The one exception is the white ring on his chest bar described above.
