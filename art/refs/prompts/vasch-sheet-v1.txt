Draw a new character, described below.

2000s TV-anime cel-shaded style: clean black ink lines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, slightly muted early-2000s colours.

Four full-body views of the same person in one row, same scale, evenly spaced, not overlapping, feet on the same baseline, head tops level:
(1) FRONT view, facing the viewer.
(2) LEFT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the LEFT edge of the image, so their LEFT arm and LEFT side are nearest us.
(3) RIGHT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the RIGHT edge of the image, so their RIGHT arm and RIGHT side are nearest us.
(4) BACK view, seen from directly behind.
In both side views the legs are in line: the far leg sits directly behind the near leg, so the far boot is mostly hidden. No three-quarter angles anywhere.
Pose: A-POSE in every view: standing straight, feet about shoulder-width apart, arms straight and raised away from the body so each arm makes a 45-degree angle with the torso, hands out at hip height, a clear gap of grey background between each arm and the body. A tiny closed-mouth smile: lips closed, the corners barely raised.

Character: Director Aurel Vasch, a 46-year-old ADULT man with a thin, lightly lined adult face and adult proportions, 188 cm, tall and thin. Pale skin, pale grey eyes. Neat grey hair combed straight back, with no parting.
Outfit: a long plain WHITE coat, closed all the way down the front and belted at the waist with a black belt. The coat ends at the knee, so the lower legs show clearly in every view. A white stand-up collar. Dark grey trousers. Plain black boots. The coat has no weapon or anything else hanging from the belt.

Chest bar, front of the coat only: one flat black bar runs straight across the chest from armpit to armpit. At the centre of the chest, one plain BLACK ring sits on the bar, so the bar cuts straight through the middle of the ring. It is simple geometry and must not look like a letter or any written character.

His RIGHT arm: the white coat sleeve is rolled up to just below the elbow, ending in a white rolled cuff. - FRONT view: on the LEFT side of the image.
