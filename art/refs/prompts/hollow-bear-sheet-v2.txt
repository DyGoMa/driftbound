Edit of Image 1 (a four-view creature reference sheet of a Hollow bear-beast). Image 2 is a reference for ART STYLE ONLY (line weight, cel shading, colours); do not copy anything else from it.

Change ONLY these two things:

1. The core. It must be built INTO the chest, not hanging on the chain like a pendant. The round glowing white core and its dark metal frame are sunk into the fur in the middle of the chest, with the fur meeting the edge of the frame all the way round and no gap between the frame and the body. The chain no longer touches the frame: it hangs from the collar and ends freely a little above the frame, just a few links long.
- FRONT view: the core is fully visible, centred on the chest, and the short chain ends above it.
- LEFT SIDE and RIGHT SIDE views: the frame sits flush against the front curve of the chest, inside the body's outline. Only its edge and a thin sliver of white glow show. It does not stick out in front of the chest or dangle. The short chain hangs straight down from the collar in front of the neck and ends above the frame.
- BACK view: unchanged, core hidden.
The cables stay attached to the frame and still run up to the shoulder plates.

2. The tail. Replace the round fluffy ball with a short, plain stub of coarse, flat, ash-grey fur, about as long as a paw, pointing down and slightly back, with a rough spiky outline. It must not look round, soft or cute. Show it in the LEFT SIDE, RIGHT SIDE and BACK views.

Keep everything else IDENTICAL: the head, blank white eyes, brow plate, ears and closed mouth; the ash-grey fur; the iron collar; the shoulder plates and spine plates; the cables' colour and thickness; the legs and claws; the neutral standing pose, the four views, their positions, the line style and the scale.

Plain flat mid-grey background. No floor, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no symbols, no emblems.
