Creature reference turnaround sheet of an ORIGINAL monster, for building a 3D model. Image 1 is for ART STYLE ONLY (the 2000s TV-anime cel-shaded look: clean black ink lines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, slightly muted early-2000s colours). Do NOT draw the human from Image 1.

Layout: four full-body views of the same creature in one row, same scale, evenly spaced, not overlapping, all standing on the same baseline:
(1) FRONT view, head-on, facing the viewer.
(2) LEFT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the LEFT edge of the image (its left side nearest us).
(3) RIGHT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the RIGHT edge of the image (its right side nearest us).
(4) BACK view, seen from directly behind.
In the side views the far legs sit directly behind the near legs and are mostly hidden. No three-quarter angles anywhere. Plain flat mid-grey background. No floor, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no symbols, no humans, no saddle, no collar, no harness.

Pose (neutral, for 3D modelling): standing square on all four legs, legs straight and evenly spaced, head level and facing straight ahead, mouth closed with a few fang tips showing, tail held straight out behind, fin fully raised.

The creature: Skarn, a storm wolf the size of a horse. A huge, heavy-boned but lean wolf with long legs, big paws and a thick neck; sharp angular lines and some menace, like a cool 2000s anime beast. Not cute, not a dog, and not resembling any existing anime or game creature. Slate-grey fur, shaggier at the neck and shoulders. Glowing yellow eyes and a grumpy, scowling brow. A tall sail-like fin runs along its spine from the back of the neck to the base of the tail: a thin darker-grey membrane stretched over long spines, with small crackling electric-yellow lightning arcs along its top edge. A forked tail: the tail splits into two separate tips about halfway along.

Every detail (fin shape and height, the fork in the tail, fur colour, eye glow) stays identical in all four views.
