Creature reference turnaround sheet for building a 3D model. Image 1 is a reference for ART STYLE ONLY (line weight, cel shading, colour treatment, layout, background): do NOT copy that creature's body, fur or colours. Draw a new creature, described below.

2000s TV-anime cel-shaded style: clean black ink lines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, slightly muted early-2000s colours.

Four full-body views of the same creature in one row, same scale, evenly spaced, not overlapping, all on the same baseline:
(1) FRONT view, head-on, facing the viewer.
(2) LEFT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the LEFT edge of the image.
(3) RIGHT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the RIGHT edge of the image.
(4) BACK view, seen from directly behind.
In the side views the far legs sit directly behind the near legs and are mostly hidden. No three-quarter angles anywhere.
Pose (neutral, for 3D modelling): standing square, legs straight and evenly spaced, head level facing ahead, mouth closed, tail held straight out behind.

The creature: Hush, an ORIGINAL silent wind owl-lynx the size of a leopard. A big cat's body: long, lean and muscular, with four strong legs, large soft paws and a short thick lynx bobtail. The head has a flat round OWL FACE: a pale round facial disc framed by a ring of darker feathers, a short hooked owl beak instead of a cat's mouth, and lynx ear tufts on top of the head. Glowing violet eyes, large and round. Smoky grey-violet fur on the body, the same colour feathers on the head, chest and wings, pale grey belly, darker charcoal-violet wing tips. A pair of OWL WINGS growing from the shoulders, FOLDED neatly along both sides of the body; the long flight feathers along the folded wing edges are narrow and pointed like knife blades, with crisp sharp tips, so they look dangerous. Sharp angular lines and some menace.
- FRONT view: the round owl face head-on with both eyes, beak and ear tufts; both folded wings visible at the shoulders.
- LEFT and RIGHT SIDE views: the near folded wing lying along the body from shoulder to hip with its blade-like feather tips, the bobtail held straight out behind.
- BACK view: both folded wings from behind, the back of the head with ear tufts, and the short bobtail.
Every detail stays identical in all four views.

Plain flat mid-grey background. No floor, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no symbols, no emblems.
