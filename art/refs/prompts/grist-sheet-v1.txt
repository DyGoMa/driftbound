Creature reference turnaround sheet for building a 3D model. Image 1 is a reference for ART STYLE ONLY (line weight, cel shading, colour treatment, layout, background): do NOT copy that creature's body, fur or colours. Draw a new creature, described below.

2000s TV-anime cel-shaded style: clean black ink lines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, slightly muted early-2000s colours.

Four full-body views of the same creature in one row, same scale, evenly spaced, not overlapping, all on the same baseline:
(1) FRONT view, head-on, facing the viewer.
(2) LEFT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the LEFT edge of the image.
(3) RIGHT SIDE view: a true 90-degree profile facing the RIGHT edge of the image.
(4) BACK view, seen from directly behind.
In the side views the far legs sit directly behind the near legs and are mostly hidden. No three-quarter angles anywhere.
Pose (neutral, for 3D modelling): standing square, legs straight and evenly spaced, head level facing ahead, mouth closed, the rear of the shell ends in a rounded plate, no tail.

The creature: Grist, an ORIGINAL iron rhino-beetle beast (in the story it is the size of a truck). A huge, heavy, low beetle body covered by a domed SHELL of overlapping dark iron plates, gunmetal grey with a dull metallic sheen, rivet-like bumps along the plate edges and a few rust-orange scuffs. ONE huge horn rising from the front of the head, curving up and forward, shaped like a blacksmith's ANVIL seen from the side: a thick flat-topped block with a pointed tip sticking forward. A short armoured head under the horn with small mandibles closed, and two small glowing rust-orange eyes. SIX thick, stubby, armoured legs, three on each side, evenly spaced under the shell, all six planted on the ground, each ending in a blunt clawed foot. No tail, no wings showing (the shell is closed), no fur. Slow, stubborn and heavy-looking, with sharp angular plate edges and some menace.
- FRONT view: the face head-on with both glowing eyes, the anvil horn rising up the middle, the front pair of legs, the shell dome behind.
- LEFT and RIGHT SIDE views: the anvil horn in clear profile (flat top, forward point), the domed shell, all three near legs visible and the far legs behind them.
- BACK view: the rounded rear plate of the shell, the back legs, and the top of the horn just showing above the shell.
Every detail stays identical in all four views.

Plain flat mid-grey background. No floor, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no symbols, no emblems.
