## COLD OPEN

## SCENE 1 — INT. CINDER PALACE, HIGH TABLE ROOM — NIGHT (≈ 1:00)

Black. Stills from last week slide past: the High Table under a chandelier, Juno's hand of cards, Krell's fist flipping the table, two bare palms, blistered and shining.

BRAM: (V.O., slow, over the stills) Last time. We put on clothes. Juno played cards with a man who has a neck like a ham. She won. He said cheat, which is what hams say. Then she took her gloves off. Also, Grist was a table. He was very good at it. (beat) I was wearing a vest. It was a good vest. Remember it.

SMASH CUT:

The room, live. Cards still falling. The table on its side. Juno Calloway, 23, in a sparkling red gown, stands in the middle of the room with both bare hands up. Fire runs across her palms like oil, blue at the roots.

Opposite her: WARDEN KRELL, 41, 195 cm of heavy trooper officer, grey coat strained over plate, a tower shield of grey forge-iron on his left arm. Behind him eight troopers lock eight more shields into a wall across the doors.

KRELL: Line! Lock! Nobody leaves this room with anything they didn't pay for, and that includes breathing!
JUNO: (grin) Evens I walk out of here. Three to one I walk out on fire.
KAITO: (hissing, beside her) Those are both bad!
JUNO: Those are both mine, sweetheart.

She throws. A fist of fire crosses the room and spreads flat across Krell's shield like water on a hot stove. The shield doesn't even steam.

KRELL: (laughing) Ha! Forge-iron, cheat. Burn it all night. I'll bring a chair.
JUNO: (looking at her own hand) Huh.
KRELL: "Huh." That's what you've got? I've had cheats beg. Cheats bribe. Cheats cry on my boots. "Huh."
JUNO: Give me a second, sweetheart. I'm working out your price.
KRELL: My price is the bird on your shoulder and the hands you hide it behind.
JUNO: Then you'll want to be quicker. The hands are going up in value.

Beside her, Kaito rips the top button off his waistcoat trying to breathe.

KAITO: Screw that. Screw this. Screw this WAISTCOAT—

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

## PART A

## SCENE 2 — INT. CINDER PALACE, HIGH TABLE ROOM — NIGHT (≈ 2:00)

The shield wall advances a step. Clang. A step. Clang.

Sela Vey, 28, in a backless midnight gown slit to the thigh, hasn't moved from her chair. She is writing in her logbook.

SELA: (not looking up) Pup, they're going to walk us into the wall.
KAITO: I can see that!
SELA: Then stop standing where the wall is.
KAITO: Where ELSE is there? It's a room! It's got walls in all the directions!
SELA: Up. Down. Through.
KAITO: Those aren't directions, those are—
SELA: Pup. Pick one.

Bram Torvald, 31, in a waiter's vest two sizes too small, sets down the drinks tray he has been holding this whole time. Carefully. He rolls his neck.

BRAM: Galley rule six. Nobody fights in formalwear.
JUNO: Then take it off, big man.

Bram's skin goes iron grey from the shoulders down. The vest surrenders. Every seam at once, a sound like a ripped sail. A button pings off a shield.

BRAM: (looking down, sad) ...That was a good vest.
SELA: (writing) Very good vest. Noted.

Krell's line hits Bram. He catches two shields on his forearms and stops them dead. Boots skid on marble.

KRELL: Push! It's one man!
BRAM: It's one cook.
KRELL: A cook! Boys, we're being held off by the kitchen!
BRAM: (mild) Kitchen's closed.

Juno throws low, under the shields, at boots. A trooper hops. Another. The line wobbles.

A small ember-coloured numeral flickers into being beside Juno's head, the way she sees her own odds: 37.

JUNO: Thirty-seven.
KAITO: What?
JUNO: Odds on you doing something stupid in the next ten seconds.
KAITO: That's not how odds—

He turns, runs for the window to call Skarn, and walks straight into the chandelier chain. Clang. The chandelier swings.

JUNO: (to Sela) Pay me.
SELA: (writing) I'll owe you.
KAITO: You rigged that!
JUNO: Sweetheart, I don't need to rig you. You come pre-rigged.

Kaito, rubbing his forehead, hauls the window open and whistles with two fingers.

SFX: a long whistle, then a wolf's howl answering from the roofs

A moment. Then SKARN, a storm wolf the size of a horse, comes through the skylight instead. Glass everywhere. He lands on the overturned table, fin crackling, looks at Kaito's waistcoat, and looks away. Disgusted.

KAITO: Don't start.
SELA: He's not wrong, pup. That waistcoat's doing something to your shoulders.
KAITO: Good something?
SELA: (writing) Noted.

Under the gaming tables something enormous yawns. The "coffee table" the crew brought in rises on six legs. GRIST, an iron rhino-beetle the size of a truck, blinks at the room with three drinks and an ashtray still balanced on his shell.

SELA: (standing at last, closing the logbook) Bram. Door.
BRAM: We came in the door.
SELA: We did. Make a new one.

Bram slaps Grist's flank. Grist walks through the wall. Not fast. Through. Plaster, brick, a painting of somebody's grandfather.

KRELL: Where is it— WHERE IS IT GOING? That's a load-bearing WALL!
TROOPER: Sir, it was a coffee table—
KRELL: I KNOW what it was! I put my DRINK on it!

The crew follows Grist out into the night through a hole shaped like a beetle. On the way past, Sela's slit skirt flicks aside as she high-kicks a trooper's shield out of her path.

SELA: That's what the slit's for, pup.
KAITO: (ears red) I didn't ask!
SELA: You were going to.

Behind them Krell, furious, points his whole arm at the hole.

KRELL: After them! Into the street! I want that cheat's marker in my fist by Ember Night or I want your coats!

## SCENE 3 — EXT. ASHMARKET, LANTERN STREET — NIGHT (≈ 2:30)

Ashmarket at night: a crag of a city, timber halls stacked up soot-black rock, a thousand paper lanterns on lines between them. Steam breathes up through long iron grates in the street. The whole island exhales.

The crew spills down a stair. Kaito is tearing the waistcoat off over his head.

KAITO: (muffled, stuck) Why is it TIGHTER going off?
JUNO: Five to one you're stuck till morning.

Sela pulls it off him in one yank. He stands there in his undershirt, half strangled, hair wrecked. Juno's eyes flick over him and off.

JUNO: (big laugh) Oh, no. Hold on. I'm fine. I'm fine.
KAITO: WHAT.
JUNO: Nothing, sweetheart. Nothing. You just look like a lamp someone forgot to light.
KAITO: That's not even— what does that MEAN?
SELA: It means run, pup.

Down the stair behind them: boots. The shield wall comes around the corner in a crescent, Krell in the middle. Lantern-lines catch on the shield rims and tear. Paper fire rains.

KRELL: There! Red dress, big bald, and the one in his vest! Shields up, cutters low! Nobody burns tonight but her!

ASHKA, a fire raptor the size of an eagle, drops out of the dark onto Juno's shoulder, sparks trailing. She screams once at the trooper on the far left, whose shield strap has come loose.

JUNO: Good girl. Left end, Kaito! Loose strap!
KAITO: On it!

Kaito runs left. Juno throws left. Kaito runs directly into where the fire is going.

KAITO: (ducking, hair singed) OI!
JUNO: You said ON IT!
KAITO: On it with LIGHTNING, not with my FACE!
JUNO: Then LEAD with the lightning! What am I meant to aim at, your good intentions?
KAITO: You could aim at THEM!
JUNO: You're STANDING on them!
SELA: (passing, calm) Children.

Kaito's mark flares yellow on the back of his hand. He slaps his gauntlet to the loose shield and lightning crawls across the iron; the trooper lets go of it, dancing. Juno's fire lands in the gap. The man goes down, coat smoking, alive.

The numeral beside her head: 38.

JUNO: Thirty-eight.
KAITO: You said thirty-seven.
JUNO: It went up.
KAITO: What went UP?

Sela, cool, drags Kaito out of a cutter's swing by the collar. She has her spyglass in the other hand like a short club.

SELA: Pup. Watch her face, not her hands.

Juno's face. Flushed. Sweat on her lip, in a market where everyone else is cold. Ashka shifts on her shoulder, uneasy.

KAITO: (quiet) ...Are those odds?
JUNO: (grin, not quite right) Everything's odds, sweetheart. Mine just come with a temperature.

Across the street a shutter bangs open. NIM HADDA, 30, one-armed, a bookmaker's green eyeshade, leans out of a betting window that is still, impossibly, open for business.

NIM: (calling, mild) Juno! The street's got you at nine to four to live. Want a piece?
JUNO: Put me down for all of it, Nim!
NIM: You haven't got all of it, love. That's rather the point of tonight.
JUNO: Then put me down for Kaito's!
KAITO: I'm not— How much is nine to four?
NIM: Bad, son. Not hopeless. Hopeless is what I had you at in the Palace.
KAITO: You were taking bets on us IN the Palace?
NIM: I take bets on the weather. You lot are more fun than weather. (to Juno, nodding at the shields) They're on the grates, love. The whole line's standing on the grates.

Juno looks down. Under the shield wall's boots: the long iron grates of the street, breathing steam.

JUNO: (slow) ...Yeah. Yeah, they are.
SELA: (pen already out) Say it out loud, so I can be against it.

Krell's wall takes another step. Bram, iron to the elbows, braces across the stair-head with Grist's head beside his hip, holding the whole street shut with his back.

KRELL: Move, cook!
BRAM: (gentle) Soup's on. Nobody's eating.
KRELL: What does that MEAN?
BRAM: It means move me.

[EYECATCH: a Cinderbun, a warm ember dumpling with tiny feet, asleep on Grist's anvil horn while Grist sleeps too. Steam rises from both of them.]

## PART B

## SCENE 4 — EXT. ASHMARKET, THE VENT STAIR — NIGHT (≈ 1:20)

Behind Bram's line. A side stair drops into the rock, and heat climbs out of it like something alive. At the bottom, a round iron throat the size of a cartwheel: a vent mouth, breathing slow.

JUNO: This whole crag's a kettle. The market's the lid. Those grates are where the lid lets the steam out.
KAITO: How do you know this town's plumbing?
JUNO: (beat) Don't ask, sweetheart.
KAITO: I just did—
SELA: And she said don't. Go on, Juno.
JUNO: Shut the side vents, pile heat into this one throat, and the pressure climbs under the grates till they blow. Krell's shields stop fire. They don't stop the street.
SELA: And the heat comes from.
JUNO: (holds up both bare hands) Where d'you think.

Silence. Steam.

KAITO: No.
JUNO: Wasn't a question.
KAITO: Those are forty-whatever odds! Those are the worst odds I've ever heard!
JUNO: (big laugh) Sweetheart, they're not odds. (quiet) They haven't been odds since thirty-eight.
KAITO: You don't even know if it works!
JUNO: I know what this rock does when you feed it. Trust me.
KAITO: WHY would I—
JUNO: Because you've got a snowball's chance on this crag of a better plan, and we both know it.

Sela stops writing. That's new.

SELA: How much do you need to hold?
JUNO: More than I've got.
SELA: For how long.
JUNO: Till it pops.
BRAM: (from the stair-head, over his shoulder, not turning) Pot stays on past the boil, it goes over the sides. Nothing you can do after.
JUNO: Then catch what spills, big man.

Bram doesn't answer.

SELA: Juno. Tell me the truth once, and I'll write it down and never ask again. Can you hold it?
JUNO: (beat) Captain, I've never held anything in my life. That's why I throw.

Kaito looks between them.

KAITO: There's another way. There's always—
JUNO: Name one.
KAITO: (opens his mouth; nothing comes) ...Screw that.
JUNO: There it is. (jabs a finger at him) You. Lightning. Keep that wall on the grates. If they step off, I cooked a street for nothing.
KAITO: How am I meant to keep nine men—
JUNO: Be annoying. You're good at it.
KAITO: I'm not ANNOYING, I'm—
SELA: (writing again) It's true, pup.
KAITO: Whose side are you ON?
SELA: Hers. Yours. The ship's. Go.

Kaito drags both hands down his face and goes. As he passes, Ashka hops from Juno's shoulder to his and screams once in his ear.

JUNO: (to the bird, soft) Spot for him. I've got mine.

## SCENE 5 — EXT. ASHMARKET, LANTERN STREET / THE VENT THROAT — NIGHT (≈ 1:45)

Intercut.

The street. Kaito walks out from behind Bram alone, in his undershirt, gauntlet crackling, and stands in the middle of the grates in front of nine shields and a Warden.

KAITO: Hey. Ham-neck.
KRELL: (incredulous) What did you call—
KAITO: You heard. I'm the one who's been cheating. She was just holding the cards. Come get me.
KRELL: Boy, I've buried louder than you before breakfast.
KAITO: Then skip breakfast! Come ON!

Lightning jumps from his gauntlet and runs along all nine shield rims at once, a crawling yellow line. Nine troopers brace. Iron shields on an iron street: every man's boots lock to the grates to hold the charge.

KRELL: HOLD! It's a tickle! Hold your ground!

The vent. Juno kneels at the throat with both hands in the heat. Fire pours from her palms down into the dark, steadily, like water from a jug.

Numeral: 39.

JUNO: (breath short) Thirty-nine.

The street. Kaito ducks a thrown cutter. Skarn drops on the man who threw it.

KAITO: Hold your ground, he says! Yeah! Hold it! Right there! NOBODY MOVE!

The vent. 40. Her skin has gone stove-glow at the wrists; the light is under it, inside her forearms. Sela crouches at her side, gown ruined, not touching her, because you can't.

JUNO: Forty. (a cracked laugh) Forty's a lot of odds, Captain.
SELA: You've stopped making sense.
JUNO: Been doing that since my boots came off. Write it down.
SELA: (pen moving) I am.
JUNO: Write that I looked good doing it.
SELA: I'm writing that you look like a stove.
JUNO: Stoves are warm. People like stoves.

The grates glow dull red under the troopers. Somebody's boots start to smoke. Krell feels it through his soles and looks down.

KRELL: ...Off the grates. OFF THE—

Kaito slams his whole arm down. Lightning floods the iron. Nine men seize to the floor one second longer.

KAITO: (screaming it) I SAID NOBODY MOVE!

The vent. 41. 42. The numeral starts to blur. Juno's eyes are wet and shining and the grin is gone.

JUNO: (barely) Forty-two. That's not odds. That's just me now.
SELA: Juno. Let go.
JUNO: Not. Yet.

The throat below her turns white.

SFX: a sound like the whole island drawing breath

SMASH CUT:

The street. Every grate under the shield wall blows upward at once. A column of white steam and fire goes up the height of the crag, taking paper lanterns with it like sparks. Nine shields go nine directions. Kaito, who was also standing on the grates, goes up too, and comes down in Skarn's jaws by the back of his undershirt.

SKARN: (a grumble, unimpressed)
KAITO: (upside down, hair on end) Yeah. Thanks. Say it back.

## SCENE 6 — EXT. ASHMARKET, LANTERN STREET — NIGHT (≈ 0:45)

Steam clears. Krell is on one knee in a crater of torn iron, his tower shield glowing cherry-red beside him, his left cheek and ear burned raw, his coat smoking off his shoulders. Troopers crawl. Two of them drag a third.

Kaito stands over him, swaying, gauntlet dead, right arm hanging.

KAITO: (panting) You want... to try the street again?
KRELL: (through his teeth, drill-sergeant even now) Pick up your wounded! Line! FORM on me!

He gets up. It costs him. He looks past Kaito toward the vent stair, where Sela is climbing into view carrying nothing and shouting Bram's name.

KRELL: Tell the cheat her marker's not paid. It's bought. Bought is worse. (beat) You think this was a fight? This was the Sever finding out what a street costs. Now we know. The Sever doesn't forget a debt, boy. I'll come back for every coin, and I'll have the bird.
KAITO: (flat) Then I'll be here. Same street. I'll draw you a map.
KRELL: Good. Saves me looking.

He turns, half his face ruined, and walks his broken line out through the burning lanterns, roaring at them to march in step. They do.

## SCENE 7 — EXT. ASHMARKET HARBOUR — NIGHT (≈ 1:30)

Bram comes down the harbour stair at a run with Juno in his arms. Her hands are fists against her chest and they are still burning, small blue flames she can't put out; the air above her shimmers like a road at noon. The gown is scorched to rags at the wrists. She's talking.

JUNO: (fever, fast) Bram. Big man. Odds. Give me odds.
BRAM: No.
JUNO: Everyone gives me odds—
BRAM: Not taking that bet.

He doesn't stop at the quay. He walks straight off the edge into the black harbour, boots, dungarees, iron and all, goes in to his chest with her held high, then lowers her into the water.

SFX: a hiss like a forge quenched, long

Steam rolls off her. The blue flames in her fists go out. Bram stands in the sea holding her, and his face does the thing it does. Misty. Jaw set.

BRAM: (very quiet) There. Cooling rack. Shh.

Kaito reaches the quay's edge and stops dead. Skarn beside him; Sela behind, barefoot now, shoes in one hand, logbook in the other.

KAITO: Is she—
SELA: Her hands have stopped. (watching) Her inside hasn't. Not yet.
KAITO: She said forty-two. Like it was a bet. Like it was a joke.
SELA: It was both. That's how she carries it. (beat) You carry yours up your arm, pup. Don't look at hers like it's stranger.
KAITO: (looking at his dead arm) ...It's not a joke, though.
SELA: No. Nothing we pay is. We just say it like one, so the deck doesn't go quiet.

Under the water, around Bram's legs, lantern-lights come on one by one along a ridge of dark scales. VESH, the serpent-dragon long as the ship, has coiled the whole harbour mouth and is holding the cold water still around the two of them. Nobody asked her to.

JUNO: (eyes half shut, to Bram) Hey. Did we win?
BRAM: You're asking the wrong cook.
JUNO: (a tiny laugh that hurts) ...Did I?
BRAM: Street says yes.
JUNO: Nim'll never pay out.
BRAM: He will. I'll ask him nicely.
JUNO: (fading) You asking nicely is... the scariest thing on this island...

Her eyes close. Bram looks up the harbour stair at Sela and shakes his head once, small: not dying, not good.

On the quay stones at Kaito's feet: her gloves. Fingerless, scorched, one lying inside the other. Sela carried them here and hasn't mentioned it. He picks them up with his good hand. He doesn't know what to do with them. He holds them.

Sela opens the logbook against her knee and writes. We read it, in her hand:

"Ashmarket. Juno bet the house on herself and won. The house is on fire. Bram has lost a vest. The pup has learned what a number means. I am against all of it. I'm keeping her."

She underlines the last three words.
