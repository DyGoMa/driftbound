## SCENE 1 — RECAP / EXT. THE GULLWAKE, PINHOLD HARBOUR — DAWN (≈ 1:00)

Stills from Episodes 1 to 4 flip past while BRAM (V.O.) talks. He is not a natural narrator.

BRAM (V.O.): Kid stole a parcel. Kid got chased. Kid lost, badly, woke up in my galley and ate three bowls without chewing. Then the Sever read their order out, and the kid said a rude word to the whole Empire.
Still: Kaito mid-air between rooftops, a goat under each arm.
BRAM (V.O.): Then he ran every partner left in town out to us over the roofs. Sela said "pup." He went red. The pylon finished. The island stopped.
Still: a wall-chart of Pinhold, a hand chalking CORRECT under it.
BRAM (V.O.): Vegetables eaten: none. That's where we are. You missed the stew.

SMASH CUT:

EXT. THE GULLWAKE, PINHOLD HARBOUR — DAWN. Kaito at the rail, jacket tied round his waist, right arm bandaged wrist to elbow. Skarn beside him, ears flat. The Sever cutters are gone from the harbour. Up the hill the pylon stands grey on grey, humming so low it's a feeling in the teeth.

Water. Flat as a plate. Nothing slaps the hull.

KAITO: It's wrong.
SELA (O.S.): Specifically.
She's above him on the quarterdeck, logbook open, pencil moving. She hasn't looked up.
KAITO: The water's not doing the thing. The— (slaps the rail in a rhythm) —thing. The hull always goes like that. It's just sat there.
SELA: Island's not moving. Water's got nothing to move against.
KAITO: And where the hell are the birds.
Her pencil stops. She looks up. Empty sky over the harbour. Not one gull.
SELA: (quiet) Birds follow the drift.
KAITO: So?
SELA: So there's nothing to follow.
KAITO: That's the stupidest thing I've ever heard. Birds don't know about— they're birds.
SELA: Then you tell me where they've gone.
Skarn lifts his head and makes a sound. Not a growl. Lower than that.

EXT. PINHOLD, WELL SQUARE — SAME. A man of about sixty kneels at the well with a bucket that came up grey, weeping. Two doors along a family hangs bunting; through their open door, a wall-chart with RIGHT chalked under it.

## PART A

## SCENE 2 — EXT. PINHOLD, HARBOUR STREET — MORNING (≈ 1:15)

Kaito walks up from the pier alone. Behind him on the deck, Skarn sits with the face of a wolf who has been told to stay and has opinions about it.

KAITO: (over his shoulder) Stay. I mean it. Half this town would sell you for a bucket of clean water.
Skarn turns his back on him. Pointedly.
KAITO: Don't give me that. You'd sell me for a bucket.

The street. Pinhold is loud and wrong in two directions at once: a tavern open at nine in the morning, men singing inside; across the way a woman on her step with her face in her apron. Nobody looks at anybody.

A chart stall. FENNA DRAY (44, broad, ink to the elbows, red eyes) is chalking a new sign: CHARTS OF PINHOLD. CORRECT. She's crying while she does it, steadily, like weather.

KAITO: Fenna.
FENNA: Brask. Still alive, then.
KAITO: Mostly. (nods at the sign) You know that's a lie.
FENNA: It's the first true thing I've ever sold. Forty years my mother sold charts of this island, and every one of them was wrong by Tuesday. (sets the chalk down) Mine won't be. Not ever. Isn't that something.
KAITO: It's right because it's dead.
FENNA: It's right because it's still. Same thing, if you sell charts. (wipes her face with the back of a wrist, leaves ink) Buy one. Last chance to own a map that's right.
KAITO: With what. I've got six eggs.
FENNA: Eggs'd do. Nobody's got coin, and the hens stopped laying when the hum started.
KAITO: Screw that.
FENNA: (almost a laugh) Yeah. That's about what I thought you'd say.
He goes. She picks the chalk up and underlines CORRECT.

## SCENE 3 — EXT. ROOKE'S COOPERAGE — MORNING (≈ 1:40)

A narrow lane. A cooper's shop, barrels stacked out front, a ladder up the side to one room over the workshop: a shutter painted red, badly. Kaito slows. This is home.

The shop door opens before he gets there. DANNEL ROOKE (34, cooper's shoulders, a mallet he hasn't put down) fills it. Behind him in the dark, HESPER ROOKE (61, small, grey plait, flour on her apron) stands at the bench and doesn't come forward.

KAITO: Dannel. I just want my—
DANNEL: No.
KAITO: My stuff's up there. My sheets. My father's—
DANNEL: I said no.
KAITO: I'll go round the back. Up the ladder. You won't even—
DANNEL: I took the ladder down.
Beat. Kaito looks past him to Hesper. She's looking at the bench.
KAITO: Hesper. I brought your cough bottle. Four days ago. You paid me in eggs.
DANNEL: And three days ago a man in a white mask put a hound through a roof two streets over, looking for you. And two days ago they lined us up. All of us. Ma stood in that queue, Kaito. With the shop cat.
KAITO: They were coming anyway. The pylon was coming anyway, that's not—
DANNEL: They were coming for the island. (one step down, the mallet still low) They came into our houses for you. You and that animal. Everyone on this street knows whose room that is.
Kaito's hands are fists. The right arm hurts. He makes the hands open.
KAITO: (not loud, for once) It's not an animal.
DANNEL: It's gone or you are. That's the order, and they left two men on the pylon to see it kept.
KAITO: Two men. I ran past forty of them yesterday.
DANNEL: And look what it got us.
Long beat. Hesper puts a hand flat on the bench, like she's steadying it.
KAITO: (to her) Say something.
She doesn't. Dannel shuts the door. The bolt goes across. It's a good bolt; Dannel made it.

Kaito stands in the lane. Turns. He's at the barrels when the red shutter creaks above. A basket comes down on a string, slow, the way you lower something you don't want broken.

Six eggs. A folded cloth under them.

Hesper's face in the window, a moment only. Not crying. Past that.
HESPER: Six. It's what the hens had. You were never going to stay here, love. Your father went to sea and the sea kept him. Go and see what it wants with you.
The shutter closes. Kaito holds the basket like it's a baby.
KAITO: (to the shutter, barely) Thanks for the eggs.

And then the ground moves.

## SCENE 4 — THE WORLDQUAKE (≈ 0:50)

Not an earthquake. Nothing falls, at first. A pulse: one slow heave under everything, like the sea has taken a breath.

The eggs rattle in the basket. Kaito's bond mark lights white-yellow through the bandage and his arm goes from aching to screaming.

KAITO: Gh— what—
SFX: every dog in Pinhold, at once

INT. CHART STALL. Fenna's shelf of brand-new correct charts slides off the wall across the cobbles.

EXT. GULLWAKE. Skarn rears on the deck and HOWLS, a sound that isn't a wolf's, a sound with lightning in it. The fin along his spine stands straight and crackles.

The harbour bulges. VESH comes up out of it: forty metres of dark-blue serpent in a harbour built for fishing boats, lantern-lures blazing, jaw-frill wide, and she SCREAMS at the empty sky.

On the quarterdeck Sela has one hand clamped below her left collarbone, over the mark. Her face has gone grey. Her other hand is still writing.

SELA: (through her teeth) Vesh. I'm here. I know. I know.

Below, Grist, who has slept through two episodes of warfare, OPENS HIS EYES. Six legs find the deck. Bram comes up the galley ladder and stops dead, towel in hand.
BRAM: (barely a whisper) Oh, that's bad. Grist. Grist, lie down, you'll go through the planks.

SMASH CUT:

EXT. A FAR ISLAND — SAME. Rain. A long iron chain across wet stone. At the end of it a BEAR, ash-grey, two metres at the shoulder, plates on brow and spine, a white core set in its chest, eyes blank as milk. A Hollow.

It looks up.

Hollows don't look up.

A black-gloved hand on the chain tightens. We don't see whose.

SFX: the chain going taut, one link at a time

Pinhold: it passes. Kaito on one knee in the lane, basket upright, not one egg broken. The pylon hasn't moved. Its hum is exactly what it was. That's the worst part.

## SCENE 5 — INT. A WHITE ROOM — DAY (≈ 0:45)

No windows. One chart pinned flat on a long table: the whole Drift, hundreds of islands, inked by a careful hand. A tray of tea.

DIRECTOR AUREL VASCH (46, tall, thin, grey hair combed back, a tiny closed-mouth smile) stands over the chart. White Sever coat, closed and belted in black. His right forearm is a glass tube between steel rings; inside it a small white core turns slowly, like something asleep. The bare metal fingers are very steady.

ADJUTANT CORREN (38, man, grey coat, a telegraph slip held like it might bite) in the doorway.

CORREN: Pinhold, Director. The pylon's seated. Full arrest of drift at... (checks) ...four minutes past six. There was a... the crews report a tremor, sir. Felt at sea. Felt everywhere, they're saying.
VASCH: (not looking up) Was anyone hurt?
CORREN: On Pinhold? No, sir. Some crockery.
VASCH: Then it was a good morning.
CORREN: The engineers are asking whether the tremor was... expected, sir.
VASCH: A body flinches when the needle goes in, Adjutant. It doesn't mean the needle's wrong. Tell them it was expected.
He takes a pen. On the chart, over the small dot that is Pinhold, he draws one short straight line. Clean. Through a circle somebody inked there weeks ago.
VASCH: Have the detachment open the stores. Nobody on that island pays for bread today. They've paid enough.
CORREN: Sir.
He goes. Vasch alone, looking at the line the way a doctor looks at a stitch he's pleased with.
VASCH: (gently, to no one) One.
The core in his arm turns over.

[EYECATCH: Grist asleep across the gangplank, one egg balanced on the tip of his anvil horn, the sky above him empty of birds.]

## PART B

## SCENE 6 — EXT. GULLWAKE, MAIN DECK — AFTERNOON (≈ 1:50)

Kaito comes up the gangplank with a basket of eggs and nothing else, climbing over Grist, asleep across it again. Skarn shoves his whole head into Kaito's chest, hard enough to stagger him.

KAITO: Yeah. Yeah, I felt it too. I'm fine. Shut up.
Sela is on a crate by the mast, logbook on her knee, mug beside her. She's been waiting and has arranged herself not to look like it.
SELA: Eggs.
KAITO: Eggs.
SELA: Your sheets?
KAITO: Behind a door. Good bolt. (sits on the deck, back to the mast, basket between his boots) Dannel made it. I watched him make it.
Sela lets it sit. By the galley hatch, Bram has quietly stopped chopping.
KAITO: They stood in the queue. With the shop cat. I didn't— I didn't even think about the cat.
SELA: Is the cat all right?
KAITO: (a breath) Yeah. It's a cat. Nobody bothers to cut a cat. (beat) What was that. At noon. My arm went off like I'd Surged and I hadn't touched him.
SELA: Don't know.
KAITO: You don't know. You're the one who feels the—
SELA: I feel the islands, pup. That wasn't an island. Vesh didn't know what it was either, and she's older than this town. (taps the logbook) I wrote down what it felt like. It'll have to do till it happens again.
KAITO: Again?
SELA: Things that happen once don't bother writing down.
KAITO: That doesn't even—
SELA: Good. Then there's one thing on this island that's all right, and you're about to leave it behind. Fair's fair.
KAITO: (sharp) You think I'm leaving.
SELA: I think you walked back up my gangplank with eggs instead of sheets, pup. I'm the navigator. I notice which way things are going.
He glares. She drinks her tea.
KAITO: Pinhold's mine.
SELA: It was. (pause; gentler) It isn't the Sever that took it off you. It's the pylon. The island was yours while it moved. You could have learned it. There's nothing left to learn now; Fenna's got it all on one sheet.
KAITO: (quiet) Screw that.
SELA: There he is.
She reaches into the crate under her and brings out a salt-stained ledger, spine broken in. Opens it on the deck in front of him, turns it round.

The ship's roster. In her hand: SELA VEY — Captain. Under that, in enormous careful print: BRAM TORVALD — Shipwright. Cook. Under that, a great many empty lines.

KAITO: That's it? Two of you?
BRAM: (from the hatch) Three. Grist does the sleeping.
SELA: And Vesh does the pulling, and nobody signs for her because she can't hold a pen.
KAITO: Can Grist?
BRAM: Won't.
SELA: Sign under deckhand.
KAITO: (reading) There isn't a line for deckhand.
SELA: Write "deckhand" and sign under it. You're educated. You stole survey sheets.
KAITO: Deckhand. I carried the whole town's partners across the roofs and I'm "deckhand."
SELA: You dropped a goat.
KAITO: I CAUGHT the goat—
SELA: Everyone starts at deckhand. I started at deckhand.
KAITO: On your own ship?
SELA: (not missing a beat) I write things down, pup. Doesn't mean I remember who was captain.
It lands wrong, and she knows it, and lets it go by with a sip of tea. Kaito doesn't catch it. Bram does, and goes back to chopping.

Kaito takes the pencil. Writes DECKHAND, too big. Signs KAITO BRASK under it, bigger.
SELA: (looking at it) Your father teach you to write?
KAITO: Nah. He taught me to draw a coastline. Writing I got off the backs of his sheets.
SELA: Hm. (takes the roster back, blows on it) Welcome aboard the Gullwake. Pay is eggs.
KAITO: (half a grin, the first today) I've got six.
SELA: Then you're rich. Bram, he's rich.
BRAM: Galley rule six. Deckhands eat a vegetable.
KAITO: Oh, come ON—

## SCENE 7 — INT. GULLWAKE GALLEY — LATE AFTERNOON (≈ 1:30)

Low beams, a stove, bunches of onions, the GALLEY RULES chalked on a board: 1 to 5, and a fresh 6 with nothing after it. Bram has produced a tailor's tape from somewhere on his enormous person.

BRAM: Arms up.
KAITO: Why.
BRAM: Measuring.
KAITO: For what.
BRAM: (unrolling the tape) Arms up.
Kaito puts his arms up like a man being robbed. Bram measures the shoulders, writes a number beside the 6.
Sela leans in the doorway, mug in hand, enjoying herself immensely.
SELA: Three eggs says he lasts to the inseam.
BRAM: (measuring the chest) He won't last to the inseam.
KAITO: WHAT inseam?
BRAM: Breathe in.
Kaito breathes in. His ears go red first, exactly as advertised. In the hatch above, Skarn's face appears upside down and settles into pure disgust.
KAITO: Is this— is this for a uniform? Is there a uniform?
SELA: Does the uniform come with a shirt, Bram?
BRAM: (writing) Hadn't decided.
KAITO: Hadn't DECIDED—
SELA: Hold still, pup. He's got the tape out; it's practically a ceremony. He's very particular about fit.
KAITO: Nobody said anything about a FIT— why is it always the ears—
SELA: Because they go first. I'm thinking of charting them.
BRAM: Arm out.
He measures the right arm, careful over the bandage. His big thumb never touches the fork scars at the elbow. He looks at them anyway, and his eyes go wet.
BRAM: Hm.
KAITO: (deflating, embarrassed a different way) It's fine. It's just the elbow.
BRAM: (gruff) Mm. Other arm.
SELA: Turn him round, Bram. I want to see if the back's as sorry as the front.
Kaito turns round to object and walks straight into the hanging galley lamp.
SFX: a brass lamp, a skull, a long slow swing
Beat.
SELA: (to Bram) That's my three eggs.
BRAM: You bet inseam.
SELA: I bet he'd not get that far. I didn't say how.
Kaito, holding his head, small:
KAITO: Is there a uniform or not.
BRAM: No.
KAITO: Then what the hell were you—
BRAM: Hammock. (rolls the tape up) You're longer than the last one. He'd have had his feet in the soup. Twice.
KAITO: You could have just ASKED how tall—
BRAM: Wanted to see if you'd stand still.
He rubs the number off the board with the heel of his hand and writes something else. The board now reads: 6. KAITO EATS A VEGETABLE.
KAITO: That was ALREADY a rule—
BRAM: Now it's written.
SELA: (already writing in the logbook) "Day one. Deckhand measured for a hammock. Walked into the lamp. Bet lost on a technicality." (looks up) I'm putting the eggs down as a ship's expense.

## SCENE 8 — EXT. GULLWAKE, FOREDECK — DUSK (≈ 0:50)

The sun goes down on a sea that doesn't move. Pinhold behind them, every window lit, a party and a wake going on in the same street.

Kaito has a crate on its side for a desk, a sheet of chart paper nailed to it at the corners. Blank. Nothing on it at all.

He looks at it a long time. Skarn lies down with his head across Kaito's boots, which is not a thing Skarn does.

KAITO: (to the wolf) First map of the Drift that stays right.
Skarn grumbles.
KAITO: Yeah. Say it back.
Nothing.
KAITO: You never say it back.
He takes a pencil, and in the middle of all that white he makes one small careful dot. Beside it, in his too-big hand: PINHOLD. Under that, smaller: doesn't move.
KAITO: (flat) Hell of a start. One island, and it's right because it's dead.
Beat. He looks at the dot.
KAITO: Fine. That's the one that stands still. Everything else, I'll chase.
SELA: (from the wheel, behind him) That's a dot, pup.
KAITO: (not turning) It's a map.
SELA: It's a very confident dot.
KAITO: First one's free. The rest I have to earn.

Sela watches him a while. She doesn't write this down. Bram comes up the ladder with a bowl, sets it by Kaito's elbow without a word, and goes.

Kaito looks in. Stew. On top, a carrot cut into the shape of a flower, pointedly.

He eats it. The carrot too. Doesn't say anything.

From the hatch, Bram's eyes: wet.

BRAM: (to himself, thick) He ate the vegetable. Didn't even look at it first.

## SCENE 9 — INT. SELA'S CABIN — NIGHT (≈ 0:40)

Bunk, desk, lamp. Through the porthole Vesh's lure-lights move slow under the water, the only thing in the harbour moving.

Sela at the desk, jacket off, hair down for the first time in the series. The logbook open. She writes.

INSERT — the page, in her hand: "New deckhand. K. Brask, 21. Loud. Draws coastlines, can't write small. Eats vegetables under duress. Pinhold barred its door; he brought the eggs anyway. Vesh screamed at noon, and I felt the island stop under us like a clock. I don't know what we felt. Write it down anyway."

She blows on the ink. Pages back idly while it dries, toward the front. Years of her hand.

The first page.

INSERT — her hand, but younger, pressed harder into the paper: "Ask him about the song. He will say you made it up. You did not."

Sela stops. Reads it twice. Her face does nothing at all, and that's how we know.

SELA: (very quiet) Who's "him."

Nobody answers. The lamp. The lures through the glass.

Her thumb finds the inside margin, where the page before this should be. An edge. Torn, not cut, and old: soft as cloth, where a thumb has gone over it for years without knowing why.

Close on the thumb on the torn edge. Hold.

MUSIC: three low notes, very slow. Nothing else.
