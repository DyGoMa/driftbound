## SCENE 1 — INT. THE GULLWAKE, PASSAGE — MORNING (≈ 1:10)

Black. A slate comes up, held in a pale hand, chalk in a looping, dreadful script.

SILAS (SLATE): PREVIOUSLY.
KAITO (V.O.): (flat, put-upon) I'm not reading these.
SILAS (SLATE): GO ON, DARLING.
KAITO (V.O.): ...Fine. "Previously. Lanternreach. The port of lamps, where nobody uses a name and everybody uses Ledger Maro." Used. Past tense. "The crew fell in with a dashing smuggler," you've written dashing twice, "who sold them to Maro on purpose, locked them in a warehouse, and then..."

SILAS (SLATE): (crammed to the edges) AND THEN CUT EVERY LAMP IN THE BUILDING WITHOUT A SOUND, SAVED ALL YOUR LIVES, AND LOST HIS VOICE DOING IT. YOU'RE WELCOME.
KAITO (V.O.): He's written "you're welcome" bigger than the rest.
SILAS (SLATE): ALSO KAITO SAID "I KNEW IT." HE DID NOT KNOW IT.
KAITO (V.O.): I KNEW it.
SILAS (SLATE): HE GAVE ME MY SLATE BACK AND TOLD ME TO GET ON THE BOAT. IT WAS VERY ROMANTIC.
KAITO (V.O.): IT WAS NOT—

The slate drops. Fade up on the passage. Kaito (21, goggles on his forehead, red to the hairline). Silas (24, white shirt open to the stomach, dark-teal hair tied low, violet eyes delighted) leaning on the bulkhead with the slate. Through the hatch, chalk on the mast: SILAS SPEAKS — DAY 3 — 7 TO 1. Beneath it, looping: 4 TO 1, DARLING.

JUNO (O.S.): It's day four, sweetheart. Nobody's spoken, nobody's paid, and he keeps changing my odds.
KAITO: Three more days of this and the slate goes in the sea.
SILAS (SLATE): YOU'D MISS ME.
KAITO: You haven't SAID anything to miss!
JUNO (O.S.): He said plenty in the galley yesterday. In chalk. Bram's still red.
KAITO: Bram doesn't go red.
JUNO (O.S.): He went a colour.

Silas taps the slate, then his throat, and holds two fingers there a beat longer than the joke needs. The smile never moves. Kaito doesn't see it. Bram (31, huge, shirtless under his dungarees, towel on his shoulder), passing with a flour sack, does.

## SCENE 2 — EXT. THE WICK, LANTERNREACH — MORNING (≈ 1:30)

The Wick: a sagging timber quay at the dark end of Lanternreach, lamp poles every ten paces, half of them dead. The Gullwake alongside, pale new planking in her hull. Grist asleep across the gangplank, the recovered cargo stacked on his shell like a shelf.

Sela (28, cropped blue jacket, silver-blue ponytail, logbook open) at the rail. Juno (23, Ashka on her shoulder) on the quay with a dock hand (forties) weighing crates. Silas on a bollard, chain-blade across his knees, Hush the owl-lynx beside him, perfectly still. Across the quay Skarn is also still, and furious about it.

JUNO: Four days tied to this plank. Why?
SELA: The ebb runs at dusk. Bram wanted the hull whole. And the port's only just stopped asking about a warehouse.
JUNO: We should've gone the morning after.
SELA: We should.
JUNO: Nine to one somebody from that warehouse is still breathing and holding a grudge.
SELA: Shorter than that. (writing) Which is why we're tied up at the end nobody lights.
JUNO: And the chalk?
SELA: Somebody needs to stop bleeding chalk on my deck.
DOCK HAND: Eleven crates. You said twelve.
JUNO: One's under the beetle.
DOCK HAND: Under the—
JUNO: Don't ask him. He's not a morning person.

Silas writes. Holds it up to the rail with a flourish.

SILAS (SLATE): CAPTAIN. THAT JACKET. WHAT DOES IT DO IN THE RAIN?
SELA: (not looking up) Gets wet. Unlike your shirt, which has never been done up long enough to find out.

Silas, wounded, writes again.

SILAS (SLATE): THAT WAS COLD.
SELA: (writing) So is the rain.

She turns the book to him.

SELA (V.O.): (writing) Day four. The smuggler has written me a compliment, a complaint and a question about my jacket. Answered all three. He has run out of slate. I have not run out of book.

Silas looks at the logbook, then his slate: a general sizing up a bigger army.

On deck, Kaito inks the quay onto his chart board.

KAITO: (to nobody) Right forever. (beat) It had better be. It's nailed to the bottom.

Silas drifts over.

SILAS (SLATE): YOU'VE DRAWN THE QUAY.
KAITO: I've drawn the quay.
SILAS (SLATE): IT'S A QUAY. IT ISN'T GOING ANYWHERE.
KAITO: That's why I drew it. One thing on this sheet that stays put.
SILAS (SLATE): I STAY PUT. DRAW ME.
KAITO: You sold us to a man with a ledger.
SILAS (SLATE): BRIEFLY.
KAITO: You don't get to be briefly sold.
SILAS (SLATE): EVERYONE IS, DARLING. SOME OF US GET A RECEIPT.

Skarn stalks the quay to Hush and growls, low. Hush turns its flat owl face all the way round to look at him. Not a sound. Skarn's fin crackles with indignation.

SFX: KRAK

He stomps back and sits with his back to it.

JUNO: Even money the wolf hates the cat because the cat's quieter than him.
KAITO: Skarn's not loud.
SFX: KRAK-K
KAITO: ...He's got a lot to say.

Bram comes up the gangplank, stepping over Grist.

BRAM: Hull's done.
SELA: Dusk, then.

Bram looks at Silas, who is pretending to look at a lamp pole.

BRAM: (quiet, to Sela) How long's the longest he's gone?
SELA: He wrote "nine days." Underlined it. (closes the book) Which is how you know it was longer.
BRAM: Nine's long.
SELA: He'll talk when he's got something to say that isn't about your shoulders.
BRAM: (misty) They're good shoulders.
SELA: They are. Go and put a shirt on them.
BRAM: Shirts catch fire.

## SCENE 3 — INT. GALLEY — MIDDAY (≈ 2:00)

Lamplit, hot. Bram shirtless at the stove, towel on his shoulder, two pots and a skillet. A stew you could bathe in. Silas slides in and sits on the flour barrel. Juno is in the doorway at once, a palm of coins out. Kaito behind her, after the bread.

BRAM: Galley rule nine. Nobody sits on the flour.

Silas stands. Writes.

SILAS (SLATE): WHERE WOULD YOU LIKE ME?
BRAM: (stirring) Out.
SILAS (SLATE): I MEANT IN THE RECIPE.
BRAM: No room. It's a stew.
SILAS (SLATE): THERE IS ALWAYS ROOM IN A STEW.
JUNO: (low, to Kaito) Ten to one he gets a word out before the stew's done. Five to one it's a filthy one.
KAITO: What odds is it a normal word?
JUNO: Sweetheart, I don't take bets I can't lose.
KAITO: Why's he even bothering? Bram doesn't notice anything.
JUNO: Bram notices everything. He just doesn't give it a bowl.

Silas props the slate against the pot handle so Bram has to read it stirring.

SILAS (SLATE): I LIKE A MAN WHO KNOWS HIS WAY ROUND A LADLE.
BRAM: It's a spoon.

Silas rubs out. Writes.

SILAS (SLATE): I LIKE A MAN WHO CORRECTS ME.
BRAM: Low heat. Everything splits if you rush it.

The chalk stops. That one landed, and Bram has no idea.

SILAS (SLATE): NOW YOU'RE DOING IT ON PURPOSE.
BRAM: Doing what.
SILAS (SLATE): THAT. THE INNOCENT. IT'S DEVASTATING.

Bram tastes, adds pepper, lifts the full pot one-handed to the other ring. Silas's chalk snaps.

SFX: tk
JUNO: (a coin to Ashka) Oh, he's gone.
KAITO: Is this what it looks like? From the outside? When I—
JUNO: Yes.
KAITO: Screw that.

Silas holds up a fresh slate like a man surrendering.

SILAS (SLATE): DARLING. THE THINGS I WOULD SAY IF I COULD SAY THEM.
BRAM: Write smaller.
SILAS (SLATE): I HAVE NEVER DONE ANYTHING SMALLER IN MY LIFE.
JUNO: Lie. I've seen his boat.
SILAS (SLATE): IT WAS A FAST BOAT.
JUNO: Sweetheart, that's what they all write.

Silas opens his mouth to laugh. His throat works. A thread of sound.

Everybody freezes. Juno goes up on her toes. Bram's spoon stops.

SILAS: (a thin cracked rasp, every word a coin he can't afford) Bram. Darling. You stir that pot the way I would very much like to be—

Gone. Mouth open on the next word, nothing in it. He taps his throat. Nothing. Juno's fist goes up.

JUNO: FIVE TO ONE! Filthy! Pay the lady!
KAITO: It wasn't FINISHED!
JUNO: Intent counts, sweetheart. I heard a comma.
BRAM: (unmoved, ladling) Rule eleven. Slates are not plates.

He puts a bowl into Silas's hands. Silas looks at it, then up at Bram, and for one second the grin isn't where it should be. He writes small, no flourish.

SILAS (SLATE): THANK YOU.
BRAM: (misty, turning back to the stove) Eat it before it's cold. Cold's a crime.

Kaito gets a hand on the bread. Bram doesn't turn.

BRAM: Vegetable.
KAITO: It's BREAD.
BRAM: There's a carrot in it.
KAITO: WHY?

## SCENE 4 — EXT. THE WICK — DUSK (≈ 1:20)

Lamps being lit along the quay; the ebb starting to pull. Kaito at the far end, dropping a wood chip in by the tide gate and counting. Silas comes down the quay with Hush, bowl in hand, scraped clean.

KAITO: Go away. I'm counting.
SILAS (SLATE): COUNTING WHAT.
KAITO: The water. Top's going out. Bottom's still coming in. If you know that, you know where to stand.
SILAS (SLATE): SHE TAUGHT YOU THAT.
KAITO: Sela. Yeah. Took her a year. Took me an afternoon. (beat) And a drowning.
SILAS (SLATE): SHE THREW YOU IN?
KAITO: Grist rolled over. Same thing.
SILAS (SLATE): SHE'D HAVE THROWN YOU IN.
KAITO: She'd have thrown me in.

Silas writes, stops, rubs it out before showing it. Writes again.

SILAS (SLATE): WHY DID YOU GIVE IT BACK? THE SLATE.
KAITO: (not looking up) Your handwriting's shit. Someone had to tell you.
SILAS (SLATE): IT'S CALLED STYLE.
KAITO: It's called a seizure.

A huff of air out of Silas: a laugh with no sound in it. Kaito, caught being kind, scowls at the water.

KAITO: You could've left. In the warehouse. You had Maro, you had the door. Why come back?

Silas looks at the slate a long time. Writes small.

SILAS (SLATE): I SAID I WOULD.
KAITO: You said you were never going to.
SILAS (SLATE): SAME THING, DARLING.
KAITO: It's not, though. You wrote it and you rubbed it out. I saw.
SILAS (SLATE): THEN YOU SAW IT.
KAITO: ...Yeah.
SILAS (SLATE): SO IT WAS SAID.

Kaito almost smiles. On the deck, far down the quay, Skarn's head comes up. The fin crackles once. Hush's head turns to the warehouse roofs.

A hum. Low, mechanical. A cable's note. The lamp nearest them flickers.

Kaito's wood chip goes under and doesn't come back up.

KAITO: ...That's not the ebb.
SILT (O.S.): (flat, from above) Found you.

On the shed roof: a smooth white mask with one slit, a ragged black coat. Under him the hound, cables for veins, white core in its chest, a brass plate riveted across one side of it. The crack from Pinhold. Patched.

KAITO: (quiet) Oh, screw that.

Silas spins to the ship and opens his mouth to shout. Nothing. Again. Nothing. He swings the chain against the bollard.

SFX: CLANK

Too far. Nobody turns. He scrawls on the slate and hurls it down the quay. It stops against Grist. Grist opens one eye.

## SCENE 5 — EXT. THE WICK — DUSK (≈ 2:45)

The hound drops from the roof; the quay bounces. Silt stays in the saddle. Kaito's gauntlet lights, the fork on his hand flaring. Far down the quay Skarn is coming, flat out, and it is a long quay.

MUSIC: brass, running, lower than Pinhold; it knows more now

SILT: Pinhold boy. You've had five islands to practise.
KAITO: Six. And I drew them.
SILT: You asked me nothing, last time. You just ran at it.
KAITO: You asked ME something. "Why do you run. It's the same sea." Remember?
SILT: It is the same sea.
KAITO: Then I'll run it till I've drawn the whole damn thing.
SILT: (flat) Draw faster.

Kaito steps left, onto the tide-gate beam, where the planks are slick. Silas goes for the hound's flank with the chain-blade, Hush with him. Silt flicks two fingers. The hound's cabled tail takes Silas across the chest and puts him through a stack of crates. Hush's wind scores the planks; the hound walks through it. Silas on his back in the splinters, mouth open on a word that isn't there.

Kaito doesn't lunge. He waits. The hound's claws hit the wet beam and skid, and he's inside its reach, lightning straight up into the jaw.

SFX: KRAK-K

The hound's head snaps round. Silt doesn't move.

SILT: Better.
KAITO: I know.
SILT: Not good.

The bond-cutter comes out, hook glowing white. He leans down like a man picking up a coin; the hook catches the gauntlet and drags Kaito off his feet. A cable-vein on the hound's shoulder, hot as a stove ring, wraps his bare forearm.

SFX: hssssss
KAITO: AGH—

He kicks off the chest plate, drops. Forearm raw from wrist to elbow. He Surges: above the burn a new fork of scar lights and climbs past the elbow. Three strikes in a breath, all on the brass patch.

SFX: KRAK KRAK KRANG

The patch rings like a bell. It holds.

SILT: (flat) You aimed at the patch. Clever. We mended it.

On the Gullwake: Sela at the masthead, legs hooked in the rigging, spyglass up. Juno at the rail, gloves off, hands lit. Bram on the gangplank shouldering Grist awake, cargo sliding into the harbour.

BRAM: Up. UP. There's a cabbage in it.
JUNO: There isn't!
BRAM: He doesn't know that.
JUNO: I can't throw from here! I'll cook him too!
SELA: (calm, through the glass) Then don't. Bram, get that beetle moving. Pup. (louder) PUP! He drops the shoulder before he swings. Watch the shoulder.

Kaito hears it. Watches. Silt's left shoulder dips; Kaito's already going right. The hook rips a plank out where he was.

SILT: She's calling it for you.
KAITO: She's better than you at everything.
SILT: Most people are.

He brings the hound at Kaito from the lamp side, light full in his eyes. The hook goes high. A killing cut.

SELA: (from the mast, a shout that carries the length of the quay) PUP, LEFT!

Kaito goes left.

The hook stops.

Dead, in the air. Silt yanks the hound's cables so hard it half rears. The white hook hangs over Kaito's head, shaking. Kaito on one knee under it, gauntlet up.

Silence. The lamps hiss.

SILT: (and the flat voice is gone; what's left is young, and torn) What did she call you?

Kaito stares up at the mask.

KAITO: ...What?
SILT: (the same cracked voice, not to Kaito; to the mast) What did you—

He stops himself. The mask turns to the mast. Sela up there, pointing at the hook.

SELA: Why have you stopped? Hit him!

Kaito hits him. Full charge, into the hook. Silt lets it go. Simply lets it go. The bond-cutter spins into the harbour and goes out: the first thing we've seen him lose. The hound screams and backs, cables sparking.

Silt looks at the mast. The flat comes back down over his voice like a shutter.

SILT: Not tonight.
KAITO: (up, swaying, gauntlet smoking) What? You HAD me. You had me and you—
SILT: (turning the hound) It's the same sea, Pinhold. I'll find you on it.

The hound goes up the pilings and over the roof. Kaito stands on the quay, arm hanging, shouting at nothing.

KAITO: COME BACK AND LOSE PROPERLY!
JUNO: (from the rail) KAITO! Is it gone?
KAITO: IT'S GONE! He just— he LEFT!
JUNO: Did you win?
KAITO: (looking at his arm) ...Ask me tomorrow.

Skarn arrives at last, skids, looks at the empty roof, looks at Kaito, and sits down heavily. Disgusted with everyone.

## SCENE 6 — EXT. THE GULLWAKE, DECK — NIGHT (≈ 1:40)

Under way behind Vesh, the Wick's lamps sliding astern. Kaito on a crate, Bram wrapping the burned forearm in a cloth that stinks. Above the bandage, the new scar fork, dull now. Silas against the mast, ribs strapped, slate on his knee. Juno chalks: SILAS SPEAKS — DAY 4 — PAID (FILTHY). Sela comes down the rigging. Nobody is joking.

BRAM: Hold still. Vinegar and onion.
KAITO: It smells like a pickle.
BRAM: You're the pickle.
KAITO: (to Sela) He stopped.
SELA: I saw.
KAITO: He had me. Dead. Hook was right there. And he stopped, and he asked what you called me.
SELA: (easy) Left. I called "left." Good word. You should try it more.
KAITO: I WENT left.
SELA: You did. Well done.
KAITO: Not left. Pup. He heard "pup" and his voice went. It cracked. Like mine does.
JUNO: (quiet, for her) It did. I heard it from the rail.
SELA: (a shrug, writing) It's a word. I've always called you that.
KAITO: Since when?
SELA: Since—

The pen stops.

She looks at the page. The pen doesn't move.

SELA: Since Pinhold. "Pup!" Off the ship, the day you ran.
KAITO: Yeah. But why that word? Where'd you get it?
SELA: (lightly; too quickly) It suits you. The ears.

She writes.

INSERT: the page, her hand. "Silt stopped at 'pup.' The pup thinks it was the word. Check the books for the word." CHECK underlined twice.

Her thumb goes, unnoticed, to the old torn edge inside the cover.

Silas has watched her, not Kaito, the whole time. He writes. Shows Sela only.

SILAS (SLATE): YOU KNOW HIM.
SELA: (reading it, flat) I've never met him.

Silas looks at her a beat. Rubs it out slowly. Writes.

SILAS (SLATE): THEN HE'S MET YOU.

She takes the slate, wipes it on her sleeve, gives it back.

SELA: Go to bed, smuggler.

Juno chalks a new line.

JUNO: New book. "Who's under the mask." Ten to one it's somebody's ex.
KAITO: Whose ex looks like THAT?
JUNO: Sweetheart, you haven't met mine.
BRAM: (tying off the bandage) He'll come back.
KAITO: Good.
BRAM: No.
KAITO: He threw the cutter away. Who throws away the cutter?
JUNO: Someone with a spare.
BRAM: Someone who didn't want to be holding it.
JUNO: Evens that's worse.

Kaito looks at his arm. The new fork.

KAITO: (quiet) Three hits on the patch. It didn't crack. Last time it cracked.
SELA: Last time he wasn't ready for you.
KAITO: He wasn't ready for you either. (beat) Sela. If you knew him—
SELA: I'd have written it down.
KAITO: And if you didn't?

Sela says nothing. She closes the book on her thumb, on the tear.

## SCENE 7 — EXT. LANTERNREACH, MARSH EDGE — NIGHT (≈ 0:50)

Beyond the last lamp. Reeds, black water, warehouse roofs a row of teeth against the haze. The hound lies down like a dog, core dimming, brass patch scorched black. Silt on its back, his back to us.

No music. Reeds.

He pulls one glove off with his teeth. A bare hand, nothing remarkable about it, which is the point. From inside the coat, a page: old, soft at the folds, one edge torn ragged.

He doesn't unfold it. He holds it. Then he does, and we are behind him, and we never see what's written there. The mask tips down toward it.

The hand holding the page is shaking, only slightly. He steadies it on the hound's plating.

He folds the page. Puts it away. Glove back on. The mask lifts toward the Wick's lamps, where a ship has just gone out on the ebb.
