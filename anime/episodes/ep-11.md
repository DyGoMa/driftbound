## SCENE 1 — EXT. GAPTOOTH REACH — GREY MORNING (≈ 1:15)

Black screen. A big slow voice, gentle, choosing each word like a potato from a sack.

BRAM (V.O.): Previously. Ashmarket. Juno had a fever. Hot enough to boil a towel. I carried her into the sea, which she says was her idea, which is fine. She got better. She beat Kaito at cards. That's like beating an egg. You don't try hard; it's mostly done already. She took his bunk. We had bath night. Rule Ten. Kaito hit the mast. Then the sea shook, and the captain drew a little circle on the chart, and nobody finished their greens. (a long beat) I left out the stew. There was a stew. It was good.

Fade up. Mist. Two chains of black rock islands, spiked like teeth, slide past each other at walking pace. Between them a small trading sloop sits where two faces are meeting.

SFX: a long low grind, like a door the size of a town

The sloop folds, slowly, like a paper box being shut. A rowboat pulls clear, three men heaving at the oars.

On the Gullwake's rail: Kaito (21, goggles on his forehead, appalled). Juno (23, Ashka on her shoulder, gloves on, a wet cloth still round her neck from the fever). Bram (31, huge, shirtless in his dungarees, towel on his shoulder, misty-eyed).

KAITO: That was a whole ship.
JUNO: That was nine to one. Somebody on this boat owes me a silver.
BRAM: She had a good stove.

The rowboat passes under the bow. The front rower (forties) twists round to bellow up.

ROWER: IT'S SHUT! TURN ROUND, YOU MAD BASTARDS!

Sela (28, cropped blue navigator's jacket, silver-blue ponytail, tin mug in one hand, worn logbook under her arm) calls down the way you'd thank a stranger for the time.

SELA: Thank you. Keep left. The water's about to change its mind.

The rowboat spins on nothing and shoots backward the way it came, oars in the air.

SFX: FWOOSH
ROWER: WHAT DO YOU MEAN, CHANGE ITS—

Gone. Kaito stares at the closing rocks. Sela sips her tea.

KAITO: That's the only way east, and it eats ships?
SELA: It used to chew. Slowly. You could plan around chewing.

Far off on the northern chain, a thin grey needle in the mist: a Great Pylon, the line-through-circle at its crown. The rocks around it are dead still. The southern chain grinds past.

SELA: Then they nailed the north chain down. The south hasn't heard.
JUNO: Give it a week, it's a wall.
KAITO: So how the hell do we get through that?

Sela walks out along the bowsprit and raises her mug like a toast.

SELA: Nicely.

## SCENE 2 — EXT. THE GULLWAKE, DECK — MORNING (≈ 1:15)

Bram lashes pots to the galley rail. Grist, the truck-sized iron beetle, sleeps across the main hatch. Kaito holds up an ancient chart, every rock named for a tooth, the corner bitten.

KAITO: The Molars. The Canines. The Wisdom. Who names a sea after teeth?
SELA: The first man to chart it was a ship's dentist.
JUNO: (on a barrel, checking her gloves) He get through?
SELA: (passing, not breaking stride) Most of him.
KAITO: (finger on the biggest tooth, a dark crooked rock) What's the Wisdom?
SELA: Comes in late, hurts everyone, and you generally end up losing it.

Juno at the mast with chalk. Above her, from last week, KAITO AND WHO? Beneath it: GULLWAKE MAKES IT THROUGH — 40 TO 1.

JUNO: Forty to one we make it through. Takers? No? Sensible.
SELA: (O.S., from the bow) Put me down for a silver.

Juno's chalk stops. She looks at the bow. Writes it.

KAITO: (looking at empty sea) What do I do? And where's Vesh?
SELA: Hold on to something. Your tongue, if you can find it.

She taps the deck once with her boot. The water lights from below, teal and gold: lantern lures rising one after another, far longer than the ship. Vesh's frilled head breaks the surface, jaw harness taut on the towline.

SFX: hhhhOOOOOM
SELA: (soft, to the water) Morning, you great lump.

Kaito's mouth hangs open. Ashka dives inside Juno's collar, tail feathers out. Juno keeps chalking.

JUNO: Still forty to one. Bigger just means more to sink.

Skarn flattens his ears and turns his back on the glowing serpent with enormous dignity.

## SCENE 3 — EXT. THE MOLARS — CONTINUOUS (≈ 2:15)

Into the Reach: a corridor of rock, left wall sliding forward, right wall sliding back. Sela sits cross-legged on the bow capstan, writing, not looking up. Bram at the wheel.

SFX: GRRRRRNNN
MUSIC: low strings, patient, a held breath

SELA: Bram. Two points to port.
BRAM: Now?
SELA: (still writing) In a moment. Finish your thought.

Her pen moves. Her head tilts, listening to something far off.

SELA: ...Now.

He turns. A spur of rock the size of a chapel sweeps through where the bow just was, an arm's length from her face. Without looking, Sela moves her mug to the other side of the rail.

Kaito clings to the shrouds. Skarn crouches under him, eyes squeezed shut.

KAITO: YOU'RE NOT EVEN LOOKING!
SELA: I've seen rocks.
JUNO: (braced on the mast, narrow-eyed, a cheat sizing up someone better) Bram. Does she ever get one wrong?
BRAM: Not one she remembers.

Ahead, two enormous rock faces lean together and fill the sky. Between them, a narrow diagonal slash of daylight.

KAITO: SELA. Wall. That's a WALL. Where's the way through?!

Sela looks up at last. Closes the logbook on one finger. Below her left collarbone the whirlpool mark glows teal.

SELA: There. It's on a slant, so we go through on a slant.

Vesh swerves hard; the towline shrieks. The walls shed boulders. Spray across the deck.

SFX: KRA-KOOM

Kaito drops from the rigging; a crack of lightning from his gauntlet smacks a falling rock away from Bram's head. He lands grinning.

KAITO: GOT IT!
SELA: Thank you, pup. Now stop helping. (to Bram, calm in the falling spray) Bram. What does Grist weigh?
BRAM: (seriously) Before lunch?
SELA: I need us on our side. Put him on the port rail.
BRAM: He's asleep.

Grist, a snore bubble at his horn. A boulder bounces off his shell.

SFX: zzzRRRNK. BONK.

He does not stir. Bram steps away from the wheel and shoves it into Kaito's hands. Kaito is yanked off his feet.

BRAM: Hold this.
KAITO: I DON'T KNOW HOW!
BRAM: Same as soup. Keep it moving.

Bram lifts a single cabbage from a crate with both hands, tenderly, like a puppy.

BRAM: That was for supper.
SELA: (O.S.) Bram.

He walks to the port rail, cabbage out at arm's length. Kaito swings off the spinning wheel, legs flying.

KAITO: WHAT'S THE CABBAGE FOR? BRAM! WHAT IS THE CABBAGE FOR?!

Grist's eye opens. One eye. It finds the cabbage.

SFX: six iron legs hitting the deck like dropped anvils

The ship heels. Wide, and hold it: Vesh in full, flung ahead in a vast arc between the walls, every lure blazing. The Gullwake follows heeled hard over through the slot, Grist leaning after the cabbage, Kaito dangling from the wheel. Sela stands easy on the high rail, eyes closed, two fingers on her mark.

SFX: SKRRRRRRRRRRRK
KAITO: AAAAAAAAAGH
MUSIC: the strings break open into brass; the show's theme, earned

JUNO: (hanging off the mast, almost reverent) She's counting cards. The whole damn ocean, and she's counting cards.

## SCENE 4 — EXT. THE BASIN — LATE MORNING (≈ 1:15)

A still, misty basin inside the Reach, ringed with rock. The Gullwake floats safe and listing, because Grist has fallen asleep on the port rail with half a cabbage in his mouth.

SFX: crnch... crnch...

Juno walks uphill across the slanted deck, counting coins out one at a time, each a small agony.

JUNO: Thirty-eight. Thirty-nine. Forty. You're a witch.

Sela closes Juno's gloved hand back over the coins without looking at them.

SELA: Keep it. That was the Molars.
JUNO: ...Meaning what?

Sela points her spyglass at the far wall, where a thin black crack runs up the rock, narrow as a knife cut.

SELA: The bet was "through." That's the Gap. Slack water's at dawn.
JUNO: There's MORE?

Kaito, hair standing straight up, pries his fingers off the wheel one at a time.

KAITO: Did we just tip the ship with a cabbage?
BRAM: Vegetable.

Later. Sela on the rail with her logbook. Kaito flops down beside her, honestly curious.

KAITO: How do you do that? You weren't even looking.
SELA: Shut your eyes in your own kitchen. You still know where the table is.
KAITO: I don't have a kitchen. And I walked into the table in Bram's.
SELA: (writing) I know. I've seen you with furniture.

She closes her eyes. Two fingers on the mark.

SELA: Vesh feels the water push on everything in it. I get what Vesh gets. Bad tooth behind you, four hundred yards. Crept a hand since breakfast. Wreck below us.

Kaito checks with her spyglass. The tooth. A dark hull in green water.

SELA: And Juno's got an ace in her left boot.

Across the deck, Juno freezes mid-step, one boot in the air.

KAITO: You can feel THAT in the water?
SELA: No. She limps.

Kaito turns to her, eager and a little shy, his failed chart rolled in his fist.

KAITO: Could you teach me?

Sela opens one eye at him. Delighted.

SELA: Oh, pup. I thought you'd never ask.

## SCENE 5 — EXT. DECK, PORT RAIL — AFTERNOON (≈ 1:45)

The low rail, deck tilted, Grist snoring beside them. Kaito kneels with his hand in the water. Sela perches on a coil of rope, logbook on her knee.

SELA: Hand in the water.
KAITO: It's wet.
SELA: (writing, perfectly serious) Lesson one. Student confirms the sea.
KAITO: What am I meant to feel?
SELA: Picture the Reach as two people.
KAITO: (suspicious) What people?
SELA: Big ones. Slow ones. Rubbing up against each other for nine hundred years.

The tips of Kaito's ears go red. Just the tips. Sela watches them with professional interest.

SELA: And neither one willing to be the first to stop.

Juno drags a crate over, a bowl of roasted nuts in her lap like it's the theatre.

JUNO: Oh, I'm getting a seat for this. (settling) Thought it was going to be the large woman turning over in bed.
SELA: That's the Wisdom. Different lesson.
JUNO: Can't wait.
SELA: (to Kaito, one hand making a slow narrowing gesture) Now. Where they get closest, it gets very tight.
KAITO: ...Tight.
SELA: And all that pressure has to go somewhere.
JUNO: (low, to Ashka, six fingers up) Six to one he asks where.
KAITO: (small voice, ears fully red) ...Where?

Ashka, disgusted, drops a silver coin into Juno's palm.

SFX: tink
JUNO: Thank you.
KAITO: (desperate) Is this still about water?
SELA: It has only ever been about water, pup. What's it about for you?
KAITO: WATER! It's about water! I'm feeling SO much water!
BRAM: (passing behind them with a pot, in total sincerity) You're rushing it. Low heat, or it splits.
KAITO: NOT YOU TOO!

Sela leans in beside him, amused, low. His knees are sliding on the tilted deck.

SELA: Lean out. Nice and slow. Now tell me: which way does it want to go?

Grist shifts in his sleep. The deck lurches. Kaito goes head first over the rail, still pointing.

SFX: zzRRNK
KAITO: IT WANTS TO GO LEF—
SFX: SPLOOSH

Sela does not look up from the logbook. Skarn looks at the empty rail. Pure disgust.

Juno at the mast, adding a name under KAITO AND WHO?, beneath SELA and THE MAST: THE SEA — 5 TO 2.

JUNO: He went in willingly. Odds shorten.
SELA (V.O.): (writing) Lesson one. Student went in too fast. They always do.

CUT TO:

UNDERWATER. Green light. Kaito sinking, twisted: hair and jacket stream one way; his boots are dragged the other. His eyes go wide.

KAITO: (bubbles) My boots are going the other way.

His open hand: the current slides over the top one way and underneath the other. He bursts up, hair plastered flat, a huge stupid grin.

KAITO: IT GOES BOTH WAYS!
JUNO: (chin on her fist at the rail) Sweetheart. I could've told you that.

The undercurrent drags him backward across the basin, still shouting. Without looking up:

SELA: Vesh.

Vesh's head rises beneath him and lifts him dripping into the air among the lures.

KAITO: TOP GOES EAST! BOTTOM GOES WEST! IT'S TWO SEAS!

Sela stops writing and looks up at him. A real smile. Not a teasing one.

SELA: That took me a year.
KAITO: (sliding down Vesh's neck onto the deck in a heap) A year? Who taught you?
SELA: Mag Tolliver. Pilot at Sorrow Point. Seventy, hands like oars. Threw me in forty-one times.

She gazes into the mist, fond, thumb on the closed logbook. She doesn't open it.

SELA: Best year of my life. That one I don't need the book for.
KAITO: (scrambling across the deck to his chart board, and finding Grist's iron foot planted on the sheet) My chart. Where's my— GRIST! Get off it, you bucket!

## SCENE 6 — EXT. DECK, CHART BOARD — AFTERNOON INTO DUSK (≈ 0:45)

Kaito, drying off, inks a rock onto the blank sheet with a proud flourish and letters it LITTLE MOLAR.

KAITO: There. Little Molar. Right forever.

He looks up. The real Little Molar is a hand's width further along than where he drew it. It looks smug, for a rock.

KAITO: ...You moved.

Dusk. The same sheet: Little Molar drawn six times, each crossed out. Ink on Kaito's face.

JUNO: Eight to one it's the rock's fault.
KAITO: STAY STILL, you lumpy son of a bitch!

Down at the sheet: six crossed-out Little Molars in a neat row. Together they make a line. Kaito's pen hand has stopped.

He draws one long line through all six, ticked at each bell. Then another, for another rock. Parallel ticked lines: a little like ruled music paper. Nobody says so.

KAITO: ...Huh.

Night. He runs down the passage with the sheet to the chart room door, ajar, lamplight inside.

KAITO: Sela! You up? You've got to see this, it's—

## SCENE 7 — INT. CHART ROOM — NIGHT (≈ 1:15)

From the doorway: Kaito stops dead. Sela, at the chart table under the lamp, has not heard him. For once she is not a step ahead of anyone.

No music.

No teasing in her face. Finger on a line of the open logbook, lips moving as she reads, like someone learning a part.

INSERT: the page, in her own hand, a margin note underlined twice in older ink. "FAVOURITE FOOD: eel pie. Too much black pepper, the way Bram does it. You'll say chowder, to be polite. You hate chowder. Stop it."

She looks up. Sees him. Closes the logbook without any hurry. The teasing comes back into her face like a lamp being turned up.

SELA: You've got ink on your nose, pup.
KAITO: (pointing at the closed book) That said favourite food.
SELA: It did.

She slides the logbook aside and squares it with the table's edge.

SELA: Bram's doing my favourite tonight. Last hot meal before the Gap. It'd be rude to get it wrong.
KAITO: How do you get it wrong? It's your favourite.

She looks at him. A long, quiet beat. Nothing on her face but something careful. No words.

Then she holds out her hand for the sheet, brisk and warm again, moving them both on.

SELA: What did you bring me?

The sheet under the lamp: no coastlines, only ruled lines, tick marks, tiny rock names. Sela leans over it. Kaito bounces.

SELA: This isn't a map.
KAITO: I know!
SELA: It doesn't say where anything is.
KAITO: It kept leaving! So I drew it leaving.

Her fingertip traces one line slowly across the sheet, head tilted, as if listening. Silence.

She opens the logbook and writes, hiding a smile. Kaito cranes to read it upside down.

SELA (V.O.): (writing) The pup has drawn a thing that should not work. Checked it twice. Nobody tell him.

## SCENE 8 — INT. GALLEY — NIGHT (≈ 1:00)

Lamplit, warm, everything at a slight slant; Grist is still on the rail. Bram, shirtless at the stove, draws a huge golden pie from the oven. Juno at the wedged table.

JUNO: Bram, sweetheart. Put a shirt on or put a price on it.
BRAM: Shirts catch fire.
JUNO: So do I.

Bram sets a plate heaped with boiled greens in front of Kaito, who recoils.

BRAM: New galley rule. Anyone who fell in today eats greens first.
KAITO: Screw that. You just made that up.
BRAM: Yes.
KAITO: (pointing out the porthole at Grist) He eats one cabbage and he's a hero. I found out the sea has TWO LAYERS—
BRAM: Vegetable.

Background: the pepper pot slides down the slanted table. Juno's gloved finger nudges it back uphill without her looking.

Bram sets the eel pie in front of Sela with both hands, misty-eyed. Across the table Kaito, fork of greens halfway up, watches Sela and not the pie.

BRAM: Eel. Too much pepper. Captain's favourite. (a beat) Well? Is it still?

Sela takes the first bite. Her eyebrows lift: honest, pleased surprise, like someone tasting a thing for the first time.

SELA: Oh. That's good.
BRAM: (laughing, wiping one eye with his towel) You say that every time.

The table laughing: Juno slapping it, Bram beaming, Sela on her second bite. Only Kaito is still, looking at Sela.

## SCENE 9 — EXT. THE GAP — DAWN (≈ 1:00)

Slack water. Thick mist. The Gullwake slides toward two walls that almost touch. Vesh's lures glow faint ahead. Bram at the wheel; Grist awake, grumpy, cabbage-less.

SELA (V.O.): (writing) Slack water. Forty to one, and I'd still take it.

Kaito at the bow beside Sela, his new chart board under his arm, trying to find the words. She watches the mist, mug in hand, not turning.

KAITO: Last night. The book. How long have you been—
SELA: Which way's the water going, pup?
KAITO: ...East on top. West underneath.
SELA: Good. Ask me after the Gap.

Her mug stops halfway to her mouth. Her face goes still and sharp, no joke in it at all. For the first time this episode she is a beat late.

SELA: ...That's not rock.

A barbed harpoon streaks out of the mist into the mast, straight through the chalked betting pool, trailing a steel cable. Chalk dust bursts.

SFX: THUNK
JUNO: That's my BOOK, you bastards!

Three more.

SFX: SHKK SHKK SHKK

One sinks into Vesh's shoulder. The cable snaps taut and Vesh rears, screaming. Sela folds forward gripping the rail, her mark flaring, the mug falling. Kaito grabs her arm.

KAITO: VESH! What the hell is out there?!

The mist tears open. A low grey Sever cutter, harpoon guns smoking. On its deck a chained Hollow bear, ash-grey, plated, blank white eyes, a white core in its chest. Holding the chain at the rail, at ease: Captain Hekka Dorn (33, woman, 193 cm, light brown skin, thick wavy black hair swept back, grey plate armour, bare midriff, a bond-cutter lance taller than she is on her shoulder like a fishing rod).

HEKKA: Best driving I ever saw, Captain. Seemed rude to interrupt.

She lifts the lance off her shoulder. Its hook glows white.

HEKKA: Hold the serpent still. It's only a count of ten.

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.
