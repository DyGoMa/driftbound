## COLD OPEN

## SCENE 1 — INT. GULLWAKE GALLEY (V.O.) / INT. GULLWAKE BELOW DECK / EXT. GULLWAKE DECK — DAY (≈ 1:10)

Black. A wooden spoon taps a pot rim, twice.

BRAM (V.O.): Last time. Pinhold got nailed down. That's an island. They put a pin through it like a moth.

Clips under him: the pylon finishing; the flat harbour; a barred door; Kaito signing the roster.

BRAM (V.O.): Kaito's neighbours shut the door on him, so he came to live with us. Signed the book under "deckhand." Spelled it wrong.
BRAM (V.O.): Sela wrote something in her logbook and went quiet. Which is like when a stew goes quiet. Means something's at the bottom.
BRAM (V.O.): Also there's no uniform. I've said.

Clip: a white room, a chart, one black line. Bram pauses.

BRAM (V.O.): And somewhere a man drew a line on a map. One line. (beat) That's it. That's the recap. Eat something.

SMASH CUT:

Below deck. A chain as thick as a leg jumps in its housing, bangs, and stops. Kaito, upside down in the bilge with a lantern, takes a spray of rust in the face.

KAITO: It's fine!
SELA (O.S.): It made a noise, pup.
KAITO: Chains make noises. That's the noise a chain makes.
SELA (O.S.): It made the noise. Then it stopped making it.

Kaito looks at the chain. The chain does nothing, which is worse.

CUT TO:

The deck. Sela at the rail, logbook open on her forearm. Under the bow, Vesh's lantern-ridge glides, towing; the chain hangs slack as a washing line. Grist is asleep on the hatch Kaito needs. Kaito's head appears beside a beetle leg, squeezing past.

KAITO: The tow housing's cracked. Vesh is dragging us by habit.
SELA: (writing) "Vesh is dragging us by habit." Good. How much to fix?
KAITO: Parts. Bram says a forge-welded link and a new housing. Real money.
SELA: And the ship's purse holds...
BRAM: (from the galley hatch) Eleven eggs.
KAITO: Nine. I had two.
SELA: (writing) Nine eggs.

She lifts the spyglass. In the glass: a black crag with a red seam of heat down one flank, every ledge crusted with timber halls and paper lanterns, soot drifting up off it like snow going the wrong way.

SELA: Ashmarket. The city that never asks how you'll pay. (snaps the glass shut) We'll fit right in.
KAITO: It looks like it's on fire.
SELA: Only parts of it. Those are the good parts.

SFX: the tow chain banging once more, then quiet
MUSIC: a hot brass sting into the opening

## PART A

## SCENE 2 — EXT. ASHMARKET — LOWER TERRACES — DUSK (≈ 1:00)

The Gullwake moored at a pier bolted into raw volcanic rock. Above: stairs, rope bridges, gaming halls stacked on gaming halls, steam leaking from street vents, lanterns in every colour. Gamblers, touts, a man with a cage of singing birds. Soot settles on everything.

Kaito looks up with the grin of a man who has walked into trouble and likes it. Skarn sneezes soot.

BRAM: (the cracked housing on one shoulder like a loaf) Chandler's on the second terrace. I'll show him what we need and what we've got.
KAITO: We've got eggs.
BRAM: Eggs are a start. Everything's a start. (to Grist) Come on. (Grist does not come on) ...Come on.

Grist rises one leg at a time and follows, taking a lantern post with him.

SELA: Pup. With me. We're going to find money.
KAITO: We don't have money.
SELA: I said find. Not ours.

She sets off. Kaito looks at Skarn. Skarn lies down on the pier with the air of a wolf not involved in this.

KAITO: Fine. Stay. Judge.

He jogs after her and steps straight into a steam vent. Hiss. Yelp. He hops out with one boot smoking.

SELA: (not looking back) Mind the vents.
KAITO: Thanks.

## SCENE 3 — INT. THE SLAG BUCKET, A GAMING BAR — NIGHT (≈ 1:30)

Low ceiling, black timber, a long bar, dice and card tables, a brass heat-pipe along one wall that people warm their hands on. A chart of the Drift on the wall, old and hopeful.

Kaito stops dead at the chart.

KAITO: This chart's wrong.
SELA: Most are.
KAITO: No, it's wrong wrong. Ashmarket's forty miles from where it says. (a slow grin) Good.
SELA: Good?
KAITO: Means this place still moves.

She looks at him one beat longer than she needs to, then steers him by the collar towards the corner, where the noise is.

A card table. One side: a Sever officer, grey coat, the black line-through-a-circle on his chest, helmet off: LIEUTENANT GARROW (34), fair, sweating, most of his chips gone. Two troopers behind him, visors down.

Other side: JUNO CALLOWAY (23). Copper braid over one shoulder, white cropped tank, open bomber with a fur collar, fingerless gloves, boots up on a spare chair. On her shoulder, ASHKA, a fire raptor the size of a big eagle, ember-red and black, trailing tiny sparks onto the sawdust. Juno's grin is the loudest thing in the room.

Beside the table, a slim one-armed man with a chalk slate: NIM HADDA (30), who runs the book.

JUNO: Twenty says you fold, Lieutenant.
GARROW: I don't take bets from the person I'm playing.
JUNO: Thirty, then. You look like you need it.

The crowd laughs. Garrow lays his hand down: three sevens. Smug.

GARROW: The Empire's luck.
JUNO: Sweetheart, the Empire doesn't have luck. It has a budget.

She fans her cards face up with a gloved hand. Four nines.

The room goes up. Nim's chalk squeaks: he wipes a whole column clean. Garrow's face goes the colour of the lanterns.

GARROW: Four nines. There were two nines in the burn pile.
JUNO: Were there? You're very good at remembering cards you didn't get.
GARROW: (standing) Gloves off. House rule in every card room on the Drift. Show your hands.

The room goes quiet. Even Ashka stops grooming. Juno's smile doesn't move, but it stops being a smile.

NIM: (mild) Not this house. Juno keeps her gloves. Been the rule since before you had a coat.
GARROW: Who the hell are you?
NIM: The bank. Which you owe. Forty-two.

Garrow looks to the troopers. The troopers look at a bar full of gamblers who have stopped pretending to look elsewhere.

GARROW: (collecting his helmet) The Warden will hear about this.
JUNO: Tell him the odds he gets his money back. I'll write them down for you. Nim?
NIM: (chalking) Long.

Garrow goes. The troopers follow. The door bangs. Juno sweeps her winnings into her jacket without counting, and only then lets a breath out.

At the bar, Sela is already writing. Kaito leans in and whispers, far too loud.

KAITO: She cheated.
SELA: Beautifully.
KAITO: You saw it?
SELA: No. That's how I know it was beautiful.
JUNO: (without turning round) Who's the one with the ears?

Kaito's ears go red. First.

## SCENE 4 — INT. THE SLAG BUCKET — CONTINUOUS (≈ 2:30)

Juno turns in her chair and looks Kaito up and down once, like a horse she's thinking of not buying.

JUNO: You said cheated.
KAITO: You palmed a card. Somewhere. Under the... (he gestures at the whole table) ...all of it.
JUNO: Prove it.
KAITO: I can't. But I know.
JUNO: (delighted) Oh, he knows. Nim, what's the line on "he knows"?
NIM: Short.
KAITO: Bet you can't do it to me.

A pause. Sela stops writing.

SELA: Pup.
KAITO: Bet you anything. You can't pull that on me. I run with a storm wolf. I've got reflexes like...
JUNO: Ten to one you can't last ten seconds against me.
KAITO: Ten seconds of what?
JUNO: Steady. Ashmarket's game. (she flicks a coin up; Ashka snaps at it and misses on purpose) Coin on the back of your hand, arm straight out. I get ten seconds. I don't touch you. Coin stays on, you win. Coin drops, you pay the stake.
KAITO: That's it?
JUNO: That's it.
KAITO: That's nothing. That's standing still.
JUNO: (standing, stretching) Then stand still, sweetheart. What are you staking?
KAITO: (patting pockets; nine eggs' worth of nothing) I've got...
SELA: He's got boots.
KAITO: Hey!
SELA: (writing) Boots, jacket, shirt. In that order. If it goes that way.
KAITO: Whose side are you on?
SELA: The ship's. We need the parts. If you win, she pays in parts.
JUNO: (to Nim) Can I pay in parts?
NIM: You can pay in anything. You just never do.

The bar rearranges itself around them like a tide. Nim's slate fills: SECONDS. Somebody bets "two." Somebody bets "he faints."

Kaito plants his boots, right arm straight out, a copper coin balanced on the back of his gauntlet hand, over the lightning mark. Juno circles him once. Ashka hops to a chair-back to watch.

NIM: Ten.

Juno leans in close to his ear. We stay on his face.

JUNO: (low) That's a nice gauntlet.
KAITO: It's a gauntlet.
JUNO: Mm. You polish it yourself?
KAITO: (ears going) ...Sometimes.
JUNO: Every night?
KAITO: Th... that's not...

The coin slides off and rings on the floor. The bar roars.

NIM: Four.
KAITO: She said gauntlet! That's not even... Sela, is that even...
SELA: Boots.

He sits on the floor and drags his boots off, furious, and stands up in his socks. One sock has a hole in it.

JUNO: Oh, that's a bad sock. Nim, side bet on the sock.
NIM: No.

Second round. Coin on the hand. Juno doesn't lean in this time. She stands square in front of him, arms folded, and just looks at him. Three seconds. Five.

KAITO: (through his teeth) This is fine. This is nothing.
JUNO: (quiet) You're doing well.
KAITO: I know.
JUNO: Shame about the eggs.
KAITO: What about the eggs?
JUNO: Your captain told the whole bar your ship is worth nine eggs.
KAITO: Nine's a lot of eggs!

The coin drops. Chalk squeaks.

NIM: Seven.
SELA: Jacket.
KAITO: I'm going to kill both of you.

He unties the red jacket from his waist and throws it at Sela, who catches it without looking and folds it.

Third round. He's shaking now, from anger, which is worse for a coin than anything Juno could say. She knows it. She says nothing for six seconds. Then:

JUNO: Ten to one you're thinking about me.
KAITO: I'm thinking about charts!
JUNO: That's worse, sweetheart.

Clink. Roar.

NIM: Six. That's a shirt.

CUT TO: Skarn, out on the pier, head lifting at the roar rolling down the hill. His face: disgust, in advance.

Back inside. Kaito in the black zip top, hands on the zip, glaring.

KAITO: No.
JUNO: House rule.
KAITO: I'm not... there's a whole bar...
JUNO: Nine to one you've got a whole bar's worth to be proud of. Who's in? Nim, open it.
NIM: (chalking, with great weariness) "Proud of."

Sela leans on the bar, chin in her hand, having the best night of her month.

SELA: Pup. We need the parts.
KAITO: How does my shirt get us parts?
SELA: It doesn't. I just want to see what she does with it.

Kaito grabs the hem.

CUT TO: Nim's slate, chalk squeaking under "Proud of." The bar roars like a goal went in.

CUT TO: Kaito, arms folded. Medium shot, eye level: a furious young man in cargo pants and one holed sock, lightning scars branching hand to elbow, ears a colour with no name. The top lands on Juno.

Juno sniffs it, makes a face, and hangs it off Ashka's beak. Ashka drops it.

JUNO: (to the room) A round for the ears!

[EYECATCH: a Dicemite, a beetle the size and shape of a six-sided die, trundles the whole length of the Slag Bucket's bar, falls off the end, and lands showing a one.]

## PART B

## SCENE 5 — INT. THE SLAG BUCKET — LATER (≈ 1:30)

Quieter. The crowd has drifted to the dice. Kaito at the bar in his red jacket, zipped to the throat for once, with a mug he didn't pay for. Sela on the next stool, logbook open. Juno behind the bar as if she owns it, which she doesn't.

KAITO: I want a rematch.
JUNO: With what?
KAITO: ...I'll bet.
SELA: He has nothing.
KAITO: I'll owe you.
JUNO: (a big laugh) He'll owe me! Everybody in this room owes me, sweetheart, and I owe everybody else. That's the whole city. It's a wheel. (she reaches across, quick as the card trick, and lifts the brass goggles off his forehead) Deposit.
KAITO: Hey!
JUNO: Against the rematch. Win them back.
KAITO: I need those. I fly with those.
JUNO: Then fly carefully.

She pushes them up into her hair. They look good on her, and everyone at the bar can see it. Kaito's face finds a new colour and he studies the chart on the wall instead.

Sela, writing, doesn't look up.

SELA: She'll give them back.
JUNO: Will I?
SELA: You hold your markers. You don't collect them. Everyone in here would have paid you by now if you'd asked. You haven't asked.
JUNO: (a beat; then, lightly) Three to one you're trouble.
SELA: I'm the captain. It's a professional qualification. (she turns the logbook so Juno can read the heading) Sela Vey. The Gullwake. We need a forge-welded link and a chain housing, we have no money, and I like you.
JUNO: That's a lot to put in writing.
SELA: I put everything in writing. I'd forget my own name otherwise.
JUNO: (grinning) You don't look the type.
SELA: (pleasant) No. I don't.

Kaito glances at her. He heard something in that. He doesn't know what.

JUNO: (pouring herself one) Pyre, the chandler, second terrace. He owes Nim, Nim owes me, so he'll do you a housing for a song if I sing it. The link's harder. The forge is Sever-leased now. (beat) Everything is, slowly.
KAITO: Since when?
JUNO: Since a Warden called Krell came to sit on the hill with forty shields and told us it was for our safety. It's a gambling town. We'll bet on anything, including that he goes away.
SELA: (writing) "Krell."

The door. Bram comes in sideways, the cracked housing still on his shoulder, and stops at the sight of Kaito.

BRAM: Where's your shirt?
KAITO: Ask her.
BRAM: (looks at Juno; at the goggles in her hair; back at Kaito) Hm. (he sets the housing on the bar) Chandler wants silver. I offered eggs. He was kind about it.
JUNO: Pyre's always kind. It's how he gets you.
BRAM: (to Kaito, gentle, final) Rule six. You don't stake anything I'd have to wash.

## SCENE 6 — EXT. THE SLAG BUCKET — CINDER TERRACE — NIGHT (≈ 1:20)

Out the back: a plank terrace over the drop, the whole city stepping down in fire-coloured light to a black sea. Kaito leans on the rail. Juno comes out with two mugs, his goggles still on her head. Ashka lands on the rail. Then Skarn's head rises over the terrace edge; he has climbed the crag out of pure suspicion. Ashka fluffs. Skarn growls, very low. Nobody is impressed by anybody.

JUNO: That's yours?
KAITO: Skarn. He hates everyone. (Skarn looks at Juno; looks at Kaito; lies down with a sigh) Huh. Usually it takes longer.
JUNO: He's got taste.

She drinks. She warms her gloved hands on the mug the way people warmed theirs on the heat-pipe inside, though the night is warm.

KAITO: Why'd the room go quiet? When he said gloves.
JUNO: (easy) Because he was being rude. It's a nice room.
KAITO: You could've just shown him. You weren't holding anything.
JUNO: Odds you let this go?
KAITO: (honest) Bad.
JUNO: (a laugh, but a smaller one) They're my gloves, sweetheart. They come off when they come off.

Beat. She looks out at the city, not at him.

JUNO: You've got your own. (a nod at his right arm) That's not a tattoo.
KAITO: Surge scars. Every big one burns another fork up. Hand to elbow, so far.
JUNO: So far.
KAITO: It only hurts when it happens. And after. And when it rains.
JUNO: (quiet) Yeah. (then brighter, turning) Five to one you've never once been cold in your life.
KAITO: What's that supposed to...
JUNO: Nothing. (she slides the goggles down, looks through them at the lanterns, pushes them back up) Everything's gold through these.
KAITO: That's the soot.

She laughs properly at that, and he nearly does, and for a second they're two people on a terrace. Then Skarn's head comes up. Ashka's too. Both looking down the hill.

SFX: a low, even drumbeat far below, coming up through the planks

Kaito feels it in the rail.

KAITO: What's that?
JUNO: (not moving) That's him going away.

## SCENE 7 — INT. THE SLAG BUCKET — NIGHT (≈ 1:40)

The front doors bang open. The dice stop.

Shields first: a wall of grey riot shields as tall as a man, edge to edge, coming in in step. The last two have to step around Grist, asleep across the threshold, who does not wake. Behind them, WARDEN KRELL (41): 195 centimetres, built like the shields, grey coat strained across the shoulders, a jaw you could break stone on. Lieutenant Garrow at his elbow, trying not to look pleased. Troopers fan along the walls.

SFX: boots, together

KRELL: (parade ground) NOBODY MOVES, NOBODY DEALS, NOBODY BREATHES UNLESS I SEE THEM DO IT. This is a licensed house under the Warden's peace. I am the Warden. I am not at peace.

Juno and Kaito push in from the back. Bram, without seeming to, is already between the shields and Sela. Sela stays on her stool. Nim sets his chalk down.

KRELL: Calloway.
JUNO: Warden. Buy you a drink? Ten to one you say no.
KRELL: I don't drink. I collect. (he snaps his fingers; Garrow hands him a leather folder) Juno Calloway. Marker to the Cinder Palace, two hundred and six. Marker to Pyre the chandler, thirty. Marker to the Ashmarket forge, ninety. Marker to the Slag Bucket, this fine establishment, eighteen and a bar tab.

Each paper carries a red wax seal. He reads them like charges.

JUNO: (light) You've been reading my post.
KRELL: I've been BUYING it. The Sever has purchased every marker on this hill with your name on it. At face. In Empire silver. This afternoon, while you were taking the shirt off a deckhand who can't afford shoes.
KAITO: I have shoes.

Krell looks at him. Looks at the socks. Kaito looks down. The bar does not laugh; the shields are too close.

KRELL: (to Juno) Your paper reads the Sever now. Every debt you had, you have to me. (he drops the folder on the card table; the papers slide, and every one, pressed into the red wax, carries a straight black line through a circle) And I am not a patient bank.

Nim, quietly, picks one paper up, reads it, puts it down.

NIM: That's legal.
KRELL: It's stamped. Same thing on this hill.
JUNO: (and the grin is all the way back, and it is armour) What are the odds, Warden?
KRELL: On what?
JUNO: That I pay.

Krell leans down. Ashka hisses; sparks tick on the floorboards.

KRELL: (not shouting now, which is worse) I don't bet, Calloway. I shield. You ever seen a shield line take a street? Nothing goes through it. Nothing goes round it. It never, ever gets tired. (he straightens) The Palace will give you terms. Take them.

He turns. The shields part, step, close, and they are gone, the drumbeat going down the hill. The door hangs open. Soot blows in. Grist sleeps on.

Silence. Sela closes the logbook, but not before we see the one line under the day's heading, in her neat hand:

INSERT — LOGBOOK: "Ashmarket. Pup lost his shirt to a woman who won't lose her gloves. Then the Sever bought her. We are not leaving."

Kaito, in his socks, looks at Juno. Juno looks at the folder on the table. Then she pulls his goggles down over her eyes so nobody can see them, and picks up the deck.

JUNO: (shuffling) Right. Who's playing?

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.
