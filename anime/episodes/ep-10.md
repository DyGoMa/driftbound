## SCENE 1 — INT. THE GULLWAKE, GALLEY — NIGHT (≈ 1:15)

Black screen. A woman's voice, thick, slow, a little slurred.

JUNO (V.O.): Previously. Odds I've got this right: four to one against, so bet accordingly. Ashmarket. Warden Krell bought my marker. We went to the Cinder Palace in fancy clothes, I won, he called me a cheat, which, fair. Then there were shields. Lots of shields. Can't burn a shield. Can burn the city, a bit. Then I got very hot and the big man carried me into the sea, which was my idea, and nobody is taking that from me. Then I slept. I think I'm still... (a beat) Is this the recap? Who's listening to this?

Fade up. The galley, one lamp. Juno lies flat on the galley table on a folded sail, bomber jacket off, a wet towel across her forehead and one on each wrist, gloves still on. Sweat shines on her. The air above her wobbles like a road at noon. Ashka sits at the end of the table, feathers dull, watching her and nothing else.

Bram (31, huge, beard, towel over one shoulder) wrings a towel into a bucket. The bucket steams.

Kaito (21, the white streak in his hair stuck flat with sweat) stands in the doorway with a second bucket, not sure if he's allowed in.

BRAM: In or out, Kaito. You're letting the cold in.
KAITO: We want the cold in.
BRAM: Then come in and be cold near her.

Kaito sets the bucket down. Juno's eyes open to slits.

JUNO: Odds I die tonight.
BRAM: Not taking it.
JUNO: Come on, big man. Give me a number.
BRAM: Galley Rule Nine. No betting on the table while you're on the table.
JUNO: That's not a rule. You just made that.
BRAM: Every rule got made by somebody.

He lays the fresh towel across her throat. She hisses. Steam lifts off it inside a second.

KAITO: (low, to Bram) How hot is she?
BRAM: Towel boils in about a minute.
KAITO: Is that bad?
BRAM: Water boils. People don't. So. Yes.
JUNO: (eyes shut) I can hear you. Both of you. Terrible bedside. Two to one Brask cries first.
KAITO: I'm not gonna cry.
JUNO: That's a yes in your voice.

Skarn's grey head fills the deck hatch, sniffs the heat coming up, and withdraws with an expression. Kaito puts his hand on the sail near her gloved one. Doesn't touch it.

KAITO: The vents. Last night. I've never seen anything like that.
JUNO: (a breath) Yeah. Me neither.

Her eyes close. Bram checks the pulse at her jaw, looks at Kaito, and for once says nothing at all. He wrings the next towel.

## SCENE 2 — INT. GALLEY — DAWN (≈ 1:00)

Grey light down the hatch. Juno asleep now, breathing slow; the towel on her forehead is merely damp. Bram asleep upright on a stool, arms folded. Ashka asleep on Juno's boot.

Sela (28, silver-blue ponytail, cropped navigator's jacket) sits at the far end of the table with the logbook open, writing. Kaito slumps in across from her, rings under his eyes.

SELA: (not looking up) She broke around the third bell. The fever, I mean. Bram says the towels stopped boiling. He's very proud of the towels.
KAITO: You were up?
SELA: I'm the captain, pup. I'm always up. I just do it lying down.

She writes. He cranes to read it upside down.

KAITO: What are you writing?
SELA: "Juno Calloway. Fever broke. Bram did not sleep. Kaito did not cry. Much."
KAITO: I didn't—
SELA: It says "much."

He gives up. Looks over at Juno.

KAITO: Her marker. Krell's gone but the paper's still somewhere. The Sever bought it, so someone's holding it. (standing) I'll go ask Nim.
SELA: You'll go ask the one-armed bookmaker in a burned city where the Sever keep their paperwork.
KAITO: Yeah.
SELA: Sit down first. Eat something.
KAITO: I'm not hungry.
BRAM: (eyes still shut) Vegetable.
KAITO: He's asleep!
BRAM: Vegetable.

Sela slides a bowl of something green down the table without looking. Kaito eats it standing up, furious.

## SCENE 3 — INT. GALLEY — MORNING (≈ 1:45)

Later. Bram and Sela are up on deck; boots overhead. Juno sits up against the galley wall on the folded sail, jacket back round her shoulders, a mug of water in both gloved hands. Kaito sits on the stool with the empty bucket between his feet, so he has somewhere to look.

Plain light. No music.

JUNO: You stayed.
KAITO: Bram made me.
JUNO: Bram's asleep on the anchor chain. I saw him go past the hatch.
KAITO: ...Fine. I stayed.

She drinks. Her hands shake a little. The water in the mug doesn't steam, and she notices that before anything else, and her shoulders come down.

JUNO: Ask it.
KAITO: Ask what?
JUNO: You've been looking at my hands since the Palace. Everyone does, after. Ask.
KAITO: (a beat) How'd you learn to hold that much fire?
JUNO: Didn't learn. Got used to it. Different thing.

She turns the mug in her hands.

JUNO: My dad liked cards. Wasn't good at them. Just liked them. When I was nineteen he ran out of things to put on the table, so he put me. My labour. Four years of it, written on a slip, stamped by a bookmaker. And he lost. Course he lost. He'd have lost his own hands if they'd let him stake them.
KAITO: They can do that?
JUNO: In Ashmarket you can do anything you can get a stamp for. So I worked the furnaces. Under the market. The ones we blew up last night, as it happens. Feeding them. Raking them. You learn to pick up a coal a long time before you learn it isn't going to kill you. Four years. My hands were like this before Ashka ever came near them. She just found a girl who could already hold it. (small) Lucky bird.
KAITO: Where's your dad now?
JUNO: Don't know. Don't bet on it either.

Silence. Kaito doesn't fill it. Then:

KAITO: Why'd you keep gambling? After that. I'd have burned every deck in the city.
JUNO: (looks at him properly) Because you can't lose what's already been wagered. He put all of me on the table at nineteen. Anything I've had since is house money. Might as well play it loud.
KAITO: That's the worst thing I ever heard.
JUNO: It's the only thing that works.
KAITO: Screw that.
JUNO: (the beginning of a smile) Yeah. Thought you'd say that.

Kaito stands. Picks up the bucket.

KAITO: Nim's got your paper, or knows who does. I'm going to get it.
JUNO: You can't. It's Sever paper now.
KAITO: Then I'll get it off the Sever.
JUNO: (tired, fond) Five to one you come back with a black eye and no paper.
KAITO: You're not allowed to bet. Galley Rule Nine.
JUNO: That's not a real rule!
BRAM: (O.S., from the deck) It is now.

## SCENE 4 — EXT. ASHMARKET, LOWER PIER — MORNING (≈ 1:30)

Ash still coming down like grey snow. Half the lantern-strings hang broken. Up on the crag, the Cinder Palace is a black shell. People sweep. A hand-painted board at the end of the pier: HADDA. BETS. NO CREDIT. NO EXCEPTIONS. "NO" underlined twice.

Nim Hadda (30, man, lean, sooty, unshaven, pleasant; left sleeve of his coat pinned neat at the shoulder) sits behind the board on a crate, a ledger open on his knee.

Kaito arrives at a jog, Skarn padding behind. Then, to Kaito's visible horror, Juno comes down the pier after him, slow, using a boathook as a cane. Sela walks beside her not helping, which is its own kind of help.

NIM: Calloway. You look awful.
JUNO: You look like a man with one ledger and no city.
NIM: It's a very good ledger. (nods at Kaito) This the one you stripped at cards?
KAITO: She cheated.
NIM: She always cheats, son. It's the only honest thing about her.

Kaito plants his feet.

KAITO: Her marker. Krell bought it off you. Who's got the paper?
NIM: Nobody.
KAITO: Don't— what do you mean, nobody?
NIM: I mean Warden Krell paid me for the debt in Sever coin and took a copy. The copy was in the Palace counting room, which your friend here turned into a kiln. (he lifts a sheet from the ledger: a brown-edged slip with a stamp) This is the original. Bookmaker holds the original. Always.

Juno goes very still.

JUNO: Nim.
NIM: Four years' labour, Juno Calloway. Plus interest. Plus the interest on the interest, which is where I make my money. (he looks at it) Krell paid full value. So, in point of fact, it's paid.
JUNO: By him. It's his.
NIM: He isn't here. And I don't hold paper for men who run. Bad for the book.

He tears the slip down the middle. Then across. He lets the quarters fall into the ash at his feet, where they go grey in a second.

JUNO: (very quiet) What do you want for it?
NIM: Nothing. I'm a bookmaker, Calloway. I don't forgive. I write off.
JUNO: (eyes bright, voice coming back) You're going soft.
NIM: I've one arm and a wet ledger. I was already soft. (turns a page) Now. New book. Who's this?
SELA: Sela Vey. I captain the ship she's about to try and join.
NIM: Try?
SELA: She hasn't asked. She's been busy being on fire.
NIM: (writing) Calloway gets a berth on the Gullwake. Evens.
JUNO: Evens? I'm a sure thing!
NIM: You're a sure thing who set the market alight. Evens is generous.
KAITO: Hang on. What's the book on me?
NIM: (not looking up) Walks into something before he's off the pier. Three to one.

Kaito turns to argue, steps back, and hits the HADDA board. It comes down on him. Ash everywhere. Skarn looks out to sea.

NIM: (writing) Paid.

## SCENE 5 — EXT. THE GULLWAKE, DECK — AFTERNOON (≈ 1:45)

The ship at anchor off the crag, clear of the ash. A barrel between two crates, a blanket over it, a deck of cards. Juno on one side, colour back in her face, jacket on, gloves on, Kaito's brass goggles pushed up on her forehead. Kaito on the other, jaw set. Sela on the rail with the logbook. Bram at the galley hatch, peeling something. Skarn and Ashka on the boom, side by side, like two bored uncles.

JUNO: Terms. One hand. I win, I'm crew.
KAITO: You're already crew. Sela said—
SELA: I said she can ask.
JUNO: I don't ask, sweetheart. I win. One hand. I win, I'm crew and I keep the goggles. You win, you get the goggles and I go back to Nim and cry on his one shoulder.
KAITO: You've got my goggles ON.
JUNO: (tapping them) They're my deposit. Deal?
KAITO: (to Sela) Why am I the one playing?
SELA: She asked for you by name. I've written it down.
KAITO: Fine. Deal. And I'm watching your hands this time.
JUNO: Good. Watch them.

She deals. Kaito watches her hands like a hawk, which means he doesn't watch her face. Behind him, Sela tilts her head a fraction: something at Juno's left cuff. Sela writes one word in the logbook. Says nothing.

Three cards each. Kaito's hand is decent. He fails completely to hide this; his eyebrows do a thing.

JUNO: Oh, that's a good hand. That's a lovely face for a good hand.
KAITO: I don't have a face.
JUNO: You have ALL the face.
BRAM: (from the hatch) He does have a lot of face.

She lays down. Three crowns. Kaito stares.

KAITO: There's four crowns in a deck.
JUNO: And you've got one. Well spotted.
KAITO: You had three crowns in your SLEEVE—
JUNO: Did you see them in my sleeve?
KAITO: No!
JUNO: Then it's a hand. Captain?
SELA: (writing) "Calloway wins a berth. By cheating. Kaito could not prove it." (looks up) Welcome aboard.

Juno sits back. Then pulls the goggles off her head and tosses them over the barrel. They land in Kaito's lap.

KAITO: What's this?
JUNO: Deposit. You get it back when the deal closes. Deal's closed.
KAITO: You said if you won you'd keep them.
JUNO: And I lied, which is cheating, which you still can't prove. (she stands, stretches, winces) Right. Where do I sleep?
KAITO: There's a hammock in the fore—
JUNO: Nope.

CUT TO:

INT. CREW CABIN. Kaito's blanket, Kaito's chart tube and Kaito's spare boots, all now on the floor. Juno in the one bunk under the skylight, boots off, hands behind her head. Ashka on the sill.

KAITO: That's my bunk.
JUNO: It's the best bunk.
KAITO: That's WHY it's my bunk.
JUNO: And now it's the best bunk with me in it. Which is better. You have to admit that's better.

Grist is asleep across the cabin doorway. Kaito tries to step over him. Cannot. Looks round at Sela, who has appeared behind him.

SELA: She did win.
KAITO: She CHEATED.
SELA: That's what I wrote.

## SCENE 6 — EXT. DECK — NIGHT (≈ 1:45)

A spare sail rigged as a screen across the stern. Behind it, a cut-down cask, steaming; Ashka perched on the galley stove-pipe keeping the water hot and very pleased about it. Lanterns. Bram nails a slate to the mast.

BRAM: Galley Rule Ten. Bath night. Everybody. (writing) Order is: captain. Then Juno, because fever. Then me. Then Kaito.
KAITO: Why am I last?
BRAM: You're the deckhand, that's the roster, and you're on watch. Face forward.
KAITO: On watch for WHAT?
SELA: (O.S., behind the screen) Me, apparently.

Kaito turns to face the bow so fast his neck clicks. Behind the screen: steam, and Sela's head and shoulders above the rim of the cask, hair pinned up, eyes closed, a cup balanced on the edge. Entirely content. Steam does the rest.

SELA: Pup, you're facing the mast.
KAITO: The mast is forward.
SELA: The mast is three feet away. You're going to—

SFX: BONK.

Kaito, forehead against the mast. On the cabin roof, Skarn looks up at the sky as if asking it something.

Juno sits on a crate waiting her turn, jacket on, gloves on, a stick of chalk in her hand. She looks at the mast. She looks at Kaito. She stands up and writes under Bram's slate, in big letters: KAITO AND WHO?

KAITO: WHAT.
JUNO: New book. Odds. Captain, three to one.
SELA: (O.S.) Flattering. Too short. He can't look at me.
JUNO: Me, five to one. I'm not that stupid.
BRAM: (counting on his fingers) Nim's board. Two to one.
JUNO: (writing) "The HADDA board, two to one." The mast, evens.
KAITO: I'm not— you can't bet on— that's not a—
JUNO: It's chalk, sweetheart. It's on the mast. It's law.

Steam blooms as the cask gurgles behind the screen. We are on Kaito's face, going red from the ears inward.

CUT TO: Grist, flat on the foredeck, snoring. The kettle on the galley stove boils over. Ashka hops off the pipe, outraged.

Sela comes round the screen in a long shirt with a towel round her hair, logbook already open, wholly unbothered.

SELA: (writing aloud) "Bath night. Rule Ten. Kaito hit the mast. Juno opened a book on him." (looks up, kind) Your ears, pup.
KAITO: I KNOW about my ears.
SELA: (to the book) "He knows about his ears."

Bram goes behind the screen. A huge, contented sigh, and the cask makes a sound like a ship running aground. Juno adds a chalk line. Kaito is still facing the wrong way.

MUSIC: something warm, a fiddle, the first easy night the ship has had.

## SCENE 7 — INT. CHART ROOM — LATER (≈ 1:00)

A small cabin. Sela's chart on the table, pins in it: Pinhold, and beside it, tiny, a straight line through a circle. Next to it, Kaito's chart, not blank any more: coastlines in his hand, careful, a lot of corrections. Sela plots a course east with a brass divider. Kaito in the doorway, towel round his neck, hair wet, ears finally a normal colour.

KAITO: Gaptooth. That's east?
SELA: That's the only east. Two island chains grinding past each other. Vesh gets us through or nobody does.
KAITO: You've done it before?
SELA: (taps the logbook) It says I have.

She stops. The divider stays in her hand, point down. She's listening.

KAITO: What?
SELA: Hush a second.

SFX: a very low hum, more felt than heard, coming up through the boards. Three small wavy lines of it. Then:

SFX: Skarn HOWLS on the roof. Ashka screams in the cabin. Grist, who never gets up, gets up, six legs hitting the deck like dropped anvils. Under the hull, Vesh: a scream through water that puts a ripple across the cup on the chart table.

The ship lurches. The sea rolls with no wind in it. Sela catches the chart. The pin at Gaptooth falls over. She looks at it. Sets it upright with a steady hand. Then, beside it, very small, she draws a straight line through a circle.

KAITO: That's what happened at Pinhold.
SELA: (quietly) Yes.
KAITO: Where?
SELA: Where we're going.

INT. WHITE ROOM — SAME MOMENT

Director Aurel Vasch (46, man, tall and thin, grey hair combed back, white coat belted in black; his right forearm a glass tube between steel rings, a white core glowing inside it) stands at a chart of the whole Drift, a pen in his bare metal fingers. One short stroke is already on it. He adds a second, gentle as a man signing a letter.

VASCH: (softly) Two.
