## SCENE 2 — EXT. PINHOLD — SKARN'S ROOST, THE DEAD CISTERN TOWER — NIGHT (≈ 0:40)

Wide: the old cistern tower on the headland, Pinhold's grey roofs below, the Great Pylon standing out of the harbour, riveted iron with a faint white light climbing it. Under everything, a low hum.

SFX: the hum, very low, three slow pulses

Close: KAITO BRASK (21) asleep against SKARN's flank on the flat lead roof, a basket of eggs in the crook of his arm, goggles crooked. Skarn's ears go up; his fin crackles once.

Kaito opens one eye.

KAITO: If that's customs again, I paid. In eggs. It was a whole thing.

Skarn growls, low. Kaito sits up.

KAITO: ...That's not customs.

High on the cliff cable above them: SILT (29), smooth white mask with one eye slit, long ragged black coat, sitting easy on the HOUND, a Hollow: hound-shaped, cables where the veins should be, a white core burning in its chest. Its claws grip the cable like a bird's.

SILT: Found you.

KAITO: You said that already. You were doing the looming thing then, too.

SILT: You weren't awake.

KAITO: Then who were you talking to?

A pause. Silt looks at the hound. The hound looks at Kaito.

SILT: Bring the wolf down and this is short.

KAITO: Short for who?

SILT: For him. He's the only one here I'm not going to hurt.

KAITO: (picking up the basket) Skarn. Hold these.

Skarn does not hold them. Kaito stuffs the basket inside his top, zips it to the neck, and steps off the tower.

## PART A

## SCENE 3 — EXT. PINHOLD — ROOFTOPS AND CHART STREET — NIGHT (≈ 2:00)

Wide: Kaito drops roof to roof, Skarn gliding above him. Behind, the hound comes down the cliff face headfirst, Silt flat along its spine, no hurry in him.

SFX: tiles going, one after another

KAITO: (running) He's riding a dog down a wall. That's cheating. That's just cheating.

Skarn barks once, flat.

KAITO: I know we glide! That's different! That's wind! (beat) Also he's got no face, so I'm assuming he's ugly. Is that fair? I think that's fair.

MUSIC: brass, running, then it cuts dead

Low angle: the hound lands in front of him on the chandler's roof, soundless. Up close its eyes are blank white glass.

Kaito skids, stops. Looks at it. Looks at his right fist; the fork on the back of it glows faint yellow.

KAITO: Right. Okay. You're a dog.

He punches it in the face.

SFX: KRANG, like hitting a stove

His whole arm rings. The hound doesn't blink. It takes the jacket at his waist in its teeth and throws him off the roof.

CUT TO:

INT. HALVARD'S CHARTS — CONTINUOUS

Kaito comes through the shop window backwards in a sheet of glass and lands among framed charts. Pinhold's coastline on every wall, every hand, all of them the same. A card on the nearest reads: CORRECTED. FINAL.

He lies there a second, bleeding from the scalp, looking at them.

KAITO: (quiet) You're all right. Every one of you is right now. Congratulations.

Egg runs out of the bottom of his top.

Silt steps through the window. Glass crunches once, then not again. The hound fills the gap behind him. Outside, Skarn circles, snarling, no way in.

SILT: You hit a Hollow. With your hand.

KAITO: It had a face.

SILT: It doesn't, actually.

He crouches. Looks at Kaito's right hand, the glowing mark.

SILT: Count of ten. That's all it is. The wolf doesn't feel it. You do, for a bit. Then you don't feel anything about him at all, and you'll thank me, because you'll have forgotten what you're thanking me for.

KAITO: That's the worst sales pitch I've ever heard.

SILT: It isn't a pitch.

KAITO: Then what do you get? You don't look like a man who gets paid.

SILT: I get to stop looking for you.

KAITO: You found me twice tonight. You're not even trying.

SILT: No. (beat) I'm not.

Silt's hand goes to his belt: a hooked blade, glowing white. Kaito's eyes go to it, then up through the broken window to Skarn.

KAITO: Skarn. All of it.

Close: Skarn's eyes blaze. The fin along his spine lights white-yellow, root to tip.

Extreme close: the fork goes from glow to glare. Light runs up the arm under the skin, lighting the old scars one by one, hand to elbow, branching like a river from above.

Then it passes the elbow.

SFX: a sound like a sheet tearing, slowly

Kaito screams through his teeth. A new branch burns into the skin above the elbow, inside the upper arm, smoking.

Lightning fills the shop. Every frame on the wall goes at once. Silt is already moving, coat over the mask. The hound takes the bolt in the chest; its core flares, dims, and it staggers one step sideways like a drunk.

Wide: Kaito out through the hole the lightning tore in the roof, Skarn catching him on the fin. One long falling glide over the harbour, the Pylon's light sliding past.

His right arm hangs. He's holding on with his left.

KAITO: (slurring) 'S fine. 'S all fine. Just need to—

His eyes roll. He goes off Skarn's back.

Skarn twists, snaps at the jacket, misses. Kaito hits the black water.

CUT TO:

Silt in the doorway, soot on his coat, watching the splash. He pats the hound once without looking at it.

SILT: Tide's coming in. He's a Pinhold boy. They come back for the town.

Under the harbour: a line of blue-green lanterns lights, one after another, very long, rising.

SFX: the hum

CUT TO BLACK.

## SCENE 4 — INT. THE GULLWAKE — GALLEY — DAY (≈ 1:50)

Black. Then steam across the lens, and somehow the smell of stew.

Kaito wakes face-down on a table under a blanket that was once a sail, right arm in a sling, bandaged shoulder to wrist, shirtless. The egg basket, against all odds, hangs on a hook with three eggs in it.

Filling the galley: BRAM TORVALD (31), near two metres, shaved head, black beard, shirtless under leather dungarees with one strap down, tattooed shoulders, grey patches on his left shoulder and forearm like old paint, a cook's towel on his shoulder. Stirring a pot the size of a drum.

BRAM: Morning.

KAITO: (up too fast) Where— who— where's my—

BRAM: Wolf's on deck. Sulking. Ate two eggs.

KAITO: He doesn't eat eggs.

BRAM: He did.

KAITO: He has never once eaten an egg. I've offered. He looks at me like I've insulted his mother.

BRAM: He was hungry. You were in the water a while.

KAITO: How long's a while?

BRAM: Long enough I put the lid on.

Kaito looks at the bandage. Flexes the right hand. The fingers close slow, like through syrup.

KAITO: How far up?

BRAM: Past the elbow. New one. Thin, like a hair. (beat) I've seen worse.

KAITO: Where?

BRAM: (touches the grey on his own forearm; doesn't explain) Here.

A bowl lands in front of Kaito. Stew, thick, orange. On top, something green, cut with great care.

KAITO: What's that.

BRAM: Vegetable.

KAITO: Which one?

BRAM: Vegetable.

Kaito eats. Stops, chews, looks at the spoon like it's lied to him.

KAITO: ...Okay. That's good. That's really good.

BRAM: (misty, immediately) Thank you.

KAITO: Don't— are you crying?

BRAM: Steam.

KAITO: It's not steam.

BRAM: Eat your vegetable.

Through the hatch above: deck, sun, and GRIST, an iron rhino-beetle the size of a truck, asleep, six legs tucked, anvil horn pointed at the sky. A gull walks along his shell.

Skarn, on the far rail, glares at Grist, glares at the gull, glares down the hatch at Kaito.

KAITO: (mouth full) That a beetle?

BRAM: Grist.

KAITO: It's the size of my house.

BRAM: He.

KAITO: He's the size of my house.

BRAM: You've a small house.

KAITO: I've got a tower. It leaks, but it's tall.

BRAM: (approving) Tall's good. (beat) Grist likes you. He's not moved.

KAITO: He's asleep.

BRAM: That's how he likes people.

A slate on the wall: GALLEY RULES. Under it, 1. NOBODY FIGHTS IN MY GALLEY.

KAITO: You've only got the one rule?

BRAM: So far. Been quiet. (beat) You'll help with that.

KAITO: I don't fight in kitchens.

BRAM: You hit a Hollow in the face.

KAITO: ...Who told you that?

BRAM: Your knuckles.

KAITO: What's rule two going to be?

BRAM: Depends what you do next.

KAITO: Who pulled me out?

BRAM: Vesh. Captain pointed. (nods aft) She's aft, behind the sail. Go on. She'll want her money's worth of looking at you.

KAITO: (standing, blanket round him like a cape) What does that mean?

BRAM: (stirring) Means go aft.

## SCENE 5 — EXT. THE GULLWAKE — AFT DECK — DAY (≈ 0:40)

Kaito comes along the deck in cargo pants and the sail-blanket, past Grist's sleeping face. The Gullwake is a battered salvage hull patched in six colours. Aft, the water heaves where something very long turns under it.

A square of old sail rigged as a screen, steam leaking over the top. A plank door in the frame, a bucket in front of it.

KAITO: Hello? Captain? The cook said you wanted to—

He moves the bucket and opens the door.

Steam. A lot of it. A copper tub. SELA VEY (28) in it, up to the shoulders, silver-blue hair pinned up and dripping, sea-green eyes, gold hoops, a logbook open on a stool beside her and a pencil in her hand. She looks up at him over the top of the book exactly as if he were late.

SELA: Oh, good. You're up.

SMASH CUT:

Skarn's face: a wolf who has seen his human do this kind of thing before.

SFX: the plank door, slammed

SELA (O.S.): (a laugh, long and warm, that goes on a while)

Over the laugh, from beyond the frame: a thud, a yelp, a second thud, then rigging creaking under weight.

SELA (O.S.): Was that the mast?

KAITO (O.S.): (very high) Yeah.

## PART B

## SCENE 6 — EXT. THE GULLWAKE — MAIN MAST — DAY (≈ 1:40)

Sela, dressed: cropped navy jacket with brass buttons over the black bandeau, the whirlpool mark showing below her left collarbone, wrap skirt, hair wet down her back in its tail. Barefoot at the foot of the mast, logbook open on her forearm, writing. She doesn't look up.

Above her, Kaito is wedged in the crosstrees in his sail-blanket, one arm in a sling, ears red enough to see from the deck.

SELA: Twelve a week.

KAITO: I'm not talking to you from up here.

SELA: You're talking now. Twelve a week, and found. Found means fed.

KAITO: I know what found means.

SELA: Then say yes to it.

KAITO: I haven't said I'm hired. You're negotiating with a man who hasn't said he's hired.

SELA: I'm negotiating with a man in a mast with his ears on fire. I've had worse starts. I've hired men who were sick over the side mid-handshake.

KAITO: Did they take the job?

SELA: One of them's cooking your dinner.

KAITO: ...The stew was a trick, wasn't it.

SELA: You looked at it like it was. (writing) "Distrusts vegetables." Fourteen a week, then, since you've a brain in there. You can climb, you can fly a wolf, and you can read a chart, which I know because you shouted about it across half the rooftops in Pinhold with customs behind you.

KAITO: I didn't see anything.

SELA: Yes you did. You saw me being right about you. It's all right. There was a bucket in front of that door for a reason and the reason wasn't you. (looks up at last) You can come down. It's over. I'm wearing all my clothes.

A pause.

KAITO: Define all.

SELA: (delighted) Oh, you're going to be fun. Jacket, top, skirt, both earrings. I'll write you a list.

Kaito climbs down slow, one-armed. She watches the bandaged arm the whole way, nothing else. He drops the last metre and stands there in his sail, trying to look like a man who has never been up a mast.

SELA: Sit.

He sits on a crate. She crouches, takes the right hand, turns it, looks at the burn-lines going up under the bandage.

SELA: Who taught you to go all at once like that?

KAITO: Nobody. It's how it works. You want more, you take more.

SELA: That's how a bucket works. It isn't how a bond works. Everyone who takes more pays for more. You've just got yours written where you can see it.

KAITO: And yours?

SELA: (lightly) Ask me when you've signed. (lets go) Fourteen a week. A hammock, Bram's cooking, and a captain who will never, ever let you forget the mast. I've a berth free. I need a second pair of eyes forward when Vesh takes us through the Gaptooth, and the wolf can roost in the foretop. The gulls'll hate it. Everyone wins.

KAITO: No.

SELA: (not surprised) Why not?

KAITO: Pinhold's mine.

SELA: Pinhold's got a pylon through its heart. You felt it start. I watched you feel it. The soil's gone grey. Half the wells are dead.

KAITO: Yeah. And every chart on every wall is right for the first time since anyone's kept one, and everybody's so damn pleased, and they think that's winning. It isn't. It's a corpse lying still. (beat) Somebody's got to stay and know that.

She looks at him a long moment. Then writes.

KAITO: What are you writing?

SELA: (reading as she writes) "Refused. Says the island is his. The island is being nailed to the seabed, but I didn't like to say." (snaps the book shut) And I'll pay for the survey sheets. They were in your parcel under the medicine, where I asked for them. Forty years old, Empire paper, and whoever drew them loved it. You can tell. Careful hand.

Kaito's face does nothing at all. He looks at the water.

KAITO: Surveyors lie for a living. They draw the sea and it moves.

SELA: Mm. (standing) Then why did you carry them so carefully?

She's walking before he can answer.

## SCENE 7 — EXT. THE GULLWAKE — GALLEY HATCH — LATE AFTERNOON (≈ 1:00)

Bram on the hatch step, peeling something root-shaped. Kaito beside him in a borrowed shirt three sizes too big, feeding the last egg to Skarn one-handed. Skarn eats it with contempt.

KAITO: He said he'd count to ten. The mask. Said Skarn wouldn't feel it.

Bram's knife stops.

BRAM: Sever.

KAITO: That a name or a job?

BRAM: Both. Empire. The quiet part of it. (peeling again, slower) They pin an island. Then they come round the houses. Anyone with a partner. (beat) They cut it.

KAITO: Cut what?

BRAM: (taps his chest, over the anvil-shaped mark) The bond. Count of ten. The animal's still standing after. It's just not anyone's.

He finishes the peel in one long strip, lets it drop.

KAITO: And the people?

BRAM: They get to keep their arms.

Silence. Skarn's ears flat. Grist, asleep, shifts one leg.

Sela comes past with a coil of rope, speaking without stopping.

SELA: If he ever goes quiet and puts that towel down, you get behind Grist. Don't ask him why. Don't wait for him. Behind Grist.

Bram touches the towel on his shoulder. Keeps it there.

KAITO: What happens when he puts it down?

SELA: I said don't ask him.

KAITO: I'm asking you.

SELA: (beat) Somebody dies. So far it's never been one of us.

KAITO: (standing) I want to go back.

SELA: (over her shoulder) I know you do. Skiff's on the port side. I'll row. You can't.

## SCENE 8 — EXT. PINHOLD — THE QUAY — DUSK (≈ 1:20)

Wide: the harbour at dusk, the Pylon's light the brightest thing in it. Sela rows the skiff in with long easy strokes; Kaito in the bow; Skarn swimming alongside, fin up like a sail, hating it.

The quay. Boats hauled up the slip. Sever grey coats at the far end, and nearer, something standing in the shallows, chained to a bollard.

Kaito stops with one foot on the stone.

Close: a shore-ox, the kind that drags boats up a slip, six-legged, broad as a cart. Grey plates riveted to brow and shoulders. Cables under the hide of the neck. Blank white eyes. A white core in the chest where the breastbone should be. On the iron collar, a brass bell, old and dented, that does not belong there.

Beside it, DASKEL (58), a dock woman in oilskins, holding out a bucket of feed. The animal isn't looking at the bucket. It isn't looking at anything.

KAITO: (very quiet) Tulip.

The ox doesn't move. Kaito goes down the slip to it, into the water to his knees.

KAITO: Tulip. Hey. Hey, girl. It's me. I used to— Daskel, she used to—

DASKEL: (not turning) I know what she used to.

KAITO: She pulled our boat up every spring. She knew the bell. You rang it and she came up the slip on her own.

Daskel reaches over and rings the bell. One small clear note. The ox stands exactly where it is.

DASKEL: Rang it all day.

KAITO: Where was everyone? Nobody stopped them?

DASKEL: Stopped them with what? Harbourmaster signed the paper. Said it was her or the boats. (beat) It was two men and a paper, Kaito. That's all it took. Two men and a paper.

Behind him Skarn has come out of the water and stopped dead on the slip, hackles up the length of his fin, a whine in him barely a sound. He won't come closer. He's looking at the core.

Kaito puts his good hand on the ox's neck where the cables run. It's warm. Something ticks under his palm, regular. Not a heartbeat.

KAITO: She was somebody's.

DASKEL: (turning at last, dry-eyed, which is worse) She was mine. They said I'd be glad. Said in a week I wouldn't miss her. (beat) It's been a week.

Sela, in the skiff, has stopped rowing. The logbook is open on her knee. She writes nothing.

## SCENE 9 — EXT. PINHOLD — THE HARBOURMASTER'S WALL — NIGHT (≈ 0:45)

The big chart of Pinhold painted on the harbourmaster's wall under a lamp, the one the whole town checks. CORRECTED. FINAL. Two troopers nail a paper over the middle of it. A third stands by: hooked blade on his belt, white collar, black bar across the chest with its circle cut in two. A crowd gathers the way a crowd does when it doesn't want to be one.

TROOPER: By order of the Director. All bonded animals on the island of Pinhold are to be brought to the pylon square and surrendered by dawn. Standing Order Twelve will apply. (flat, by rote) This is mercy.

WOMAN IN THE CROWD: Dawn tomorrow?

TROOPER: Dawn.

He turns and walks. The crowd doesn't move. Then it does, in pieces, nobody saying what they're thinking.

Kaito at the back, Skarn's shoulder against his hip. He reads the sheet. Reads it again. Looks down the harbour to the Pylon, white, humming, and past it to the dark where one skiff lantern rocks, waiting.

Skarn's yellow eyes in the lamplight, on him.

KAITO: (not looking down) Don't look at me like that. I'm thinking.

His hand finds the fur at Skarn's neck.

KAITO: (barely out loud) By dawn.

SFX: the hum, three slow pulses

The paper lifts in the wind and settles back over the chart.
