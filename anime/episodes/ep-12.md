## SCENE 1 — EXT. GAPTOOTH REACH — DAY (≈ 1:15)

Black screen. A deep, slow, gentle voice.

BRAM (V.O.): Previously. Gaptooth. Two islands grinding past each other like two pans on the one hob. Captain taught the lad to read water. He fell in it. Twice. Second time on purpose, he says. Captain read her own book to find out she likes dumplings. She does. I made dumplings. Everyone was happy. Then four harpoons came out of the fog and one of them's in my galley. I'd have swept if I'd known. (a beat) That's not previously. That's now.

Fade up. Mist thick as milk. Black rock walls grind past on either side. Between them, under tow, the Gullwake. Four harpoon chains hang taut off the hull.

SFX: THOOM. THOOM. THOOM.

INT. GALLEY. A harpoon head has punched through the wall and stopped a hand's width from the stew pot. Bram (31, 198 cm, shirtless in work dungarees, towel on the shoulder) looks at it.

BRAM: Galley Rule Thirty-One. Knock.

EXT. DECK. Kaito (21, goggles askew) clings to a shuddering rail. Juno (23) beside him, one gloved hand on her hip, Ashka on her shoulder, calm.

KAITO: WHO THE HELL HARPOONS A SHIP?!
JUNO: Someone who doesn't want you to leave, sweetheart. I've had dates like that.
KAITO: Did they go well?
JUNO: I'm here, aren't I.

Out of the mist slides a grey Sever cutter. Chained on its bow: a Hollow bear, two metres at the shoulder, ash-grey, blank white eyes, a white core in its chest. Beside it, a very tall figure with a lance.

SFX: KRRRNNK.

## SCENE 2 — EXT. THE GULLWAKE, QUARTERDECK — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:30)

Sela (28) at the wheel, raising her spyglass as if checking the weather.

SELA: Bram. Is the stew on?
BRAM: (O.S., from below) Your stew's on, Captain. It's got a harpoon in it.
SELA: In the stew or in the wall?
BRAM: (O.S.) Wall.
SELA: Then it's on. We'll want something hot after.
KAITO: (at her elbow, pointing) After WHAT? Who IS that?

Through the spyglass: Hekka Dorn (33, 193 cm, light brown skin, thick wavy black hair swept back, grey plate on chest, shoulders and forearms, bare midriff, black gloves), one hand on the bear's chain, looking straight back down the glass, patient.

SELA: Hekka Dorn. Sever captain. Cuts bonds for a living, and she's very well reviewed.
KAITO: Reviewed by WHO?
SELA: Nobody who's still bonded.
JUNO: (at the rail; the grin has gone thin) I've seen the book on her. Nobody's ever collected.
KAITO: Collected what?
JUNO: Exactly.
KAITO: That's not an answer!

SFX: SKRANNG.

A bond-cutter harpoon the size of a door shears the tow cables. Vesh lunges on alone, then rears as chain nets drop over her.

KAITO: They cut the tow! Why cut the tow if you want the ship?

Hekka walks a taut chain like a plank, lance on her shoulder. It barely dips.

HEKKA: Captain Vey. Requesting permission to come aboard.
SELA: Denied.
HEKKA: Noted.

Sela unhooks a plain cutlass from the rack, casual as picking up a pen.

SELA: She doesn't want the ship, pup. She wants what comes back for it. (pointing the cutlass at Vesh without looking) Vesh is tangled. Go and cut her out.
KAITO: And leave you with THAT? She's carrying a flagpole!
SELA: Then she'll be slow turning round.

Troopers slide down the chains, blades out. Two land on a dark mound of iron plate near the stern. It is Grist, asleep.

TROOPER: Aft deck clear! Just cargo!

Hekka steps aboard. The planks groan.

KAITO: I can HELP!
SELA: (patting his cheek, already turning away) Go on. The grown-ups are talking.
KAITO: (ears going red) I'm TWENTY-ONE!
SELA: Yes. Off you go.

Skarn waits behind him with a look of deep, tired contempt. Kaito swings up.

Sela and Hekka face each other, a head and a half between them. Hekka looks at the cutlass.

HEKKA: That's your sword?
SELA: Mm. Would you like to see what it can do?

## SCENE 3 — EXT. QUARTERDECK — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:15)

SFX: KRAK.

Hekka's lance smashes a water barrel to staves where Sela stood a heartbeat ago.

Sela is already inside the reach, cutlass point at a seam in Hekka's shoulder plate.

SELA: It's not the length, Captain. It's knowing where the other person is going to be.
HEKKA: Everyone says that about length.
SELA: Everyone's right.

Hekka takes the cut on her forearm plate and shoves Sela off with the shaft. Sela skids and keeps her feet.

HEKKA: You turn off the hips. Most sailors turn off the shoulder.
SELA: Are you flirting or grading?
HEKKA: In my unit, it's one form.
SELA: What did I get?
HEKKA: Haven't finished marking.

Sela flicks her free hand. Deck water gathers into a hard jet and slams Hekka's chest plate. Below Sela's collarbone the whirlpool mark glows teal. Hekka slides back one step, heels scoring the planks.

SFX: FSSHHK.

Hekka looks at the scrape marks. The corner of her mouth moves.

HEKKA: One step. Last person who moved me back a step, I proposed to.
SELA: And?
HEKKA: Buried with honours.
SELA: (tapping the logbook at her hip) I've had proposals with less steel in them.
HEKKA: Did you say yes?
SELA: I'd have to check.
HEKKA: Check later.
SELA: I'll pencil you in.
HEKKA: In ink.

Overhead, Kaito swoops past on Skarn, head twisted toward the duel, ears glowing.

KAITO: Did she just— are they— is this a FIGHT or—

SFX: KLANNG.

Skarn flies them both straight into a chain. Skarn did not flinch. Skarn knew.

At the mainmast, Juno chalks a new line under KAITO AND WHO? while her other hand throws fire over her shoulder at a trooper without looking.

JUNO: New line. Kaito and the Sever captain, forty to one.
KAITO: (O.S., distant) SHE'S TRYING TO KILL US!
JUNO: Thirty-five.
KAITO: (O.S.) THAT'S THE WRONG DIRECTION!
JUNO: Thirty.

## SCENE 4 — EXT. VESH, IN THE NETS — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:00)

Vesh in full: long as the ship, jaw roaring, wrapped in dozens of hooked chains. Kaito and Skarn are a speck at her neck.

KAITO: HOLD STILL, YOU GIANT NOODLE!

Vesh's eye, big as a cabin door, rolls round to look at him. Kaito reflected in it, very small.

KAITO: (both hands up) Sorry. Big respect. Love the scales. Noodle was a term of— (small) Please hold still.

His gauntlet crackles. A lightning arc parts one link.

SFX: KRAK.

KAITO: One! That's one! How many's left?

Skarn looks, very deliberately, at the forty other chains.

KAITO: Don't look at me like that. You can't count either. Two!

SFX: KRAK.

KAITO: Three! See? This is fine. This is— Skarn, why is the water getting higher? Four. FOUR.

INT. GALLEY DOOR. A trooper charges, blade raised. Bram fills the doorway, holding out a warm bread roll.

BRAM: You eaten?
TROOPER: ...No?
BRAM: Rule Thirty-Two. Nobody goes over hungry.
TROOPER: Over what?

The trooper takes the roll by reflex. One gentle shove. He sails over the rail into the sea, still holding the bread.

SFX: BOMF.

BRAM: (to the splash) That.

Juno, fire curling off one palm, waves a fistful of chalk like a bookie at the races.

JUNO: Captain's six to four! Tall one's evens! Book's open!

A trooper locked blade to blade with her pauses. Genuinely interested.

TROOPER 2: ...What's a knockout paying?
JUNO: Finally. An educated man. Nine to two, and I'll take Sever coin, it spends.
TROOPER 2: On who?
JUNO: Sweetheart, if I told you that, it wouldn't be gambling.

She pockets his coin. Behind him, Bram looms, fist drawn back.

JUNO: Relax. Towel's still on his shoulder. You'll only limp.
TROOPER 2: What towel—

SFX: a sound like a door closing on a man.

## SCENE 5 — EXT. MAIN DECK — CONTINUOUS (≈ 0:45)

SFX: KRRRRAAAKK.

The deck tears along its seam, mast to rail. Bram has jammed himself into the split, arms gone iron-grey, anvil mark blazing, a beam in each fist. Holding the ship shut.

TROOPER 2: (through a bloody nose) Is he... holding the SHIP?
BRAM: (straining, calm) Ship's like a pie crust. Goes at the edges first.
TROOPER 2: You can't hold a ship together with your—
BRAM: Watching me do it, son.

The two troopers on Grist look down as one huge sleepy eye opens under their boots.

BRAM: Grist. Supper.
TROOPER 3: The cargo's got an eye.
TROOPER 4: Cargo doesn't have—

Grist stands. Troopers slide off him into the sea. He props his anvil horn under the torn deck beside Bram and is asleep again before they splash.

SFX: SPLSH. SPLSH.

BRAM: Good lad. Mind the pot.

## SCENE 6 — EXT. QUARTERDECK — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:30)

The duel again. Harder. Both bleeding a little now.

MUSIC: drops away. Just steel and breath.

HEKKA: File says you go deep, you pay in what you know. How much is left?
SELA: Enough. I've got it in writing.
HEKKA: Whose writing?
SELA: Mine. Mostly.

A low parry. Sela's point ends up resting against Hekka's left side, below the plate. Her eyes drop to what's beside the blade: a dull, puckered, old ring-shaped scar. Shown as a wound. Flat light, faces in frame.

SELA: That's where a bond mark goes.
HEKKA: Went.
SELA: Where's yours now?

Hekka tips her head toward her cutter. Through the mist: the bear, sitting, white eyes on nothing.

SELA: Who ordered that?

Hekka swings flat. Sela ducks it.

HEKKA: Nobody.

Sela parries. Sparks.

HEKKA: People like it better if somebody ordered it. Tidier story.

MEMORY. Cold white wash. A Sever lab. Hekka, two years younger, holds out a bond-cutter to a woman in a lab coat (mid-twenties, face out of frame). The bear sits at Hekka's heel, unplated, bright brown eyes. A round mark glows on Hekka's side.

HEKKA (V.O.): Walked into the lab with the bear at my heel. Gave the doctor the cutter.

The doctor's empty hand, held out, steady. At the wrist, a small crystal shard nobody is looking at. Hekka's hand takes the cutter back.

HEKKA (V.O.): Then I took it back.
SELA (V.O.): Why?

PRESENT. Blade and lance locked, faces close. No heat in it.

HEKKA: I'd been sending troopers to hold that blade on other people's. You don't order a march you won't walk.
SELA: Most officers manage.
HEKKA: I'm not most.

MEMORY. Almost all white. Hekka standing straight, the glowing cutter to her own side. The bear looking up at her face. Nobody else in the room.

HEKKA (V.O.): Standing up. Full count. It kept looking at me till about seven. After that it looked at the door.

PRESENT. Hekka brings the lance down from high, face flat.

HEKKA: Now it wears a chain and doesn't know me. I feed it by hand. It could take the hand. Hasn't. That's as close as we get.
SELA: And the chain?
HEKKA: Regulation.
SELA: Whose?
HEKKA: Mine.

Sela blocks. Asks it quietly, like it's the next obvious question.

SELA: What was its name?

Hekka's face. Nothing moves in it. Spray on her cheek. A long beat.

SFX: KRANNG.

The next blow shatters Sela's cutlass and slams her against the mainmast. Hekka advances, lance level.

HEKKA: You asked that to make me angry. Angry people miss. I don't. (a step) Want to see what seven looks like?

## SCENE 7 — EXT. MAINMAST — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:00)

Shoulders up, both women; the faces carry it. Hekka at full lance length. The white tip rests on Sela's mark, which flickers teal, white, teal.

HEKKA: One.

Far ahead, Vesh convulses. Her lures go out, one by one, head to tail. Kaito and Skarn are thrown clear.

SFX: a low drowned sound, AAAOOOOOOMMM.

Sela's eyes. Not on Hekka. Past her, toward Vesh. She will not make a sound.

HEKKA: Two. Most people have screamed by two.
SELA: (through her teeth) Give me a minute.
HEKKA: You haven't got one.
SELA: Then I'll borrow.

Kaito, thrown off Vesh's neck, catches Skarn's fin and sees her pinned to the mast. His face goes white.

KAITO: SELA!

Lightning pours off his right gauntlet far too long, the fork mark blinding. He stands up on Skarn's back.

KAITO: Skarn! THROW ME!

Skarn looks at him. Kaito looks back.

KAITO: I KNOW. DO IT.

On deck, Juno and Bram look up as a burning streak of a man drops through the chains.

JUNO: Hundred to one he's got a plan.
BRAM: No takers.
JUNO: Fifty he's got half of one.
BRAM: ...Go on, then.

Hekka, lips mid-word.

HEKKA: Thr—

Kaito's lit right hand closes around the lance shaft.

SFX: KRA-KOOOOM.

He hangs off the shaft by his right hand and sends everything down it. Hekka lit white and flung across the deck. Sela drops free. Up Kaito's arm, past the elbow, a new branching scar burns itself in.

KAITO: COUNT THIS!

Smoke.

## SCENE 8 — EXT. MAIN DECK — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:10)

Hekka on one knee ten paces off, her lance scorched black. She gets up without hurrying.

HEKKA: Huh.

Sela sits against the mast, hand over her mark, breathing. Smiling at him.

SELA: Thank you, pup. Never do that again.
KAITO: You're welcome. No.

His right arm hangs, the new fork fresh and red past the elbow. He stares at his hand.

KAITO: Hey. ...Hey. Close.

The fingers will not close.

KAITO: (quieter) Come on. Close.
SELA: Pup. Your arm.
KAITO: It's fine.
SELA: It isn't. Hold it still.

Hekka looks him over, then at Sela, eyebrow up.

HEKKA: Who's the loud one?
SELA: Deckhand.
HEKKA: Hits like a bad idea.
SELA: He is one.
KAITO: (swaying, raising his left fist) Come on then! I've got another one!
HEKKA: (regarding the fist) You do.
SELA: Wait. (hand flat on the rail) Do you feel that?

Silence. Water in a broken barrel trembles in rings. The mist streams one way, down the channel.

SELA: It's stopped grinding.
KAITO: That's good, right?
SELA: (reading the rock walls like a page; teal in her eyes) One chain's been nailed down, and nobody told the other one. It isn't grinding past any more. It's closing.
KAITO: Closing like— closing closing?
SELA: Like a door, pup. With us in it.

SFX: a horn from the cutter. HWOOOM. HWOOOM. HWOOOM.

Hekka looks at the rock walls. Sour.

HEKKA: Tables said we had till Thursday.
SELA: Whose tables?
HEKKA: Ours.
SELA: You drew it. Then it moved. That's the Drift, Captain. Every chart's a rumour.
HEKKA: (to her troopers, not loud) Recall. Cut the lines.

Troopers scramble up the chains. White blades shear the lines. The Gullwake lurches free, drifting.

KAITO: You're just LEAVING? We're not DONE!
HEKKA: (one boot in the last chain) Serpent comes out one end of this or the other. I'll be at the end.
KAITO: Which end?
HEKKA: The one you're not at.

She looks back at Sela. Two captains, both marked, both entirely serious.

HEKKA: You live, I'll buy you a drink.
SELA: And if I don't?
HEKKA: I'll drink it.

She swings away into the mist. Sela already has the logbook open.

SELA: Dorn. One R?
HEKKA: (O.S., fading) One R.
KAITO: ARE YOU WRITING THAT DOWN?!

The page, in her neat hand: Hekka Dorn. Good reach. Better manners than most. Owes me a drink.

KAITO: She was WINNING! Why's she running?
SELA: She wasn't running, pup. She was leaving. Soldiers know the difference. (looking up at the rock) We're about to learn it.

## SCENE 9 — EXT. GAPTOOTH, THE CLOSING — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:10)

Wide. The two walls of cliff lean together until the sky is a thin grey strip. Between them, tiny, the untowed Gullwake. Vesh relights her lures and turns back.

SFX: GRRRRRRNNNNNNNNN.

KAITO: Oh.

Vesh's head surfaces beside the rail, bleeding from the chains.

KAITO: Okay! Okay! Hook her up, she tows us out!
BRAM: Tow's on the bottom. New one's an hour's work.
KAITO: Then we do it in half!
BRAM: Takes an hour, Kaito. That's what an hour's work means.
KAITO: How long have we got?

Bram looks up at the rock, measuring it with a cook's eye.

BRAM: About an egg. Soft-boiled.
KAITO: We haven't GOT an egg!
BRAM: Then it's less.

Kaito grabs Juno's shoulder. She's turning a coin over her knuckles, staring at the walls, no grin.

KAITO: Juno. Odds.

The coin stops. Ashka tucks her head against Juno's neck.

JUNO: Sweetheart. I haven't got a number.
KAITO: You've always got a number.
JUNO: I know. Shut up.
KAITO: (quiet) Okay.

Wordless. Kaito grabs a rope with his right hand. It slides through his fingers. Behind him Sela stands at the rail, looking down into the water.

EXT. SEVER CUTTER, STERN. Hekka has pulled off one scorched glove; the palm is burned red. She takes raw meat from a bucket in that hand.

TROOPER: Captain, your hand. The medic—
HEKKA: After.

The bear takes the meat from her bare burned hand, teeth a finger's width from her skin. Her hand does not move. She watches the Gap close on the little ship.

TROOPER: They won't clear it, Captain.
HEKKA: No. Pour two.
TROOPER: ...Two what, ma'am?

She doesn't answer.

EXT. THE GULLWAKE. Sela turns from the rail, as unhurried as at the start. The walls shadow the deck.

SELA: Hatches shut. Lash down everything you like, then lash yourselves.
JUNO: (colour coming back, tying the chalk to the mast) Captain's got her look. Six to one I hate this. (a breath) ...Oh, thank god. A number.
SELA: Nothing on top of this water gets out. So I'll go under it, and bring the sea back up with me.
KAITO: You can DO that?
SELA: I've done it.
KAITO: WHEN?
SELA: (tapping the logbook) Page forty-something. There's a drawing. It's not a good drawing.
KAITO: Under it HOW? It's rock all the way down!
SELA: It's rock till it isn't. Deep enough, there's a current going somewhere, and anything going somewhere can be asked to go up. Vesh knows the way. I just have to go with her far enough to ask.
JUNO: (quiet) Captain. Odds you come back.
SELA: Write them down for me. I'll want to read them.

She wraps the logbook in oilskin and puts it into Bram's huge hands. He takes it like an egg. Neither looks surprised.

SELA: Keep that dry. And keep the stew on.
BRAM: Captain.
SELA: I know.

## SCENE 10 — EXT. THE RAIL — CONTINUOUS (≈ 0:40)

Wordless. Vesh lowers her head to the rail. Sela lays her palm, then her forehead, against the scales.

MUSIC: one low note, held.

She tosses Kaito the spyglass. He catches it clumsily, left-handed.

SELA: Hold that, pup. Don't look through the wrong end.
KAITO: (pointing the spyglass at Skarn) I'm coming with you. Skarn can—
SELA: One arm and a wet dog. Stay and keep my ship.
KAITO: He's not a dog. And I can keep a ship with one arm.
SELA: Prove it.
KAITO: ...That's not fair.

Skarn, drenched, gives Kaito a flat look and a low growl.

KAITO: Whose side are you on?

Bram holds the bundle. He isn't looking at Sela; he's looking at the logbook. A tear runs into his beard. Kaito sees it.

KAITO: Bram's crying. Bram cries at soup, but this isn't soup. Juno. Why's he crying?
JUNO: (not looking at him) Ask her.

Sela stands on the rail, in the rock's shadow. Kaito at the foot of it, the loudest man on the ship, very quiet.

KAITO: Sela. What's it cost?

She steps off into the dark, looking back over her shoulder with a half-smile. Below her, Vesh's lures bloom in black water. Above, the rock almost meets. Kaito's left hand reaching, corner of frame.

SELA: I'll let you know. If I remember.

SFX: the water takes her. Then nothing.

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.
