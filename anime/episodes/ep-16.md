## SCENE 1 — EXT. LANTERNREACH, SEVER PEN YARD — BEFORE DAWN (≈ 1:10)

Black. A voice like silk dragged over gravel: warm, amused, a faint rasp.

SILAS (V.O.): Previously. A port with no names, lit by a thousand lamps, and into it stepped a very beautiful man.
KAITO (V.O.): No.
SILAS (V.O.): Three people tried to stab him, one with good reason. He talked his way out, and in the confusion a certain ship's cargo found its way—
KAITO (V.O.): You STOLE it.
SILAS (V.O.): —into other hands. I was magnificent on some roofs. Somebody's love letters got loose over the harbour, which I regret, as several were mine. And at the docks a boy with goggles caught up to me just in time to see absolutely nothing.
KAITO (V.O.): I saw you open eleven cages.
SILAS (V.O.): (a beat) ...Nothing of importance.

Fade up. A lamp-yard behind the Sever's dock warehouse. Eleven cages, every door hanging open. Empty chains.

Kaito (21, goggles up, red jacket at his waist) has Silas Moreau (24, dark-teal hair tied low, shirt open to the stomach, red sash) by the sash against a bollard. Silas looks like a man being helped into a coat. Skarn growls behind Kaito. On a crate opposite, Hush, the owl-lynx, sits perfectly still.

KAITO: Our cargo.
SILAS: Sold, darling. About an hour after I liberated it. To the only man in Lanternreach who buys.
KAITO: And that? (jerks his head at the open pen) I watched you. Cage by cage.
SILAS: A lamp blew out. Doors do that in the dark.
KAITO: Doors don't have LOCKS. Why would you even?
SILAS: (pleasantly) Why would I even what?

Skarn's growl climbs. Hush blinks once, slowly. Skarn stops.

Boots on cobbles. Sela (28, cropped blue jacket, silver-blue ponytail, logbook under her arm) and Bram (31, huge, shirtless in dungarees, towel on his shoulder) arrive.

SELA: Pup. Put the thief down.
KAITO: He—
SELA: I know what he did. I'd like him breathing while I decide what it was for.

Kaito lets go. Silas straightens his sash with great care. Something quick crosses his face when he sees the logbook, and goes.

SILAS: Captain. You've come to shout at me. Join the queue, it winds round the harbour.
SELA: I've come about Maro.
SILAS: (lightly) Nobody says that name out loud in this port.
SELA: I'm not from this port. Forty crates of ours sit in his warehouse, and I'm told they're not mine, because here nothing's yours till it's written down.
SILAS: That's the whole town in a sentence. You've been here a day.
SELA: Then we take the writing.

The quick thing crosses Silas's face again, and stays a half-second longer.

SILAS: Ah. (soft) The Ledger lives in the baths.
KAITO: The what lives where?

## SCENE 2 — INT. THE GULLWAKE, GALLEY — MORNING (≈ 2:00)

Bram at the stove. Juno (23, copper braid, gloves, Ashka on her shoulder), boots on the table. Kaito glowering by the door. Silas sits on the table itself, like it's his.

SILAS: The Lantern Baths. One dome, one hot pool, a stone island in the middle. On the island, a woman with a brass box. In the box, the book. Every deal in Lanternreach is written in it, and no deal's a deal till it is.
JUNO: Why a bath?
SILAS: Because nobody can hide a wire in a towel, sweetheart. Or a knife. Or a friend. You wade over carrying nothing but your word, and she writes it down.
JUNO: (pointing) I'm "sweetheart". You're "darling". Don't cross the streams.
SILAS: Noted, sweetheart.
JUNO: ...Fine. That one was earned.
SELA: (writing) When's it in the open?
SILAS: Eighth bell to ninth. Then she locks it and takes it through the dry door to his safe, and it's gone till tomorrow.
KAITO: So we go in, grab it, run. Easy.
SILAS: In a towel.
KAITO: ...
SILAS: Up a ladder. In a towel.
KAITO: WHY is it always—

Bram sets a bowl in front of him. Greens.

BRAM: Eat before a job.
KAITO: It's a HEIST, Bram.
BRAM: Vegetable.

Juno hops up through the hatch to the mast. Under last week's KAITO AND WHO? she chalks SILAS DROWNS — 3 TO 1.

SELA: Juno. You run a bet. Loud. The whole hall looking at the water.
JUNO: Sweetheart, I was born for it. What's the bet?
SELA: How long the new one can hold his breath.
SELA: Silas is under the water with a decoy. (to Silas) You have a decoy.

Silas draws a slim oilskin-wrapped book from his sash.

SILAS: Her box is from the same stationer as everybody's. So was mine.
KAITO: Why do you HAVE one?
SILAS: Habit.
SELA: Bram. The stones.
BRAM: (thinking it over) Stones want a ladle. I'll give them a bucket.
SELA: I'm on the island. We've an account with the man now, so I've business. The box opens when she writes.
JUNO: And Goggles?
SELA: Runner. Roof vent at the cold end. When the steam's up, pup takes the bundle off the island step and goes up. Skarn's on the roof.
KAITO: Finally. Something that isn't—
SILAS: In a towel.
KAITO: I HEARD YOU THE FIRST TIME.

Silas slides off the table and leans beside Bram at the stove, close, watching him stir.

SILAS: And you, enormous, you'll be at the stove. Which is where I like you.
BRAM: I'm always at the stove.
SILAS: Yes.
BRAM: New galley rule. Nobody flirts with the cook while the pan's hot.
SILAS: Define hot.
BRAM: (not looking up) Rule stands.

Silas, grinning, flicks the towel on Bram's shoulder.

SILAS: And put the towel down, darling, you'll want both hands for—

The galley goes dead quiet. Juno's chalk stops. Kaito's fork stops. Sela's pen stops. Bram's hand has gone still on the spoon.

Silas looks round at all of them. For once he has no line.

BRAM: (very gently) It's fine. He didn't know.

He goes on stirring. Juno drops from the hatch, right into Silas's face, and speaks low.

JUNO: Don't ever say that to him.
SILAS: ...Understood.

Silas looks at Bram's back a moment with a face nobody here has seen. Then puts it away.

Kaito catches Sela by the sleeve at the door.

KAITO: You trust him?
SELA: Not one word.
KAITO: Then why's he coming?
SELA: Because he wants that book more than we do, and I'd like to watch him want it.
KAITO: I saw him open that pen.
SELA: (writing) I know. You've told me. Four times.
KAITO: Why would he?
SELA: Ask him.
KAITO: He'll lie.
SELA: Then you'll learn how he lies. That's worth more than the truth, pup. Truth you only get once.

## SCENE 3 — EXT./INT. THE LANTERN BATHS, LOBBY — NIGHT (≈ 1:20)

Lanternreach at night: a port made of lamps, no signs on anything. The Baths: a low dome sweating light from every seam.

SFX: a bell. Eight slow strokes.

A tiled lobby. Behind a brass cage, the Door (44, woman, bored, forearms like a stevedore's). In the middle of the floor, where he isn't wanted, Grist lies asleep. Two bathers in towels sit on him. Ashka perches on his horn, feathers puffed.

THE DOOR: No names past the lamp. Everything in a basket. Towel each. (up at Bram) One each.
BRAM: I'll need two.
THE DOOR: Everybody says that. (to Kaito) Goggles.
KAITO: They're mine.
THE DOOR: Didn't ask whose. Goggles. That's you now. Basket. (to Bram) Beard.
BRAM: Yes?
THE DOOR: Nobody hides a wire in a towel. I've no rule about beards. Lean down.

She combs the beard with the gravity of customs. Finds a wooden spoon.

BRAM: That's mine.
THE DOOR: Basket.
BRAM: (misty) It's been through a lot.

Juno steps up. The Door looks at the gloves.

THE DOOR: Gloves.
JUNO: They stay.
THE DOOR: Nothing stays.
JUNO: Burns, sweetheart. I'd be doing you a favour. Trust me, nobody is hiding anything in these.

A beat. The Door's eyes go to Juno's eyes, not her hands, and she lets it go.

THE DOOR: Gloves. (to Silas) Shirt.
SILAS: You flatter it.
THE DOOR: (to Sela) And you.
SELA: Captain.
THE DOOR: No names.
SELA: It's not a name, it's a job. Like Door.

The Door looks at her a long second, and very nearly smiles.

THE DOOR: Captain. Go through.

Skarn's head fills the street door behind them, ears flat.

THE DOOR: No animals.
KAITO: He's not an animal, he's my—
SKARN: (one grumble that is a whole sentence)

He withdraws with dignity. Claws on roof tiles overhead.

KAITO: (low; ears already red, and nobody has so much as removed a boot) I don't like this.
SELA: You haven't done anything yet.
KAITO: That's why.

## SCENE 4 — INT. THE LANTERN BATHS, HOT HALL — NIGHT (≈ 1:40)

The dome. Coloured lanterns hang in steam so thick they're smudges. A round hot pool; at its centre a stone island with a table, a lamp and a brass box. At the table, bone dry in a grey robe, the Pen (45, woman, thin as her name, glasses on a cord) writes in a long book. Bathers in towels sit along the pool edge, shoulders up, talking low; now and then one wades to the island, says a sentence, and she writes it down.

Everything here is faces, shoulders and steam. We never see more than that.

The crew comes in. Kaito, looking strictly at the ceiling, walks straight into a pillar.

SFX: BONK
JUNO: (not looking) That's one.
KAITO: (hissing) It's the STEAM.
JUNO: Sure it is, Goggles.

Bram ambles to the stove: red stones, a ladle, a bucket. The attendant (56, man) holds up one finger.

ATTENDANT: One ladle.
BRAM: (nodding solemnly) One ladle.

Silas strolls to the pool like a man arriving at his own party and slides in to the shoulders.

SILAS: Most clothes I've worn all week, and a woman in a cage has them. Enormous, you're in my lantern.
BRAM: Good.

Sela walks the pool edge without hurry. A big tattooed man has the island step; she looks at him; he gets up and moves. She didn't say a word.

JUNO: (to herself) Witch.

Juno climbs onto an upturned bucket, gloves spread.

JUNO: Sweethearts! Friends! Whatever we call each other in here! Who'd like to watch a man drown?

Heads turn in the steam.

JUNO: This one. (a glove at Silas) Says he can out-hold any lamp-lit lowlife in this port. Three to one he's up gasping inside a count of sixty. I take coin, chits, and anything written in that book.
BATHER: Sixty? He's got the lungs of a flute.
SILAS: I've had no complaints.
JUNO: He's had complaints. Who's in?

The crowd surges to the edge. Coins appear from towels that shouldn't hold coins. The Pen does not look up.

Sela wades to the island, chest-deep, carrying nothing. The Pen looks up over her glasses.

SELA: The Gullwake. I've come about our account.
THE PEN: No names.
SELA: You know mine and I don't know yours. That's the only deal this port makes, isn't it? I thought I'd make it face to face.

The Pen unlocks the box with a key from round her neck and lifts out the book: oilskin, long and narrow, corners soft with handling. She opens a late page.

THE PEN: Forty crates. Lamp oil, rope, salt pork. Held against a debt of—
SELA: Whose debt? We'd been here an hour.
THE PEN: Whoever brought them to us. Debts travel with the goods here. (writing) First thing anyone learns.
SELA: Then write down that I'd like to be the second.

Behind her, the hall begins to count.

CROWD: ONE! TWO! THREE!

Silas has gone under. Only the ripple where he was.

## SCENE 5 — INT. HOT HALL — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:20)

CROWD: ...SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! TEN!

On "ten", three faces in the steam: Sela's pen hand on the island, stopped. Bram at the stones, ladle halfway. Kaito by his pillar, left hand gone to his right arm without his knowing. The same word in the same rhythm, and for one second each of them is standing in a dawn queue on Pinhold.

No music.

JUNO: ELEVEN!

Hers is the only voice. Then the crowd catches up. Kaito lets a breath out and looks at the water where Silas went down.

A soft voice at Sela's elbow.

MARO: Evening, friend.

A small man (Ledger Maro, 52, neat, grey, robed like the Pen) sits on the island step and lowers his feet into the water with a small sigh. A kind, tired, careful face.

MARO: I don't believe we've been introduced. Which in this port means we're already well acquainted.
SELA: (not turning) You'd be the man everyone owes.
MARO: I'd be a man who writes things down. You'd know something about that.

He smiles.

MARO: Gullwake. Crew of four. A serpent, a beetle, a bird, a wolf. Forty crates, one opened. Vinegar.
SELA: Lamp oil.
MARO: It's vinegar now. (to the Pen, pleasantly) Where's our friend with the hair? I was told he'd be in tonight.
THE PEN: Haven't seen him.
MARO: No. (a glance at the counting crowd) Nobody has. For about a minute and a half.

CROWD: FORTY-SIX! FORTY-SEVEN!

Underwater, lit green: Silas, eyes open, hair streaming, the decoy flat to his chest. The feather lines on his neck glow faint violet. Bubbles leak from his nose. He looks up at Maro's feet hanging above him and goes very still.

JUNO: (watching the ripples, the showman gone out of her voice) ...Fifty. Fifty-one. Come on, you idiot.

Sela looks at Maro at last. Then past him, to where Bram stands with a full bucket and the attendant saying something urgent to his elbow.

SELA: (perfectly pleasant) Do you take your steam heavy?
MARO: I beg your—

SFX: a full bucket on red stone: KSSSSSHHHHHHHHH

White. The whole hall. Everything.

## SCENE 6 — INT. HOT HALL, WHITE-OUT — CONTINUOUS (≈ 1:30)

Nothing but white, voices, and dripping water.

THE PEN: (O.S.) NOBODY MOVES. Nobody moves in the steam. That's the rule!
SELA: (O.S., calm) I'm not moving. I'm settling.
CROWD: (O.S., ragged) SIXTY! Did he come up? Can't SEE—
JUNO: (O.S.) SIXTY-ONE! Is anybody still betting? No? Then WHY AM I COUNTING!

At the island's back step a pale hand rises from the water holding the decoy. Sela takes it without looking down, lays it in the open box, lifts the real book out, and wraps it one-handed into a towel on the step. The Pen is a smudge a yard away, shouting at the room. Maro is a smudge, perfectly still, listening.

MARO: (O.S., mild) ...Pepper. Somebody's supper. Yours, I think.
THE PEN: (O.S.) Don't you start.

Kaito, blind, hands out, inching along the pool edge.

KAITO: (whisper) Sela. Sela. Where's the—

His hand finds a thick hanging rope. He grabs it to steady himself.

BRAM: (O.S., very close, very gentle) That's my face.
KAITO: SORRY—

He lets go, turns, and walks into the same pillar.

SFX: BONK
JUNO: (O.S.) Two!

Then Sela's voice at his ear, and a wrapped bundle pressed into his hands.

SELA: Roof. Go.

He goes. A ladder rattles; a vent cover scrapes.

CROWD: (O.S.) EIGHTY-ONE! He's DEAD. Pay out, Gloves—
JUNO: (O.S.) He's not dead till I SEE him dead! EIGHTY-TWO!

The steam thins. The Pen, glasses fogged, locks the box on the decoy. Maro has not moved. His eyes rest on the towel-wrapped bundle on the step by Sela's hip.

Sela looks at him. He looks at her. Then she picks it up, tucks it under her arm like any towel, and stands.

SELA: Mine. Sorry. Pepper.
MARO: (a small warm smile) Of course it is.

SFX: a splash like a dropped sack

Silas comes up at the back step, open-mouthed, and hauls himself onto the stones.

SFX: SHREEEEEEEEE

A kettle on the stove, boiling over. Hold on it while the hall roars.

CROWD: (O.S.) NINETY-ONE! HE'S UP! HE'S UP!
JUNO: (O.S.) ...Son of a bitch. Fine. Who had ninety?

Back: Silas on the step, shoulders heaving. The feather lines fade to pale. He tries to speak. What comes out is a thread.

SILAS: (barely) Did we... win, sweetheart?
JUNO: (counting coins out of her own glove, sour) I had you at drowning.
SILAS: (a whisper of a laugh) Everybody does.

Sela is already at the far door, bundle under her arm, nodding good night to the attendant. Maro watches her go, feet in the water, and says nothing at all.

SFX: the bell. Nine strokes.

The Pen takes up the brass box and carries it through the dry door.

SELA (V.O.): (writing, later) Lesson for the pup. Nobody hides a wire in a towel. Nobody has ever looked twice at what a woman carries out of a bath.

## SCENE 7 — EXT. THE LANTERN BATHS, ROOF — NIGHT (≈ 0:40)

Wet tiles, lamps below. Kaito, towel and goggles, crouches by the vent with Skarn, unwrapping the bundle, triumphant.

KAITO: Got it. Got it. Skarn. We GOT—

Pie. A whole lidded fish pie, slightly squashed, a fork tucked down the side.

KAITO: ...It's a pie.

Skarn looks at the pie. Looks at Kaito. The face. Then eats the pie in two bites and lies down.

KAITO: Screw that. Screw ALL of—
SELA: (O.S., from the street below, unhurried) Pup. You've got the supper.
KAITO: I KNOW I'VE GOT THE SUPPER.

Below: Sela dressed again, bundle under her arm; Bram with his spoon; Juno counting; Grist waking with two bathers sliding off him. Silas comes last, saying nothing, because he can't.

Kaito drops off the roof and lands beside Silas. Walks with him. Doesn't look at him.

KAITO: The pen. Eleven cages. Why?
SILAS: (a whisper) Which pen? I open a lot of things.
KAITO: You know which.

Silas stops. When the words come they are very short, and there's no silk on them.

SILAS: Lamp went out.
KAITO: ...
SILAS: Doors do that.

He walks on. Kaito watches the back of the open shirt a second, then follows.

## SCENE 8 — INT. THE GULLWAKE, GALLEY — LATER (≈ 1:20)

Lamplight. Sela unfolds the towel, the oilskin, the book. Juno, Bram and Kaito lean in. Silas is not in the room; from the deck above, the drip of someone wringing out his hair.

JUNO: Right. Forty crates, one vinegar. Find us and cross us out.

Sela opens it at random. Columns, in the Pen's small dry hand.

INSERT: "4th. Hound, bonded, grey. 90. Seller: Carrow." "Heron, bonded, blue. 60. Carrow." "9th. Plough-beast, bonded, Pinhold stock. 40. Seller: —"

Nobody speaks. Bram's big finger has stopped on "Pinhold".

KAITO: (very quiet) These aren't crates.
SELA: No.
JUNO: Bonded. Every line says bonded. Why would a smuggler's book care if— (she stops)
SELA: Because that's what he was selling. Not goods. Partners. Every page. (turning one) And who he sold them on to.

At the foot of the page, a stamp in grey ink: a straight line through a circle.

BRAM: Every one of these was somebody's.

He says it to the table. Nobody answers, because there isn't an answer.

Sela turns back. Back. To the front. Page one, the oldest ink, gone brown.

INSERT: a dozen lines, years old, every seller the same: "Moreau." Across the top, large, in another hand: "S. MOREAU. Account opened."

Juno's mouth opens, and for once nothing comes out of it. Not even a number.

SFX: a step in the passage

Silas in the doorway, towel round his neck, shirt open, grinning. He sees the book. He sees their faces. The grin stays exactly where it is, and it is the wrong grin.

Sela lays her hand flat on page one, looks at him, and does not raise her voice.

SELA: Sit down, Silas.
